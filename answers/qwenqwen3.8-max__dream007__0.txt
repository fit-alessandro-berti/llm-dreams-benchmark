You are in the middle of a heated argument with a close friend. The disagreement is about something important to both of you, and you decide to stop speaking and take a step back. As you do, the air between you shimmers like heat rising from summer pavement. Your friend’s face softens, not with agreement, but with sadness, and you realize the argument was never really about the topic itself—it was about fear, about wanting to be understood.

You decide to reach out and place your hand over theirs. The moment you touch, the scene folds like paper, and you are both standing in a quiet field under a sky full of slow-moving stars. There is no need to win anymore. The dream asks you a simple question: would you rather be right, or would you rather keep the connection?

You choose the connection. Your friend smiles, and though the disagreement remains, it becomes a door instead of a wall. You both walk through it together, waking up with the strange, tender feeling that something unresolved in the dream has been gently set down.
