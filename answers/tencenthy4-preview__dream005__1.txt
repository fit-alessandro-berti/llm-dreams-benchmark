You are at a large social gathering where you only know one person. As you try to find them, you encounter various groups of people and the geometry of the room keeps shifting around them. 

To your left, a cluster of people in shimmering, old-fashioned evening wear are having a lively, entirely silent tea party, their teacups filled with tiny, swirling galaxies that they sip with intense concentration. To your right, a group of strangers are playing a card game, but the cards are actually thin, translucent leaves that flutter like nervous moths in their hands. 

You try to ask a passing waiter if he’s seen the person you’re looking for, but when he turns to you, his face is a comforting blur, like a watercolor painting left out in the rain. He simply smiles, hands you a glowing silver key, and points toward a grand archway that wasn't there a moment ago.

You clutch the key and navigate through the shifting crowd. The ambient chatter of the room sounds like a hundred radios tuned to different stations, blending into a soothing, nonsensical hum. The floor beneath your feet feels strangely like warm, sunlit moss.

Finally, near a massive, spiraling staircase that seems to stretch infinitely upward into a ceiling of soft clouds, you catch a glimpse of a familiar silhouette. You try to call out their name, but your voice emerges as a ripple of golden light that floats through the air like a soap bubble.

They turn, and their face is the only thing in the entire room that is perfectly, brilliantly in focus. They don't seem surprised to see you at all.

"There you are," they say, their voice sounding like a warm summer breeze. "I was starting to worry you got stuck in the hallway of mirrors again."

"What *is* this place?" you ask, though the strange logic of the dream makes the question feel perfectly normal.

"It's just where the quiet thoughts go to stretch their legs," they reply, taking your hand. Their touch is electric and incredibly grounding. "Come on, the band is about to play."

They lead you through a curtain of cascading velvet, and instantly, the crowded, shifting room dissolves. You are no longer at a party, but sitting on the edge of a calm, glowing ocean under a sky heavy with purple and silver stars. You realize you don't care where you are or how you got there, because the feeling of simply being with them is the most real thing you've ever experienced. 

As the music starts—a beautiful melody composed entirely of wind chimes and distant bells—you begin to stir, the phantom feeling of their hand in yours lingering just long enough to carry you gently into the morning.
