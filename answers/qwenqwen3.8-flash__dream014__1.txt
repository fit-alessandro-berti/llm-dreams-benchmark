You follow them through the crowds, their voices soft but steady. As you walk, the tangled streets begin to unfold like a map you once knew in childhood. Neon signs blur into warm lantern light, and the city’s noise becomes a kind of song. One stranger hands you a small key and says, “It opens wherever you need to be.” When you turn it in the air, a door appears on the wall—and behind it is not a destination, but yourself: calm, found, and finally not alone.
