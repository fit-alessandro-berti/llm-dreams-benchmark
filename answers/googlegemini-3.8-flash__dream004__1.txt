...a palette that holds neither oils nor ink, but sensations: a smear of sunlight warm against the skin, a droplet of quiet indigo that smells like rain on dry earth, and an electric tremor of gold that hums like an unspoken word. 

Without needing a brush, you reach out and press your fingertips against the pristine white surface. 

The moment your skin meets the fabric, the frantic chaos of the shifting world around you halts. The liquid sky freezes mid-cascade; the morphing geometry of the ground beneath your feet holds its breath. From your touch, a single drop of crimson ripples across the weave, not bleeding as paint would, but expanding like a heartbeat.

You drag your fingers downward, and the color pulls the surrounding landscape into it. The wild, restless colors of the dream are drawn toward the canvas like a river finding a sudden gorge. Emerald fractals fold themselves into the silhouettes of ancient, whispering pines. Wandering ribbons of violet settle into deep, reflective tidepools. A runaway pulse of amber becomes a solitary lantern resting at the edge of a path you have never walked, yet instantly remember.

You realize the canvas isn't just capturing the world—it is stilling it, giving form to a dream that was waiting for an anchor. 

As the painting deepens, the boundary between the frame and the horizon blurs. A cool, fragrant breeze begins to blow, not from the surreal sky behind you, but from the quiet pine forest living inside the canvas. The lantern’s light flickers, casting a warm shadow across your hands. 

There is nothing left of the formless world at your back now. There is only the path, wet with painted dew, winding away into the calm of the trees. 

You lift your hand, take a quiet breath, and step through the frame.
