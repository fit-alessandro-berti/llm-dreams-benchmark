You try to run, to find shelter, but you are hit with that familiar, frustrating dream-heaviness—your legs feel like they are wading through chest-deep water. 

A low rumble vibrates through the soles of your shoes, rising up into your chest. The wind finally hits, not with a howl, but with a sound like a thousand whispering voices. As the first raindrops fall, you realize they aren't water. They are warm, glowing droplets of liquid golden light. 

Wherever the golden rain touches, the forest transforms. The rough bark of the ancient trees turns into smooth, translucent glass, revealing veins of pulsing starlight running from the roots to the branches. The ferns at your feet unfurl into delicate feathers. 

Panicked but mesmerized, you look for somewhere to hide and spot a massive, hollowed-out oak tree that wasn't there a moment ago. Set into its trunk is a heavy wooden door with a brass handle. Thunder cracks overhead—a sound like a giant mirror shattering—and you finally break free from your heavy legs, lunging forward.

You grab the brass handle, pull the door open, and throw yourself inside. 

Expecting a dark, cramped hollow, you stumble onto a plush velvet rug. You are in a warm, dimly lit library that stretches out infinitely in every direction. There is no ceiling; instead, the storm rages safely above an invisible barrier, the violet clouds and golden rain illuminating towers of floating books. 

In the center of the room sits a single velvet armchair. Resting on the cushion is a dusty pocket watch, ticking loudly in the quiet room. You walk over, pick it up, and realize the hands are spinning backward. As you touch the glass face of the watch, the floor beneath you dissolves into mist, and with a sudden, weightless jolt... you wake up.
