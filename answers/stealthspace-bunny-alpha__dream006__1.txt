…and you begin to say, “Good evening.”

But the words come out in a voice that isn’t quite your own—older, calmer, as though someone else has been waiting inside you to speak.

The crowd leans forward.

“**Tell us where you’ve been,**” they whisper, all at once inside your head.

You look down at your notes. Every page is blank except for one sentence:

*Describe the place you saw before you fell asleep.*

Your heart pounds. You have no idea what it means. Then you look up, and the auditorium is gone. The crowd lies beneath you, their faces illuminated by moonlight. Above them stretches the ceiling of your childhood bedroom.

The ceiling is opening.

Beyond it is a darkness so deep it seems to have weight—and somewhere inside it, something is rising toward you.

You try to step back, but the floor has become a stage, and the spotlight is a small warm sun.

“Do you remember?” someone whispers from the darkness.

You open your mouth to answer, and every word you have ever known begins to tumble out.
