You trace one with your fingertip. The metal hums, and the lines rearrange themselves into a map of a forest filled with silver trees and drifting stars. The lid clicks open, though you never touched it.

Beyond it lies a garden beneath an upside-down moon. Fireflies flutter between the branches, carrying fragments of memories that aren’t yours—a red kite above a summer hill, a train window at dawn, someone laughing in a kitchen while rain taps the roof.

“You brought them back,” says a familiar voice.

You turn, but no one is there. Your reflection stares from the box’s brass trim, wearing your face and an expression you can’t quite read.

“I missed you,” your reflection whispers.

Before you can answer, the garden begins to glow brighter. Its light pours through your hands, warm and impossibly real.

Slowly, you lift the lid all the way open.

The dream doesn’t end.

You wake.
