You begin to speak, but the words come out as tiny glowing moths. They flutter up over the audience, and every face turns to follow them. The crowd doesn’t laugh—they breathe them in, and suddenly you hear your own thoughts echoing from a thousand mouths, softly, in different languages. The stage becomes a raft on a warm black sea. The seats become waves. You keep talking, and the speech writes itself across the ceiling in constellations. Just as you reach the last line, you realize the audience is you—every age, every fear, every hope—and they are nodding. Then the spotlight becomes a doorway. You step through, still speaking, and wake with the sentence “I am here” on your lips.
