...written in a language you've never seen before, but as you read them, you realize you *understand* them completely — not with your mind, but with your gut. The first question asks you to draw a picture of your deepest fear. The second asks you to write a letter to someone you never met but somehow owe an apology to. The third simply says: **"Now that we know everything about you, what would you like to change?"**

You flip through page after page, and each question becomes more personal, more *true* — like the exam was written by someone who has known you your entire life. Some answers come easily. Others make your hands shake.

Then you reach the last page. It's blank except for a single line at the top:

**"There is no right answer. There is only your answer."**

You look up from your desk and notice the exam room is empty now — no other students, no proctor, no clock ticking on the wall. Just you, a pencil, and a feeling in your chest like standing at the edge of a diving board.

You take a breath, press the pencil to the page, and begin to write — and the moment the tip touches the paper, you realize you already know exactly what you're going to say.

And just before you write the first word, you wake up.
