One stranger walks beside you, pointing out small wonders hidden in the ordinary—a cat asleep in a bakery window, rain turning the pavement into a field of stars, a child holding a red umbrella like a tiny parachute. Another buys you a warm coffee and tells you that every unfamiliar street has a story waiting to be noticed.

Before long, the crowd no longer feels so hostile. The strangers lead you to a quiet bridge where the city glows beneath you, countless windows shining like distant earthbound stars. Then they smile, step back, and disappear into the evening.

You realize you were never truly lost. You had simply forgotten that sometimes the way home is shown not by a single light, but by a handful of kind lights walking beside you.
