...and each challenge peels away something you thought you knew about yourself.

First, you’re asked to build a bridge out of moonlight and the sound of a forgotten lullaby. You hesitate, but the bridge appears beneath your feet, shimmering as you step forward.

Next, you enter a hall of mirrors where every reflection is a version of you that took a different path. One is a painter. One is a scientist. One is a coward. One is a hero. They all speak at once: *“Which one are you?”*

You answer, “All of them.”

The mirrors shatter into a thousand soft pieces.

The final challenge is a plain white door labeled **DREAM JOB**. Behind it is a tiny room with a desk, a single chair, and an old typewriter. A voice says, “Type your dream job.”

You sit down. Your fingers hover over the keys. Your mind goes blank. You think of every version of yourself in the mirrors, every path you could have taken. Then you smile and type:

*“I want a job that lets me keep becoming.”*

The typewriter glows. The room dissolves.

You wake up in a bed that isn’t yours, and your phone is already open to an email:

**“You’re hired. Start Monday.”**

You have no idea what the job is. But for the first time, you’re not afraid.

Then your real alarm clock rings. You reach over and turn it off, smiling.

The dream is complete.
