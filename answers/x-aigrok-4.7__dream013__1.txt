You find the corridors narrowing, the walls shifting between stone and something softer, like thought itself. The next puzzle is a door with no handle, only a riddle etched in light: “What grows smaller the more you understand it?” You answer aloud—“the unknown”—and the door dissolves. Beyond it the maze opens into a quiet clearing where every path you didn’t take is still faintly visible, glowing, waiting. You realize the maze was never trying to trap you; it was teaching you how to choose.
