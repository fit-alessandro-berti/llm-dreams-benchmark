...and each group seems to be waiting for something. One cluster is dressed entirely in mirrored costumes, silently watching their own reflections in each other’s surfaces. Another is building a tower out of mismatched chairs, and when they see you, they ask if you have the missing piece. You shake your head and keep moving.

Then you hear a familiar laugh from behind a curtain of hanging vines. You push through—and there’s your friend. They’re sitting at a small table with a single glowing candle between them and an empty chair across. They smile and say, “I saved this seat for you. Did you have trouble finding me?”

You sit down. The room hums. The party slowly fades behind you. Then your friend blows out the candle, and in the dark, they say, “Now we can go somewhere else.”

But before you can answer, you feel the dream tipping into something stranger—a soft, salty wind, and the sound of the chair beneath you becoming a wooden boat. Your friend is rowing, but there is no water, only a silver twilight sky. You ask where they’re taking you. They whisper, “Somewhere we don’t have to be anyone.”

And you realize you’ve been smiling in your sleep.
