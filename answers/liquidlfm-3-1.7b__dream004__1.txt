You come across a **blank canvas**—a vast, immaculate expanse of pure white that seems to float above a sea of ever‑changing shapes and hues. It doesn’t lie still; instead, it ripples like liquid glass whenever you glance at it, and faint outlines of distant landscapes flicker along its edges. As you step closer, the air hums with a low, melodic vibration, and a gentle breeze stirs the surrounding geometries, turning them into dancing mandalas. 

When you reach out, the canvas pulses with a soft, golden glow, and a single, trembling line of light appears at its center, waiting for your touch. With a breath, you dip your fingertip into the luminous ink, and the moment your brush meets the paper, the world outside blurs into a cascade of colors—each hue spreading outward like a wave, reshaping the very geometry around you. The canvas becomes a portal: every stroke you lay down births new forms, and the shifting shapes around it respond, weaving together into a living, breathing masterpiece that only exists while you create.
