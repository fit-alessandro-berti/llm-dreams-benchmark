...they keep shifting like smoke—first a cluster of old friends from a life you never lived, laughing at a joke you almost remember, then a knot of strangers in animal masks who insist you join their silent waltz. You slip past them, calling the one name you know, but the rooms stretch and fold; hallways become gardens, gardens become crowded train cars. Every time you think you spot them, the face turns and it’s someone else wearing their smile. Finally the crowd thins to a single figure waiting by an open door that wasn’t there a moment ago. They look exactly like the person you came for… until they speak in your own voice and ask why you ever thought you were lost. The door closes behind you and the gathering is gone.
