You start to head toward the central square, where the city’s heartbeat feels louder than usual. The streets are lined with flickering lanterns casting dancing shadows, and every corner hums with voices you’ve never heard. Just as you reach the grand fountain, a familiar face appears—a colleague from last week, now wearing a sleek coat—and waves a glowing tablet. “The meeting is at the Grand Hall on Fifth Street, but only after midnight,” she says, her eyes alight with urgency. You nod, clutching the device, and feel the weight of the hour pressing against your chest. As you walk deeper, the buildings seem to shift and blur, turning the skyline into a watercolor of impossible hues. Despite the disorientation, you sense a pull toward that hall, and you resolve to find it before the clock strikes twelve.
