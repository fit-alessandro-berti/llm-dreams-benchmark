Behind you, footsteps echo softly across the floorboards. Your friend appears at the end of the aisle, no longer angry, just looking tired and strangely translucent, like sunlight through frosted glass. Without speaking, they hold out a small wooden music box with a keyhole on its lid.

You walk over and fit the brass key into the lock. As you turn it, the bookshelves around you begin to fold inward like paper, and the ceiling lifts away to reveal a quiet, twilight sky. Instead of music, the box releases the distant sound of ocean waves and half-forgotten laughter, drifting upward into the air like silver mist. 

Your friend offers a faint, relieved smile and reaches out to catch some of the mist in their palm. The tightness in your chest vanishes completely. Before either of you can speak again, the floor beneath your feet dissolves into warm, shifting sand, and the whole world gently blurs at the edges until nothing is left but the sound of the tide.
