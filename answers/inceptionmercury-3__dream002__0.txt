A wind starts to pick up, warm at first, then cool, carrying the smell of rain and wet earth. The branches above begin to sway and creak, and the forest, which felt so peaceful moments ago, now feels wild—alive in a way that sends a strange thrill down your spine.

You feel the storm approaching not as something to fear, but as something that has been waiting for you. The path ahead disappears into deepening shadow. A single drop of rain lands on your forehead. Then another.

And then the sky opens.

But you don't run. You stand there, letting the rain soak through your clothes, feeling the thunder shake the ground beneath your feet. And for the first time in the dream, you realize you are not lost. You are exactly where you need to be.

The storm surrounds you. But you are not afraid.

You are the calm at its center.
