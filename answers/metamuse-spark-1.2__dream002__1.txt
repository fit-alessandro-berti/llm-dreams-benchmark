You feel a storm approaching and...

The air, which was just warm and still, suddenly turns cool against your skin. The sunlight that was filtering through the leaves begins to flicker, as if someone is dimming the sky.

The birds go silent all at once. The forest holds its breath.

A wind rises from nowhere, rustling the canopy high above you. It doesn't feel threatening at first - it feels *alive*. It carries the smell of rain and ozone and wet earth, even though not a single drop has fallen yet. The leaves swirl around your feet in a slow spiral, defying gravity for a moment before settling.

You look up. The blue sky you saw through the branches just moments ago is now a deep, bruised violet. Clouds are rolling in impossibly fast, churning and folding into each other like an ocean above the trees. A low rumble of thunder rolls through the forest floor, up through the soles of your feet.

And then you realize - you are not afraid.

Instead of running, you stand still and close your eyes. You let the wind pull at your clothes and your hair. The first heavy raindrop lands on your cheek, surprisingly warm.

When you open your eyes again, the forest has changed.

The trees are no longer just trees - their bark is glowing faintly with soft, golden light, as if the storm has awakened something inside them. In front of you, where there was only a dense thicket before, a small, moss-covered cabin has appeared. Light spills from its single window, warm and inviting. The door is slightly ajar, just for you.

You understand, in that strange, certain way you only understand things in dreams, that the storm was never meant to chase you. It was meant to guide you here. To wash away the noise.

As thunder cracks directly overhead and the rain finally pours down in a cleansing roar, you step inside the cabin, completely dry. Outside, the forest drinks in the storm. Inside, you feel perfectly calm, perfectly safe, listening to the rain on the roof - knowing you can stay here as long as you need to.

And somewhere, deep in the dream, you smile, realizing you are the one who called the storm in the first place.
