But the drops never reach the ground. They hang between the branches like tiny glass bells, each holding a flicker of light. When you touch one, you hear your name whispered from somewhere deep in the forest.

A narrow path appears where there was none before. You follow it to a clearing, where the storm has gathered into a dark, turning cloud no bigger than a house. Beneath it stands a deer with silver antlers. It looks at you, then steps aside.

At the center of the clearing is a small door set into the earth. You open it, and warm sunlight spills across your feet. Beyond it lies the forest as it was a moment ago, peaceful and still. You step through. Behind you, the thunder fades into the sound of leaves rustling overhead.
