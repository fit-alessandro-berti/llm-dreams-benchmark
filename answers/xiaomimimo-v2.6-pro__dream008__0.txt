# The Dream Continues...

You are at your workplace, and suddenly, you are given a project with an impossible deadline. Your boss is waiting for you to start, and you look down at the file in your hands. The words on the pages keep rearranging themselves — every time you try to read the instructions, the letters swim like fish and settle into different sentences.

You open your laptop, but the screen is **blue** — not a blue error screen, but an actual endless ocean. Tiny paper boats bob on gentle waves, and somewhere deep below, you can hear the hum of office printers.

You start to panic, but then you notice something strange: the office is **growing**. The ceiling stretches upward like taffy. The fluorescent lights drift apart and become **stars**. Your cubicle walls melt into skyscrapers made of stacked sticky notes, and your coworkers are all speaking in perfectly rhyming sentences.

You grab the nearest binder to check the deadline, but the date on it says:
> **"Due yesterday."**

You run to your boss's office, but the hallway stretches longer and longer with every step. Doors line the walls — behind one you hear applause, behind another someone is crying about missingpellings. You finally reach the door marked **"BOSS"** and push it open.

Inside, the boss is sitting behind a desk that floats on a single cloud.

**"You're late,"** he says, but his voice sounds like your alarm clock — a buzzing, insistent, repeating tone.

The walls start to **ripple**. The floor tilts. The desk, the cloud, the boss — everything begins to **unravel** like a sweater being pulled from a single loose thread. The world folds in on itself, and you feel that familiar sinking sensation...

---
⏰ **BEEP. BEEP. BEEP.** ⏰

You jolt awake in bed, heart racing. For a brief, terrifying moment, you're *sure* you forgot to finish the project. Then reality sets in — there *was* no project. There's no office. It was all a dream.

But as you reach to silence your alarm, you notice a sticky note on your nightstand you don't remember writing:

> *"Due yesterday."*

And the clock says it's **4:58 AM**.

*Your dream isn't over yet.* 😶‍🌫️
