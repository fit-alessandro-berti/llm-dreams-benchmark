…written in a language you somehow understand but have never seen before. Each question describes something from your past in exact, impossible detail: the exact sentence you whispered to your mother when you were six, the name of the street you got lost on, the dream you had last Tuesday. The first question asks, “Why did you stop believing it could be easy?”

You look around, but everyone else is writing with their eyes closed. The clock on the wall has no hands. The proctor is now sitting in your seat, watching you with your own tired expression.

You try to answer, but the pencil moves by itself and writes:  
*“Because no one told me I was allowed to want it.”*

Then the exam paper folds itself into a paper airplane and drifts out the window, and you remember—you already have the career. You always did. You’re just dreaming you forgot.
