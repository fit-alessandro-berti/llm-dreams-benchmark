It lifts from the box and circles your head. Each time it passes your ear, you hear a different voice whispering your name. You recognize one as your own, though it sounds much younger.

The moth settles on the lid. A seam appears, and the box opens with a soft sigh.

Inside is a small room, warmly lit. A cup of tea sits on a table beside an empty chair. Through the room’s window, you can see yourself standing in a garden, looking up at the sky.

You lean closer. The cup trembles, and the figure in the garden waves. Then, from somewhere behind you, a kettle begins to whistle.
