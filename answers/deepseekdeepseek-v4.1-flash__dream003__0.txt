...and glyphs that seem to crawl just beneath the surface, rearranging whenever you blink. They form a map of a place you’ve never been but somehow miss. You press your thumb to the largest symbol, and the box sighs open—not with a key, but with a memory. Inside lies a single black feather and a note in your own handwriting: *The lock was never on the box.*

You turn. The room has become a corridor of doors, each painted with the same shifting patterns. Behind you, the box is gone. Ahead, one door is already open. You step through, and somewhere far away, you wake up still dreaming.
