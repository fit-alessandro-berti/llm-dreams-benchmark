You are at your workplace, and suddenly, you are given a project with an impossible deadline. Your boss is waiting for you to start, and you reach for your laptop to open the brief, but the laptop is gone.

Your desk is empty except for a single sticky note that says "URGENT" in your boss's handwriting. You look up and your boss is standing right there, very close, tapping a pen against a clipboard. You try to ask what the project is, but your voice comes out muffled, like you're talking through water.

You nod anyway because you don't want to look incompetent, and you open your mouth to say "Okay, I'll get started" but the words turn into a long sigh. The office around you starts to stretch. The hallway gets longer the more you look at it. Your coworkers are moving in slow motion, typing on keyboards that make no sound.

You try to stand up to go find a computer and realize you're still in your pajamas under your blazer. Your shoes are gone. Your ID badge is blank. The clock on the wall is spinning backwards, and every time you blink the deadline on the whiteboard gets closer: 5pm, 4pm, 3pm.

Your boss smiles and says "Great, you're on it!" and points to a door you swear wasn't there before. It's just a white door with a doorknob shaped like a question mark. You know if you open it you'll have to do the project, so you stand frozen in front of it, sweating, while the whole floor hums louder and louder until...

You wake up.
