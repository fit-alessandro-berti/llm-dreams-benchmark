...find that the puzzles are learning you back. The corridors rearrange whenever you try to retrace your steps, and the riddles stop being written on stone—they appear as flickering images: a key made of a forgotten birthday, a door that opens only when you name a fear, a bridge that holds only while you believe it will. Every solution costs something: a memory, a certainty, a familiar face. Each answer unlocks a deeper chamber, until you reach the center, where there is no monster—only a mirror. Your reflection asks the final question: “What did you come here to find?” When you answer, the maze folds itself into a single bright thread, and you follow it up through sleep into waking.
