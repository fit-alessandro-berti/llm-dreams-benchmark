…not ordinary test questions. They’re printed in your own handwriting, then slowly change into handwriting that isn’t yours.

The first page asks, “What are you afraid you’ll become if you pass?”
The second asks, “Which memory did you choose to keep—and which did you keep by mistake?”
Then the questions start to fold back on themselves:
“If this course opens one future, what future does it close?”
“Answer honestly enough for the exam to be real.”

And in the margin, in small silver letters:
“Some questions don’t have right answers. They ask what you’re trying to become before you get the answer.”

You realize the final question isn’t on the page at all. It’s written above the paper, in the air, in a voice that sounds like your own:
**When you wake up, what will you choose to remember from this?**
