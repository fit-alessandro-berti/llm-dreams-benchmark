...feel the wheel humming beneath your hands, as though the ship itself is whispering to you. Lightning splits the sky, and in its flash you see the waves are not water at all. They are rolling dunes of silver sand, hissing as they break against the hull.

"Captain!" shouts your first mate, her voice bending strangely, half human, half gull cry. "The compass has stopped pointing north!"

You look down. The needle spins lazily, then settles, pointing straight at *you*.

A hush falls over the deck. The crew stares, rain dripping from their faces in slow, glittering beads that refuse to fall. You understand, the way you understand things in dreams, that the storm isn't something happening *to* the ship. It is waiting for a decision.

You raise your hand and say, "Hoist the lanterns. All of them."

The crew hesitates, then scrambles. One by one the lanterns flare to life, and each burns a different color: amber, violet, a green like new leaves. The light doesn't push back the dark. It *talks* to it, and the clouds begin to part like curtains on a stage.

Beyond them, the sea grows calm and mirror-still, and the sky is full of upside-down stars reflected from somewhere far below. Ahead, a lighthouse rises from the water. Its beam is slow and steady, and it sweeps across the deck like a greeting.

The first mate leans close. "Do we go to it, Captain?"

You notice the ship has already begun to turn, the wheel moving gently under your hands. The lighthouse door stands open, and in the doorway is a figure who looks familiar, though you can't say why. They are holding a ship's wheel identical to yours, and they are smiling as if they have been waiting a long time for you to arrive.

You take a breath, and the dream holds still, waiting for you to decide what comes next.

*Shall we sail on and see who it is?*
