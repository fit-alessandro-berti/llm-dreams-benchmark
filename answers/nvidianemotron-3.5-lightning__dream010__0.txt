You are participating in a competition where the grand prize is your dream job. As the competition progresses, you face various challenges and find that each obstacle isn't a test of skill, but a mirror: the first task asks you to draw the silence between heartbeats, the second forces you to navigate a labyrinth whose walls are made of your own forgotten "no"s, and the third leaves you floating in a room filled with every version of the job you once wanted, each one whispering a different reason you let it go. The final challenge isn't a test at all—it's simply a door that only opens when you stop performing and actually listen to the quiet competence you've been carrying all along. Behind it, the job isn't a title or a salary, but a space where your peculiar obsessions and hard-earned strengths are not just accepted, but are the very reason you were invited. The judges—faceless but warm—hand you not a certificate, but the key to a workshop that only exists when you step inside.
