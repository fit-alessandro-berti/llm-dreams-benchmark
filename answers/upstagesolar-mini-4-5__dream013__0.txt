As you move deeper into the maze, the puzzles grow more intricate—their rules shifting like riddles wrapped in equations—and you begin to notice that the walls themselves are not merely stone but stories: carvings of numbers, maps of stars, echoes of questions you've asked before. You realize the maze is not hiding a single exit but a pattern, and that solving each puzzle is not a step forward but a way of learning how to think differently, until finally the maze dissolves into a single quiet question floating in the center of the floor, and you understand that the answer was never a place—it was the way you were already moving.
