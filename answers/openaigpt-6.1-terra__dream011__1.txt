The first question asks, *Where did you leave the key?* The second is a drawing of your childhood kitchen. The third simply says, *Hurry.*

You look up. Everyone else is writing furiously, though their pencils make no sound. The clock above the door has no hands, only a small red eye that follows you as you turn the page.

There’s a key taped to the back of the exam.

You raise your hand, but the professor is gone. In her place stands a door you’re certain wasn’t there before. A small brass plaque reads: **YOUR FUTURE**.

The key fits. You turn it, take a breath, and step through—

into the same exam room, where someone who looks exactly like you is already sitting at your desk, writing the final answer.
