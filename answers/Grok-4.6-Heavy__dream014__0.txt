You are feeling lost and alone in a bustling city. Suddenly, a group of kind strangers offers to help you find your way, and you take their hands before you even decide to.

The crowd parts like water. Neon rewrites itself into street names you almost remember. One stranger laughs with your mother’s laugh; another has a scar you thought only you remembered. They do not ask where you were going. They walk you through streets that keep changing their names, past windows that show rooms from houses you have not lived in yet.

The noise folds shut behind you like a curtain. You step into a quiet square that feels like the inside of your own chest. You are no longer lost. You are being found. When you look down, your shadow is no longer walking alone.
