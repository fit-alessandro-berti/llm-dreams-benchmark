You find yourself in a post-apocalyptic world, with resources scarce and dangers everywhere. Despite the hardships, you decide to plant a garden.

There is no soil left in the city, so you carry a cracked teacup to the roof of an abandoned library and fill it with dust from the stairwell. You bury three seeds you found sewn into the hem of your coat. Each morning, you give them a few drops from your drinking water.

For days, nothing happens.

Then, one night, a green shoot appears. By dawn it has wound around the chimney, crossed the roof, and climbed into the sky. Leaves unfold overhead, broad enough to shelter the whole city. People emerge from hiding to stare up at them. Rain begins to fall, and nobody runs from it.

You reach into your coat for the last seed, but find a small paper label instead. In handwriting you almost recognize, it says: *You can wake up now. The others know what to do.*
