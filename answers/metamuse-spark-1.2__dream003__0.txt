You are given a mysterious locked box with no key in sight. As you examine it closely, you notice intricate patterns and...

...they aren't carved at all.

They are *moving*.

At first you think it's a trick of the light, but as you hold your breath, you see it clearly - thin lines of gold and deep blue light tracing over the dark wood like veins, like rivers, like constellations slowly swirling just beneath the surface. When you tilt the box, the patterns tilt with you.

You run your fingertip across the lid and the lines ripple away from your touch, as if the box is shy. It's warm. Faintly warm, like skin. And it hums - not a sound exactly, but a low vibration you feel more in your teeth than in your ears. A quiet, patient heartbeat. *Thump. Thump. Thump.*

There is no keyhole. There was never a keyhole. The seam where the lid should open is covered by a perfect, unbroken spiral pattern right in the center. It looks like a fingerprint. No - it *is* a fingerprint. As you lean closer, you realize with a strange jolt that it's yours.

The hum gets a little louder when you understand. The box isn't locked to keep you out.

It's locked to wait for you to remember how to open it.

You don't need a key. You need to answer it.

Instinctively, you place your entire palm flat over the spiral. The light flares, warm and bright, and the lines rush to meet your hand, aligning perfectly with the whorls and arches of your own skin. The patterns lock into place with a soft *click* that you feel all the way up your arm.

The lid doesn't creak open. It *dissolves*. Like mist burned away by morning sun.

Inside, there is no gold, no jewel, no letter.

Inside is light. Soft, deep, endless light - and in the light, a small, quiet scene floats like a snow globe without glass. You see yourself, much younger, sleeping peacefully in a bed. On the nightstand next to that younger you is this exact box, closed, waiting.

And you understand, in the strange, perfect logic that only exists in dreams - you have been carrying this box your whole life. You just finally stopped to look at it.

You reach inside to touch that light, and just as your fingers are about to brush it...

You wake up.

Or do you?
