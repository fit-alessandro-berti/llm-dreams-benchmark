You are participating in a competition where the grand prize is your dream job. As the competition progresses, you face various challenges and obstacles, each one seemingly designed to test not just your skills but your character. 

The first challenge is a puzzle: you must navigate a labyrinth of mirrors, each reflecting a different version of yourself—some confident, some terrified, some entirely unfamiliar. To move forward, you must choose which reflection to trust. You realize the only way out is to walk through the mirror showing you as you truly are, flaws and all. The mirrors shatter, and you step into a hall of whispers, where each voice offers a different job offer—a comfortable office, a risky startup, a fellowship abroad. None of them sound like your dream. Then, a quiet voice speaks from the shadows, and it sounds just like your own. It says, "The dream job isn't one of these. It's the one you haven't built yet."

You wake up to the sound of your alarm, and the competition is still ahead—but you have a strange new resolve.
