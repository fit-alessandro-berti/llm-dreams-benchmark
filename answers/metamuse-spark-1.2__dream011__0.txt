You notice the questions are not questions at all.

At first you think it's a printing error. The paper is blank. Pure, stark white. You blink, and then the ink *bleeds* onto the page slowly, like it's being written in real time.

You look closer.

**1. When was the last time you did something just because you loved it, even though you weren't good at it?**

You frown. You flip the page. This isn't your subject. This isn't anything you studied.

**2. Describe the day you decided this career was what you wanted. Was it your decision?**

**3. If no one would be disappointed, angry, or proud - what would you write on the next blank page?**

**4. Draw the life you want in 10 years. You have 30 seconds. Don't think.**

Your heart starts pounding. This is wrong. You look up to ask the professor for the right exam, but the professor is your 8-year-old self, swinging their legs under the desk at the front of the room, watching you patiently.

You look around the room. All the other students are writing furiously, heads down. You glance at the paper of the person next to you. Their exam is completely different.

Theirs says: *"List every reason you should not be afraid."*

The clock on the wall isn't moving. It has no numbers, just the words *TOO LATE* and *JUST IN TIME* where the 12 and 6 should be. Both hands are pointing at *JUST IN TIME*.

You look back down at your paper and your hand is already moving on its own, answering question 4. You're not drawing a corner office or a salary or a job title. You're drawing something you haven't thought about in years - that little workshop, that bookstore, that studio, that silly childhood dream you told yourself was impractical.

And for the first time since you walked into the exam hall, you understand.

This was never a test of what you memorized.

It was a test of what you remembered about yourself before you were told who you were supposed to become.

The final question fades in at the bottom of the page, in your own handwriting:

**5. Are you ready to choose? Yes / No**

Your pencil hovers over the paper.
