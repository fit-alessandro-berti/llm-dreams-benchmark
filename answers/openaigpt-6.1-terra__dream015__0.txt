You find a handful of seeds in the pocket of an abandoned coat and carry them to the roof of an old library. Each morning, you climb the broken stairs with a cup of water. Each night, you listen for footsteps below.

For weeks, nothing grows. Then a green shoot appears between two cracked bricks.

Others begin to notice. A woman leaves a jar of rainwater by the door. A boy brings a rusted trowel. Someone paints a small sign at the foot of the stairs:

**PLEASE USE THE OTHER ENTRANCE. THE ROOF IS GROWING.**

By autumn, there are tomatoes, beans, and more people than the library has seen in years. They trade stories while they work, and for a little while, the world feels less empty.

Then, one morning, you wake to find the tallest sunflower has turned toward you.

“Finally,” it says. “I thought you’d never fall asleep.”
