…you begin to notice the puzzles are not just locks to pick—they are memories in disguise. Each solved riddle opens a corridor that smells faintly of a forgotten year: a summer kitchen, a hospital hallway, a road at dusk. The deeper you go, the less the maze feels built around you and the more it feels built *from* you. Walls murmur in voices you almost recognize. Finally, you reach a small chamber with no door, only a mirror and a single blank tile. The last puzzle is written there: **“What are you still trying to solve?”** You answer honestly, and the maze folds inward like a paper crane. You wake with the answer still warm in your hands.
