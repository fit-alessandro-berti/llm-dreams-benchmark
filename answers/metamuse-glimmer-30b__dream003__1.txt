You are given a mysterious locked box with no key in sight. As you examine it closely, you notice intricate patterns and symbols etched into the metal, but they aren't static. They move when you're not looking directly at them, like tiny rivers of light crawling under the surface.

You run your fingers over the cold lid and the patterns warm under your touch, pulsing faintly in time with your heartbeat. Each symbol you trace glows briefly and then fades, leaving behind a faint after-image in your vision.

A soft click sounds from somewhere inside, though the box is still sealed. You lean in closer and hear it again — not a mechanical click, but a voice, very small and very far away, saying a word you almost recognize.

The box suddenly feels heavier, as if something inside is settling. You try to lift it, but your hands pass through it for a half-second, and the box is suddenly in your chest, not in your hands. You can feel its edges against your ribs.

You panic and try to open your eyes, but there is no waking up in this dream. Instead the room around you thins out, and the box is the only thing left, floating in a dark, quiet space. The lock is gone now. The patterns have spread across the whole surface, forming a map of a place you've never been but somehow miss.

You realize you don't need a key. You just need to remember what the box is asking you for, and the lid begins to lift on its own.
