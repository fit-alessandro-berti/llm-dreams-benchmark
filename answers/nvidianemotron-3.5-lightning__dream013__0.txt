You are in a maze filled with complex puzzles. Each puzzle requires intense concentration and logical thinking to solve, and as you move deeper into the maze, you find that the walls themselves are made of half-remembered equations, and each solution doesn't just open a door—it reshapes the geometry around you, turning corridors into impossible angles and riddles into constellations of light, until the heart of the maze opens not into a chamber, but into a vast, star-filled sky where the puzzles dissolve into wind, and you realize the only way out was the part of you that kept solving even when the path vanished.
