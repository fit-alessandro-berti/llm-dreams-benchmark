Panicking, you duck into the nearest tavern to gather your bearings. But the tavern door swallows you whole—teleporting you into a corridor where the walls are made of breathing, moss-covered sponge and the ceiling slowly drips heavy, glowing honey. You don't question this; you simply accept the honey light.

Following the trail of honey, you spiral downward and emerge suddenly onto a vast plaza bordered by towering obsidian monoliths. The street signs here aren’t written in any alphabet you know; they’re more like shifting inkblots that occasionally arrange themselves into the shape of a clock face, ticking away the minutes.

A street vendor rolls by on a cart painted with swirling, metallic colors. He has no face, just a mouth floating where his nose should be. "Are you lost?" he asks, his words tasting faintly of cinnamon. You nod, desperately clutching the brass ingot. He hands you a napkin with a route drawn on it in hot, red wax. 

Following the red line, you climb a flight of steps that slowly curve upward into the sky, detaching from the pavement entirely. You walk on air. The sky shifts from a twilight violet to a deep, resonant green. You arrive at a door hovering in the middle of the atmosphere, framed by nothing but clouds. 

You knock, and the door rings like a bell. You step through the threshold. Suddenly, the floor materializes beneath your feet as polished white tile. You are in a sterile conference room suspended in an infinite void. A long glass table stretches out before you, refracting the light of a thousand miniature suns dancing along its edge.

The others for the meeting are already sitting there, draped in suits made of shadow, their faces blurred like sanctioned masks. They are all speaking at once, in a harmonic, underwater tone that you somehow perfectly understand. The clock on the wall is a blinking, slow-moving eye, confirming you arrived with two minutes to spare. You pull out your chair—and as you sit, the air tastes faintly of cinnamon again, locking you perfectly into the snapshot of a surreal life.
