“Be gentle,” it whispers.

You touch its tail to the canvas. A blue door appears, then opens onto a room full of rain falling upward. You step inside, and the door folds itself into a paper bird and flies away.

At the center of the room sits a table set for two. Across from your empty chair is a version of you made entirely of shifting colors. It pours tea into your cup, though the teapot is full of stars.

“Is this my dream?” you ask.

Your colorful self considers this. “I was about to ask you the same thing.”

You sit together in comfortable silence until the stars in your cup begin to dim. Then your companion reaches across the table and takes your hand. For a moment, the colors settle. The rain hangs motionless. Somewhere far away, an alarm clock begins to sing.

“Come back when you can,” your companion says.

You promise you will, though when you wake, you can no longer remember which of you said it.
