For a moment, the ship glides through towering waves frozen like walls of dark glass. Inside them, you see strange things: a whale swimming through a forest, a city lit by stars, your childhood home.

Then a great eye opens beneath the water.

The crew gasps, but you recognize it somehow. You turn the wheel toward the eye, and the sea folds open before you, revealing a narrow path of moonlight. The ship sails down it, deeper and deeper, until the clouds become the ocean and the ocean becomes the sky.

At last, you arrive at a quiet harbor. Someone is waiting on the shore, holding a lantern and waving as though you are exactly on time.
