...and each group seems to be speaking a language you almost understand. One cluster laughs softly, and you catch your name in their laughter—but when you turn, their faces are blurry, like watercolors smudged by rain. Another group offers you a drink that changes color as you reach for it, and someone whispers, “You’re looking for the wrong person.” You wander through rooms that keep rearranging themselves, past a hallway of doors that all lead to the same balcony, until you hear a familiar voice humming a song you used to love. You follow it through a curtain of paper lanterns, and there they are—your one person—smiling like they’ve been waiting for you the whole time. “Where did you go?” you ask. They tilt their head, confused. “I never left. You’re the one who kept walking past me.” And then you wake up, the feeling of their hand still warm on your wrist, even though you know it was just a dream.
