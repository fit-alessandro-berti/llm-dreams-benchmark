Instead of paint, a forest of silver-fractal trees emerges, reaching out toward you. Their leaves aren't made of matter, but of softly glowing, chilled wind that shimmers as it brushes against your skin. The branches twist and unfurl into letters from an alphabet you have never seen, yet somehow understand.

Around you, the chaotic, shifting landscape that was previously bleeding neon greens and ultraviolet purples suddenly pauses. The floating geometries stop their random tumbling and fall into perfect alignment, transforming from chaos into a quiet, respectful audience standing beside your silver forest.

Curious, you reach out to touch one of the hovering, glowing letters. The moment your fingertips make contact, the world inhales. Space folds around you, and the sky shatters upward into a million floating mirrors. Each mirror reflects a slightly different version of the canvas, a million different drawings bleeding into one another.

You step backward, retreating from the canvases, and watch as your silver forest begins to un-grow. The branches retract, the letters dissolve into light, and the trees drain backward into the empty brush. The canvas flattens once more, condensing its entire history into a single, blindingly bright orb of floating indigo.

You reach out to hold the orb. But as your fingers cup it, you realize the sphere isn't an object at all. It is a pupil—your eye—slowly dilating as the dream collapses inward. The surreal landscape folds itself into your palm, the colors and shapes whirling into a singular point of white, before vanishing completely. 

And then, there is only the quiet, vast dark, waiting for the morning to begin.
