The competition is held in a railway station beneath a sky full of turning pages. Above the platforms, the departure board lists no cities—only **ALMOST**, **SOMEDAY**, **IF ONLY**, and **TOO LATE**. At stake is a silver envelope containing your dream job. Whenever you try to read the title through the paper, the words swim away.

For the first challenge, you must board a train that has no tracks. The other contestants study maps and argue about engines. You stand at the edge of the platform, terrified, until you notice a narrow line of silver appearing beneath the judge’s feet whenever she moves.

So you step forward.

A rail flashes into existence beneath your shoe. Another step, another rail. Behind you, the contestants begin shouting—not in mockery now, but in encouragement. By the time the train arrives, you have walked far enough into the empty air that it simply curves down to meet you.

The second challenge takes place in a library where every book is blank. “Find the one that explains why you deserve to win,” the judge instructs.

You search until your fingers turn blue with dust. Finally, in the smallest room, you discover a single book with your name on its spine. You open it, expecting an answer, but the pages remain empty. So you borrow a pen and write:

*I don’t know yet. But I am willing to learn.*

The library shelves fold themselves into a staircase.

At the top, only one room remains. It contains a desk, two chairs, and a window overlooking a city still under construction. The judge sits opposite you. Up close, you see that her hair is full of tiny stars.
