Behind you, the ground splits open and reveals an upside-down forest hanging from an invisible ceiling. The trees are made of stained glass, and their leaves are pages of old books you once read but can't quite remember. Each leaf that falls becomes a butterfly with wings shaped like compass needles, all pointing in different directions.

You walk through the glass forest barefoot, and the ground beneath you is warm — like sun-heated stone. Suddenly, a figure steps out from between two trunks. It looks exactly like you, but wearing a different skin — shifting between ages, from child to elder and back again in a slow, breathing rhythm.

"Are you me?" you ask.

They smile and hold out their hand. Sitting on their palm is a tiny door, no bigger than a coin, with light pouring from underneath it.

"Every color you've ever seen," they say, "you only dreamed."

You kneel down, press your eye to the keyhole, and see *everything* — every moment of every life, folded into a single infinite spiral, humming a lullaby you haven't heard since before you were born.
