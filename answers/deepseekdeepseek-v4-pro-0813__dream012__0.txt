You are the captain of a ship in the middle of a turbulent sea. Your crew looks to you for guidance as the storm worsens, and you realize the thunder is keeping time with your own heartbeat. You don’t shout orders; instead, you place a hand on the nearest sailor’s shoulder and say, “This storm is just the dream trying to wake us.” The crew’s fear softens into wonder.

You turn the wheel toward the tallest wave, which rises like a dark cathedral. The ship climbs and climbs, and then the wave doesn’t crash—it lifts you through the roof of the storm into a quiet dawn. Below, the sea flattens into a mirror. You see your own face reflected, sleeping, and you let go of the wheel as the dream gently sets you back into your bed.
