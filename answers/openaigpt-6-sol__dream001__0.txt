You start to ask a passerby for directions, but the moment you say “meeting,” every clock in the city begins to chime.

The passerby points across the street, where a narrow doorway has appeared between two buildings. A brass plaque reads **YOU ARE EXPECTED**.

Inside, a staircase rises into the clouds. You climb it two steps at a time, worried you’ll be late, until you notice that each step has a different time written on it. You stop on a step marked *one hour ago*. Through a window, you can see yourself arriving at the meeting: out of breath, but smiling.

Then the person at the head of the table turns around. It’s you, much older, holding an empty chair open.

“You’re right on time,” they say.
