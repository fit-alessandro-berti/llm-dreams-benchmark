The box grows warm in your hands. Somewhere inside, a clock ticks backward.

You trace the brightest star with your finger, and the lid opens without a sound. Inside is a small paper boat floating on a pool of moonlight. As you lean closer, the boat unfolds into a note, written in your own handwriting:

*You left this here so you could find your way back.*

The floor beneath you turns to water. You step into the boat, which is suddenly large enough to hold you, and drift toward a distant shore where someone is waiting with a lantern.

Just before you reach them, they lift the light—and you recognize yourself, a little younger, smiling as though you’ve been expected all along.
