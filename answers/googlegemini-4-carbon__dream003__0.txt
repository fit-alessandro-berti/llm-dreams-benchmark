The ink immediately flows upward, spilling onto my skin, tracing the lines of my palm. It tickles, cold and bright like peppermint and starlight.

Inside, there is no velvet lining, no hidden treasure. Instead, the interior drops away into an endless, dizzying chasm of deep blue sky. From the depths of the box rises the unmistakable scent of autumn bonfires and rain, accompanied by a faint, muffled melody—a song I used to know all the words to, though I can't remember its name.
