Each stroke you make on the canvas reshapes the world beyond; a burst of orange ignites a sunrise over the distant hills, and a ripple of indigo summons a river of liquid night that flows through the landscape. In the center of the canvas, a mirror‑like surface reflects not your face but the dream itself—a living portrait of your thoughts, constantly rewriting the environment. You realize the blankness was never truly empty; it was a waiting screen for the colors of your imagination, and as you paint, the world bends to match the story you choose to tell.
