You tap it. Thunder rolls through the auditorium. In the front row, a woman opens a yellow umbrella, and you recognize her as your third-grade teacher. She gives you the little nod she used to give when you got stuck reading aloud.

You look down at your notes. The first page says, in your own handwriting: *Tell them about the duck.*

You don’t know what duck. You say so.

A man in the balcony stands up. “Oh, thank goodness,” he says. “I thought I was the only one.”

Then, from somewhere behind the curtain, comes a single, indignant quack.
