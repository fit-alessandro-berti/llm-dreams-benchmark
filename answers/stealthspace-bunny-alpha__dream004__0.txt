…and the instant your fingers touch it, the world goes quiet.

The canvas hums beneath your palm—warm as sunlight, cool as shallow water. Shapes begin to move across its surface: a staircase folding into a bird, a circle stretching into a doorway, a shadow learning to ripple. They rearrange themselves with every breath.

Beside the canvas, you find a brush you don’t remember picking up. Its bristles shimmer like captured starfire.

You paint a single stroke.

It doesn’t stay on the canvas. It leaps into the air and unfurls into a ribbon of aurora, wrapping around the moon. For a moment, the moon smiles. Then it folds itself into a flower.

You laugh and paint again.

Amber rivers spiral upward. Indigo birds scatter into geometric fragments. The ground softens beneath your feet, becoming clouds, becoming wings, becoming a path that leads farther into the sky. With every brushstroke, the horizon widens until there is no horizon at all—only color, shifting shapes, and room for one more impossible thing.

At last, the brush falls silent.

The canvas is no longer blank.

It has become a window—and beyond it, a new world is waiting for its first dream.
