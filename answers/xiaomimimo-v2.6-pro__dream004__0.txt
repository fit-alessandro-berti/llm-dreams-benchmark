...a palette of paints that shimmer like liquid starlight.

You dip your fingers into the colors and notice they *feel* like emotions — the blue is calm, the red is exhilarating, the green hums with growth. As you drag them across the canvas, the world around you *responds*. Where you paint a tree, the morphing chaos of the landscape momentarily stills, and a real crystalline tree takes root in the ground before you. Where you paint a river, water flows between the floating islands.

But the canvas is small, and the world is infinite.

You paint faster. A bird — it takes flight and sings a note so pure that all the shifting shapes freeze in place, harmonizing around it like a sudden chorus. A door — and it *opens*, revealing a room made entirely of remembered conversations, whispered words echoing off walls like wind chimes.

You realize you are not simply painting the world. You are *remembering* it into existence. Every brushstroke is an anchor in a dream that wants to dissolve back into formless, shifting color.

The blank canvas shrinks with each use.

A voice — your own, but from somewhere behind you — whispers, *"Save some for morning."*

You look down at the canvas. Only a small corner remains unpainted. You think carefully about what deserves that last space. A sky? A face? A word?

You close your eyes and paint from memory. When you open them again, the canvas is gone, and in its place stands a **window**. Through it, you see a world of ordinary, imperfect, *beautiful* things — the messy, stubborn world that doesn't need magic to be worth returning to.

The colors begin to shift again. The dream is ending.

But somewhere, one crystalline tree stands still, remembering the moment it was painted into being.
