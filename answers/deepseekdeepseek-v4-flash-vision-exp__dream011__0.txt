You flip through the pages. Each question is impossible—until you look at it sideways. Then the words turn into images: a river of clocks, a door made of music, a small fox asking you to solve a riddle about your own name. You realize the exam is not testing what you memorized. It’s asking you what you truly want.

You reach the last page. There is one question: *“What will you do with the rest of your life?”* Before you can write anything, the paper folds itself into a paper crane and flies to the ceiling. The professor is now a giant cat. The cat says, “The exam is over. You already know the answer.”

Then the alarm goes off. But when you wake, there’s a note on your pillow that wasn’t there before. It says, *“Good. Now go live it.”*
