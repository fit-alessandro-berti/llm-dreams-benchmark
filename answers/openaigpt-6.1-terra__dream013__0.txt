You solve each one, but the answers vanish from your mind as soon as you speak them.

At the center of the maze, you find a small room with no doors. A mirror hangs on the wall, and beneath it is the final puzzle:

**What have you been looking for?**

You stare at your reflection. It looks tired, but patient. You try every answer you can think of—an exit, a treasure, the way home. Nothing happens.

Then your reflection raises a hand and touches the glass. You do the same. It is warm.

“I was looking for you,” you say.

The walls unfold like paper, and morning light spills in.
