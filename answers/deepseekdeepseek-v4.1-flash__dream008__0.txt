You are at your workplace, and suddenly, you are given a project with an impossible deadline. Your boss is waiting for you to start, and you notice the clock has no hands. The project brief is a blank page. Every time you blink, the office empties a little more; your boss’s voice slows to a hum. Instead of panicking, you stand, walk to the whiteboard, and draw a door. It opens. On the other side is a version of you who has already finished, already failed, already survived. They hand you a folder and say, “Just start anywhere.” Inside is a single sentence: “The deadline was never real.” Then the alarm rings, and you wake up with your hand already reaching for a pen.
