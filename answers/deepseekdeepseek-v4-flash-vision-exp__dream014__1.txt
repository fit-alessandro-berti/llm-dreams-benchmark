You hesitate at first, still wrapped in that fog of loneliness. But one of them smiles—gentle, familiar somehow—and takes your hand. They lead you through crowds that part like water, and the city’s noise softens into a hum of laughter. You pass glowing windows, each one showing a different life, and they tell you stories that feel like memories you forgot you had. When you reach a quiet courtyard bathed in moonlight, you realize you’re not lost anymore. You’re home—not a place, but the feeling of being found. They nod, still smiling, and fade into the light as the dream cradles you, whispering: *You were never alone.*
