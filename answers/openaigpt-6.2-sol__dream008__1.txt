“How much time do I have?” you ask.

“Until the elevator gets here,” your boss says.

You can hear it coming up through the building. It stops at every floor. At each stop, someone gets in and asks, “Is it done?”

You open the project folder. Inside is a photograph of your desk, taken from just behind your chair. In the photograph, you’ve already gone home. Your coffee cup is washed and upside down on a paper towel.

You stare at it for a while. Then you take your cup to the break room and wash it. The water is warm. It’s the first thing all day that feels like it’s happening at the right speed.

When the elevator arrives, your boss holds the door for you. Inside, six people are holding their coats.

“Is it done?” one of them asks.

You look down. Your hands are empty.

“I’m going home,” you say.

“Oh, good,” she says, and presses the button for ground.
