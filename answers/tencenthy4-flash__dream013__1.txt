…begin to notice that the puzzles are changing.

The symbols on the stone doors rearrange when you look away. A riddle about mirrors asks for your reflection, but the reflection answers first. Corridors twist into spirals, and every turn demands not just logic, but trust — the kind of trust you give a staircase in the dark.

At the center of the maze, you find a room made entirely of glass. In it floats a single key, suspended above a pedestal engraved with the words:

**“The way out is the question you have not yet asked.”**

You lean closer. The key turns into a question mark.

Then the walls begin to whisper numbers, and the floor shifts beneath you like a sleeping animal. You solve the final puzzle by forgetting everything you know, and suddenly the maze folds inward like paper.

You step through the crease —

and wake with the taste of ink on your tongue, certain that somewhere, a locked door has just opened.
