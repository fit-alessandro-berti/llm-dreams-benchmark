...and the air turns cold and electric, as if the forest has drawn a breath and held it. The trees begin to sway in unison, their leaves whispering warnings you almost understand. You start to run, but the path stretches longer with every step, and the storm follows close behind—not a storm of rain, but of memories: old voices, forgotten rooms, faces you’ve loved and lost swirling through the wind.

Then lightning splits the sky, and for one bright instant the forest is gone. You are standing in a quiet house you’ve never seen before, rain tapping softly on the windows. A familiar voice calls your name from the next room. You reach for the door handle—and wake just as your fingers touch it, with the distant rumble of thunder fading from your bedroom walls.
