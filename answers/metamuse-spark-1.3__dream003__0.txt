Of course — let's slip back into that dream:

---

You are given a mysterious locked box with no key in sight. As you examine it closely, you notice intricate patterns and — wait — they aren't just carved. They are *moving*.

Thin lines of gold and deep blue swirl slowly across the dark wood like rivers seen from the sky. When your fingers hover over them, they warm slightly, pulsing in time with your heartbeat.

There is no keyhole, no latch. Only a small, smooth circle in the center, about the size of your thumbprint.

In the logic of the dream, you just know what to do. You press your thumb there.

*Click.*

Not a loud sound, but a soft one, like a memory unlocking. The lid doesn't pop open — it unfolds. First one corner peels back like paper, then another, then another, until the box is no longer a box at all, but a flat star of petals in your palms.

In the middle, there is no treasure of coins or jewels. There is light. A small, warm, breathing light. And inside the light you hear something faint — a music box melody you haven't heard since you were a child, but instantly remember.

As you lean closer to listen, the light rises, floats up to eye-level, and gently dissolves into tiny moths made of sparks. They circle you once, twice, and then fly off into the darkness of the dream, lighting up a path you hadn't seen before.

You understand, without anyone telling you, that the box was never locked to keep you out. It was locked to wait until you were ready to open it with just a touch.

And you start walking down that lit path, curious about where it leads...
