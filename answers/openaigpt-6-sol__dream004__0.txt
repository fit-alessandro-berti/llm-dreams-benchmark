pick up a brush that feels warm in your hand. Before you can choose a color, the brush paints a small blue door in the middle of the canvas.

You touch the door. It opens onto a staircase made of folded paper, rising into a sky full of slowly turning moons. With every step, the colors around you settle: red becomes the scent of rain, green becomes the sound of leaves, and gold feels like sunlight on your face.

At the top, you find another canvas. This one holds a painting of your bedroom, just as you left it—except its window is open. A breeze moves through the painted curtains and brushes your cheek.

You step closer. The staircase rustles beneath you, and you wake to morning light spilling across the floor. For a moment, you can still smell rain.
