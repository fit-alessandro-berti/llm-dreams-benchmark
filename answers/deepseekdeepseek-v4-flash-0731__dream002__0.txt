...the air turns thick and electric. The trees, once whispering softly, begin to sway and groan. Leaves spiral upward like startled birds. You stop, heart thrumming, and look to the sky—clouds boiling from pale gray to deep violet, a spiral of silver light twisting at their center.

Then the wind hushes. The forest holds its breath.

A single drop of rain lands on your forehead. It feels warm, almost alive. Then another, and another, until the whole world is humming with the sound of water on leaves. But the rain isn’t cold—it glows faintly, each drop carrying a tiny pulse of light, like falling stars.

You realize the storm is not just weather. It is speaking.

A voice, woven from thunder and dripping branches, rises around you: *“You have walked far. Now walk deeper.”*

The ground begins to shimmer, roots rising like gentle hands, opening a path where none existed before. You feel no fear—only a strange, magnetic knowing. Somewhere beyond the curtain of glowing rain, something is waiting for you. Something that has always been waiting.

And in the dream, you step forward, into the storm’s luminous heart.
