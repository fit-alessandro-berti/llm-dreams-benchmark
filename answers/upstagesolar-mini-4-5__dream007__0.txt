The very ground beneath you ripples like water. You try to speak, but only bubbles come out. The argument doesn't end, it transforms. You're not in a living room anymore, but floating in a vast, silent space that is somehow both the room and the sea. Your friend's voice, now a low, resonant hum, echoes from everywhere at once: "You have to decide what you're made of." You realize the argument was never about the original topic. It was a metaphor. The heated confrontation was the storm outside, the pressure you've been holding in. And now, the choice is to either pull into the depths and sink, or to find the light that pierces the surface and break through. The water is waiting, and for the first time, you don't feel the need to argue with it. You just have to swim.
