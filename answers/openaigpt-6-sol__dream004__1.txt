A blue doorway appears on the canvas. When you touch it, your hand passes through, so you step inside. On the other side is a quiet garden where every flower is a different color, but none of them has a name. The brush follows you, painting labels in the air. Each time it names a flower, that flower turns gray.

You gently take the brush and paint a question mark instead. The gray flowers bloom with color again, and the labels drift upward like little clouds. A path unfolds beneath your feet, winding toward the sound of the sea.

When you reach the shore, the waves are folding blank canvases into paper boats. You climb into one and let it carry you toward the horizon, where the sun is still deciding what shape to be.
