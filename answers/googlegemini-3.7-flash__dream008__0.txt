You try to speak, to explain that the physics of the task are impossible, but your voice comes out as a quiet rustle of dry autumn leaves. 

Your boss leans down closer, tapping a wristwatch that has no dials, only a tiny goldfish swimming in circles behind the glass. "We're losing daylight," they whisper.

And then, a sudden, weightless clarity washes over you. 

You look down and realize the office floor has completely dissolved into the surface of a calm, midnight-blue ocean. The fluorescent lights overhead hum a single, beautiful musical chord, and you realize how absurd the urgency is.

You push back from the desk, your feet lifting off the ground as gravity quietly lets go of you. You look at your boss, hand them the water-pages, and simply say, *"I don't work here."*

The office walls peel backward like wrapping paper, blowing away into the dark. The desk, the stones, and the goldfish sink harmlessly into the depths, and you float upward into a sky full of violet stars, light, unburdened, and entirely free.
