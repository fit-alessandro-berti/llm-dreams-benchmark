Your boss checks a watch with no hands.

“End of day,” they remind you.

Outside the windows, the sun drops six inches.

You chase the keys under a filing cabinet and find a staircase where the carpet should be. Below it, you can hear the familiar whine of the office printer. You descend, carrying your empty keyboard like a tray.

The basement is enormous. Printers stretch into the distance, each one producing a single page. You take the nearest sheet.

**PROJECT COMPLETE**, it says.

Underneath, in your own handwriting:

*Please deliver to the ocean.*

When you turn around, your boss is standing on the stairs, holding your coat. They look much younger now, and frightened.

“Do you know the way?” they ask.

You don’t, but you put on the coat. Something heavy settles in its pockets. You reach inside and find all the missing keys, warm and faintly moving.

Beyond the printers, a wave breaks against the loading dock.

You carry the page down to the water. Before you can let it go, the wet edge curls around your finger.

Behind you, your boss calls, “Will you be back before five?”

You turn to answer, but the office is already too far out to sea.
