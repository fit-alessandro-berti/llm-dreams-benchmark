You feel a storm approaching and the air thickens with anticipation. The first gusts sweep through the canopy, scattering leaves like confetti, while distant thunder rumbles like a drumbeat beneath the earth. Rain begins to fall in sheets, each drop a tiny mirror reflecting the flickering lightning that splits the sky. The trees sway, their branches reaching out as if trying to catch the falling water, and the scent of wet moss rises—sweet and sharp all at once.

In the midst of the chaos, a strange calm settles over you. A luminous doorway opens at the foot of a towering oak, its edges glowing with a soft amber light. The storm seems to part around it, as if respecting the threshold. Drawn by an unseen force, you step through, and the world beyond dissolves into a swirl of colors—deep blues, violet, and gold—each hue whispering a memory you had forgotten.

Inside the vortex, fleeting images appear: a child laughing under a summer sun, a quiet night of stargazing, moments of joy and sorrow intertwined. The storm outside becomes a distant murmur, as if the forest itself is breathing in rhythm with your heart. When you finally emerge, the rain has slowed to a gentle drizzle, and the forest is bathed in a soft, ethereal glow. The storm has passed, leaving behind a trail of sparkling droplets that linger like tiny stars on the leaves.

You stand there, feeling the afterglow of the tempest, knowing that the forest will always hold the memory of this fleeting, magical moment. The peace returns, but the echo of the storm lingers, reminding you that change—however sudden—can be both terrifying and beautiful.
