You start to run, but the streets rearrange themselves with every step—alleys folding into courtyards, staircases rising where crosswalks should be. A clock tower looms ahead, its hands spinning backward. You duck into a café whose menu is written in a language you almost remember, and the barista slides you a cup that tastes like the meeting you were supposed to attend. Outside, your own voice calls your name from a passing tram. You chase it.
