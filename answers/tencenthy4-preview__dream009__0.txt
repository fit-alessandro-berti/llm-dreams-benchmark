Your sneakers sink into thick, foul-smelling mud as you step out onto the main thoroughfare. You look painfully out of place. Your modern clothes are far too bright and pristine against the drab, earthy tones of this medieval town. The air is a chaotic symphony of unfamiliar sounds: the rhythmic clanging of a blacksmith’s hammer, the bleating of livestock, and the overlapping cadences of a language you somehow understand but have never formally learned. Towering over you are crooked, timber-framed houses with sagging roofs of straw. They lean precariously over the street, blocking out the sky and casting long, crooked shadows.

Panic begins to rise in your chest. You duck into a dark, narrow alleyway to catch your breath and figure out your next move. You reach into your pocket for your phone, desperate for a map or a familiar distraction, but your fingers only brush against rough, heavy fabric. 

You look down and realize that in the short time you were walking, the dream has shifted. Your modern clothes are gone. In their place, you are wearing a coarse linen tunic, a heavy wool cloak, and scuffed leather boots that lace up to your knees. 

A satchel hangs heavily from your shoulder. You open it with trembling hands, and instead of your wallet and keys, you find a small pouch of hand-struck silver coins—stamped with the profile of a stern, crowned king—a lump of hard yellow cheese, and a folded piece of thick parchment sealed with red wax. 

Just as you begin to inspect the parchment, a heavy hand clamps down on your shoulder. You spin around, your breath catching in your throat. A weathered stranger stands before you, his face half-obscured by the shadows of his hood. 

"The ship leaves on the evening tide," he growls, his eyes darting nervously toward the busy street. "Do you have the papers?"

You don't know who he is. You don't know what papers he's talking about, and you have no idea how you got here. But then, the rational part of your brain catches up with you, reminding you of the strange, detached feeling that has been lingering in the back of your mind since you woke up: *you are dreaming.* 

The realization washes over you like a wave of calm. The rules of the waking world don't apply here. You don't have to be afraid of the unknown; you can shape it. 

You meet the stranger's gaze, allowing a small, confident smirk to touch your lips. You reach into your satchel and pull out the folded parchment. 

"I have them right here," you say, surprised by the steady, melodic lilt of your own voice. "Lead the way."
