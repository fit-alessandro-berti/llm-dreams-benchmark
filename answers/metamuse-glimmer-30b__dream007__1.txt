You are in the middle of a heated argument with a close friend. The disagreement is about something important to both of you, and you decide to **stop talking and just walk out of the room.**

You push the door open and the hallway is wrong. It's too long, lit with that buzzing yellow light from school hallways, and your shoes make no sound on the floor. Your friend doesn't follow. They just stay there, yelling your name, but their voice is muffled like they're speaking through a pillow.

You keep walking because walking feels safer than listening. The walls are covered in Polaroids of you two, but the faces in them keep changing — sometimes it's you, sometimes it's someone you don't recognize, sometimes it's just blank.

At the end of the hall there's a kitchen you know is yours, but the table is full of water up to your ankles and your friend's old houseplant is floating there, still alive, leaves dripping. You sit down in it anyway and try to explain why you're leaving, and your mouth opens but instead of words, a small flock of paper cranes flies out.

Your friend finally catches up, stands in the doorway, and is suddenly wearing your childhood pajamas. They say, "You were never going to let me finish," and then they dissolve into steam.

You wake up before you can answer.
