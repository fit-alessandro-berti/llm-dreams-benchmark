You are given a mysterious locked box with no key in sight. As you examine it closely, you notice intricate patterns and symbols carved into its dark surface—glyphs that seem familiar but not quite readable, like a language you once knew in a previous life.

You turn the box over in your hands, feeling its weight. It's heavier than expected. Something inside shifts when you move it, sliding with a muted thud against the interior wall. You shake it gently, listening. One object? Two? The sound is muffled, absorbed by whatever lines the walls—velvet, perhaps, or something softer than any material you can name.

The patterns on the surface begin to catch light strangely when you tilt the box toward the lamp. Hidden geometries emerge—a fox hidden in the whorls of the carving, an eye, a coastline. You trace them with your fingertip, pressing slightly too hard, and feel the surface give under your touch. Not break, just… flex. Like skin.

You set the box down on the table and step back to think. When you look again, it has rotated slightly. You're certain of it. The glyph that faced you moments ago now faces the wall.

You leave the room. When you return, there are two boxes.

You never do find out what was inside.
