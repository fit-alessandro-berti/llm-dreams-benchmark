You feel a storm approaching and the air turns cool against your skin. The trees lean away from you, their leaves trembling all at once, though no wind has touched them yet. The path ahead begins to shimmer like water, and with each step the ground grows softer, as if the forest itself is breathing.

Lightning flashes silently, not illuminating the sky but revealing glimpses of other paths branching into trees, into clouds, into the memory of rooms you have never entered. Rain falls upward from the roots of the trees, silver drops rising toward the darkened heavens, and as they pass over you, you remember things in dreams you cannot name.

Then a door stands between the trunks, small and glowing at its edges. The storm whispers your name. You realize the dream has been waiting for this moment, and before you can hesitate, you reach for the handle.
