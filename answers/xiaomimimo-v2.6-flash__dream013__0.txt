# The Dream of the Endless Maze

...find that the puzzles are becoming **stranger and more personal**.

---

At first, they were straightforward — shifting tiles, locking mechanisms, word riddles that tested your wit. But as you descend deeper, the walls themselves begin to whisper.

**Puzzle one:** A clock with no hands. The gears grind, and each tick plays back a memory — your own, but slightly *wrong*. You must identify which memory is altered before the clock strikes thirteen. Your fingers tremble as you adjust the time, and the hallway ahead glows faintly open.

**Puzzle two:** A room of mirrors, but each reflection asks a different question:
- *What would you sacrifice for knowledge?*
- *When did you last do something purely for yourself?*
- *Can you solve what has no answer?*

You realize each mirror only opens when you answer *honestly* — and the harder the truth, the brighter the path.

**Puzzle three:** Two doors. One says *"Almost"*, the other says *"Enough."* You study them for what feels like hours. The walls are closing in slowly, and you hear your own voice — faint, echo-like — saying: *"Stop trying to be perfect. Move."*

You choose **"Almost"** and it swings open.

---

As you push deeper, the maze itself begins to **change**. The corridors fold like origami. Symbols you've never seen before form across the floor — half letters, half something living. You place your foot on one, and it pulses like a heartbeat.

Then you reach the center.

There is no final puzzle. Just a **simple question written in your own handwriting**:

> *"Why did you keep going?"*

You pause. The maze hums. Every corridor behind you flickers with the faint light of puzzles you conquered. You realize that solving each one wasn't about intelligence — it was about **patience, curiosity, and the willingness to be stuck without quitting**.

You answer:

> *"Because I was curious about what was on the other side."*

The walls dissolve into starlight. You are standing in an open field under a sky made of equations and constellations. You can see the **whole maze from above** — and it looks exactly like a human brain.

---

You wake up — breathless, smiling — with the strange, lingering feeling that you solved *something important*... even if you can't remember what.
