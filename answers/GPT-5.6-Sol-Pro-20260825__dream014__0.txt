As you walk, the noise of the city gradually softens. The strangers share stories, food, and laughter, and the loneliness that had weighed on you begins to lift. At last, they lead you to a quiet rooftop overlooking thousands of lights. You realize you never told them where you were trying to go.

One of them smiles and says, “You weren’t looking for a place. You were looking for people who would notice you were lost.”

When you turn to thank them, the rooftop is empty—but in your hand is a small map. Instead of streets, it contains the names of everyone who has ever helped you. At the center, a new path appears, glowing warmly beneath your feet, and for the first time, you know exactly which direction to take.
