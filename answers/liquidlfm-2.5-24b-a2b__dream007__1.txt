The room itself begins to fracture, the walls cracking open to reveal a void of swirling stars behind them.

In the dream, you realize that the argument wasn't actually about the topic you fought over. It was a manifestation of the fear that you are drifting apart. As the void pulls at the edges of your vision, you feel a profound sense of loss, but also a strange, quiet relief. You understand that you cannot force the other person to stay, only your own willingness to bridge the gap.

You wake up just as the image of your friend fades, leaving you with the lingering, bittersweet feeling that some things are worth fighting for, even if the cost is the peace we have now.
