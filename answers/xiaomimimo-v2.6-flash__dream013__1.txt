As you press deeper, the air grows heavier, thick with the hum of unseen machinery. You solve one puzzle and the path splits into three, each option glowing with a different color — amber, silver, and violet. You choose the violet path and suddenly the maze dissolves, revealing a vast underground chamber where a towering clockwork device sits at the center, its gears turning in reverse.

A voice, neither male nor female, speaks from everywhere and nowhere: *"The final puzzle is the only one you cannot solve alone. Others have been here before you. Others have failed."*

You turn and see figures — dreamlike silhouettes of people you've known, each standing before their own version of the maze, locked in their own struggles. You realize the puzzles aren't obstacles meant to trap you. They're lessons. Each one mirrors a challenge you've faced in waking life — a fear, a doubt, a moment where you gave up.

With that understanding, the walls of the maze melt away like wax, and the puzzles rearrange themselves — not into harder challenges, but into a single, elegant solution. The clockwork device at the center begins to turn forward, and the chamber floods with warm golden light.

You step forward, and the light becomes so bright that you close your eyes—

And you wake up, heart pounding, with the faint echo of that voice still whispering: *"You were never lost. You were learning."*
