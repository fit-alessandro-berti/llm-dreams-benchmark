**…the sky splits open like a cracked mirror.**

The peaceful forest you were walking through suddenly contorts. The trees twist and stretch impossibly tall, their branches clawing at bruised clouds that pulse with violet and amber light. The ground beneath your feet softens, turning from earth to something like water — you can see roots swimming below like translucent veins.

A wind rises, but it doesn't blow *past* you. It circles *around* you, holding you in place as if the storm recognizes you. Leaves whirl upward in a perfect spiral, each one carrying a faint whisper — fragments of conversations you've never had, names you can't quite place.

Then the rain begins — but it falls *upward*. Each droplet rises from the forest floor and ascends into the sky, carrying tiny reflections of a life you might be living in another time. In one droplet, you see yourself laughing at a table. In another, you're standing at the edge of an ocean.

**Suddenly, you realize you are the storm.**

The electricity crackling through your fingertips, the pressure building in your chest, the roar you can feel but cannot hear — it's all you. You look down and see your hands glowing with soft blue light, and where you step, the forest regenerates with flowers you've never seen before.

A voice — your own voice, but deeper, older — speaks from everywhere:

*"You walked in as a dreamer. You will wake as the weather."*

The forest bends around you in reverence, and you feel something vast and beautiful settling into you — as if every storm that ever passed over a world had chosen to stay.

**And then, gently, the sky becomes blue again — and it's your sky.**
