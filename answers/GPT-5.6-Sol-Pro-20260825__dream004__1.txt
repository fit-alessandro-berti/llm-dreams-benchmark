You are in a world where colors and shapes are constantly changing. As you explore this surreal landscape, you come across a blank canvas and **a paintbrush floating beside it, though there are no paints anywhere nearby**.

The moment you take hold of the brush, its bristles begin to glow. You touch it to the canvas, and a line of deep blue appears—not only on the cloth, but across the sky. You paint a yellow circle, and a warm sun rises above you. With every stroke, the shifting world responds.

Excited, you begin painting quickly. You create trees made of spirals, rivers that flow upward, and birds shaped like silver triangles. But the faster you paint, the more unstable everything becomes. Mountains fold into the sea. The sun splits into hundreds of flickering eyes. The ground beneath you turns transparent, revealing an endless darkness filled with drifting doors.

Then the canvas paints something by itself.

A small house appears in its center. It is plain and familiar, untouched by the changing colors around it. Light shines through one window. Somehow, you know that someone inside is waiting for you.

You try to paint a road leading to the house, but each road twists away. At last, you lower the brush and stop trying to control the picture.

A white path quietly forms beneath your feet.

You follow it as the surreal world softens around you. The spinning colors slow. The impossible shapes settle into flowers, clouds, and stars. When you reach the painted house, you open its door and find the blank canvas hanging inside.

On it is a picture of you standing before the door.

As you watch, the painted version of you turns around, smiles, and writes a single sentence across the canvas:

**“The world changes when you do, but you do not have to decide what comes next.”**

The house dissolves into light. The brush slips from your hand, and you begin falling gently through layers of color—blue into gold, gold into violet, violet into complete darkness.

Just before you wake, you notice a tiny streak of blue paint on your fingertip.
