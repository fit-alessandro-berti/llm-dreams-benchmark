…and you open your laptop, but the screen is a warm, glowing mirror. Your reflection has already finished the project and slides a report through the glass toward you. Before you can take it, the office clock begins ticking backward, and every tick peels a page off the calendar. Your boss smiles without blinking and says, “Perfect. Now do it again before yesterday.” The walls stretch into rows of filing cabinets, each drawer humming with your name. You reach for the nearest handle, and it opens into a conference room where everyone is waiting for a presentation you remember giving years from now. You take a breath, step forward, and your voice comes out as the sound of typing—steady, endless, already halfway done.
