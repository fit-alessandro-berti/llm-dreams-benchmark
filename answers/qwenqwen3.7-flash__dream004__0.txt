...and the emptiness begins to hum, a low thrumming vibration that tastes like copper and smells like ozone. Your reflection in the pristine surface doesn't show your face; instead, it displays a constellation of eyes blinking in unison, watching you from within the white void.

Instinctively, you reach out to steady yourself, but your fingers pass through the surface like cool mist. Where your touch lingers, the mist hardens into liquid light. Without conscious thought, you drag your palms across the expanse, leaving trails of impossible hues—colors that shift between emerald and ultraviolet depending on the frantic rhythm of your heartbeat.

With every stroke, the chaotic landscape responds. The jagged spikes of the horizon smooth into rolling hills of velvet moss; the sky, previously a whirlwind of geometric shards, stops spinning and coalesces into a single, giant eye that watches you with benevolent curiosity. The canvas drinks your motion, translating your inner turbulence into structure. You aren't just painting; you are dreaming the world into coherence.

Suddenly, the strokes merge, forming words, then shapes, then a melody that plays directly inside your skull, tasting of honey and static. A path emerges from the bottom of the canvas, winding up over the frame and stretching out into the newly formed world. At the end of the path stands a figure wearing your clothes, holding a brush dipped in the raw material of stars.

The figure looks back, smiles, and beckons you forward. The canvas whispers, its voice echoing like wind chimes in a cathedral: *"Which layer of yourself shall we build first?"*
