Then, in the center of the clearing, something stands out: a single stone lantern, glowing with a soft, warm blue light, unnaturally calm amid the chaos. Its glow pulses in time with the thunder.

You feel a pull toward it, drawn by a sense of wonder rather than fear. The storm is not meant to scare you anymore; it is a prelude to something that happens only when the sky splits open and the light finds ground.
