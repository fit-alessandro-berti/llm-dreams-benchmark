You reach for your keyboard, but the keys have turned into soft gray pebbles. Every time you press one, the monitor fills with the words “soon, soon, soon,” though your hands are barely moving. Your boss leans closer, nodding, as if this is exactly the progress report he expected. The clock on the wall begins to melt upward, and the deadline date rearranges itself into yesterday, then tomorrow, then never. You try to explain, but your voice comes out as elevator music. Then the office door opens not to the hallway but to your own bedroom, and you walk through it, still holding a coffee mug full of moonlight, and the project is suddenly finished on your nightstand, signed with your name in gold ink.
