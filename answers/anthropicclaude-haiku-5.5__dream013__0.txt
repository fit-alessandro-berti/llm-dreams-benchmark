The walls of the maze hum softly, like the sound of a distant choir, and the stone beneath your feet shifts with each step, rearranging itself into new patterns. You are not sure how you got here, only that the way out must lie somewhere ahead.

As you move deeper into the maze, you find yourself standing before a great iron door. Its surface is covered in rows of glowing symbols, and in the center, a brass lock shaped like a spiral waits patiently. Beside it, a plaque reads: *"Arrange the seven lanterns so that no two of the same color touch, and the path will open."*

Seven lanterns hang from the ceiling above, flickering red, blue, and gold. You reach up and touch one, and it warms your palm like a living ember. Somehow you know that if you get the pattern wrong, the floor will tilt and send you back to the entrance, where the walls will have rebuilt themselves into a new labyrinth.

You study the lanterns. Each color seems to hum at its own pitch, and when you concentrate, you can almost hear the solution: a rhythm, a sequence, a quiet logic threading through the chaos. You lift your hands and begin to move the lanterns, one by one, and with each correct placement, a soft chime rings out through the halls.

Then, as the seventh lantern clicks into place, the iron door swings open without a sound. Beyond it lies not another corridor, but a wide, open sky filled with floating staircases, each one leading somewhere you have never been. You take a breath, feeling the maze fall silent behind you, and step forward into the light.
