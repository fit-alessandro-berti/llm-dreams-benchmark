The room around you falls away. You’re standing beneath a vast night sky, the box warm in your hands. A single star slips from its place and falls toward you, no larger than a firefly. It settles into the keyhole and turns.

The lid opens. Inside is a small, folded piece of darkness. You lift it gently, and it unfolds into a doorway. From the other side comes the sound of someone laughing—a voice you recognize, though you can’t remember from where.

You step through just as the first light of morning reaches the stars.
