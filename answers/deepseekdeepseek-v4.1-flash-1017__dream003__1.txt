You are given a mysterious locked box with no key in sight. As you examine it closely, you notice intricate patterns and tiny hinges no larger than eyelashes, shifting each time you blink. The patterns look like a map of a city you almost remember. You trace one line with your finger, and the box hums softly, warm as a sleeping animal. A seam opens—not where a lock should be, but along the center of your palm. Inside the box is no treasure, only a small black mirror reflecting a room you have never seen but somehow miss. In the reflection, a key hangs from a red thread. As you reach for it, the mirror fogs over and the box closes itself. The key is already in your pocket, warm and faintly beating. Behind you, a door you never noticed clicks open. You wake just before you turn to see who is standing there.
