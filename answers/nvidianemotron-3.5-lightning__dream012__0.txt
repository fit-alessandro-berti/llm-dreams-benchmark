The storm roared above, a living thing of black thunder and writhing wind, while the sea below heaved like a wounded beast. The ship creaked under the strain, timbers groaning, sails tearing at the seams. My crew huddled on deck, faces illuminated by intermittent lightning, their eyes reflecting both terror and an unspoken trust in me. I felt the weight of every heartbeat in the rigging, every desperate grip on the wet rails.

And I—
I stood at the helm, boots braced against the tilting deck, hands wrapped around the cold wood of the wheel. The wind howled, trying to snatch my voice, but I shouted over the chaos, "All hands to the pumps Keep her bow to the waves!" My words were ripped away, yet the crew moved, a well-oiled tide of muscle and will, driven by the instinct to survive and the knowledge that someone was holding the line.

A particularly massive wave crashed over the bow, drenching us in icy saltwater, and for a moment the world tilted so far I thought we'd spill into the abyss. Lightning seized the sky, and in that blinding flash, I saw the ocean not as an enemy, but as a relentless, ancient force that cared nothing for my plans—only for the shape I gave it. I stopped fighting the storm’s rhythm and instead let the ship dance with it, turning with the swells, using the wind’s fury to fill the tattered sails rather than resist them.

The tide turned slowly. The clouds, though still heavy, began to ripple. A sudden hush fell as a shaft of pale, watery light cut through the gloom, catching the spray and turning it to shimmering diamonds. The wind eased, the waves softened, and the storm lost its teeth. We were battered, the deck scarred, the masts listed, but we were moving. The horizon bled pink and gold—a break in the weather, a promise of calm.

The dream softened after that. The creaking wood faded into the gentle rocking of a quiet sea. The crew’s shouts turned to tired laughter. I woke to the sound of rain against the window, my heart still thudding from the voyage, the ship's name lingering on my tongue like salt on my skin.
