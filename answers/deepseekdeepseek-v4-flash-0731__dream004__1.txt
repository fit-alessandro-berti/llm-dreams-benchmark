…you come across a blank canvas and a brush made of light.

You pick it up, and the moment the bristles touch the surface, colors spill out like water released from a dam. You paint a circle, and it becomes a sun, warm and breathing. You paint a line, and it grows into a path, winding into the distance. Every stroke becomes a living thing, and the landscape shifts to match your imagination.

You paint a door, and it opens. You paint a window, and you see yourself standing inside. The world is no longer changing around you—it is changing *with* you. Shapes melt into feelings, colors hum with voices, and the canvas grows larger with every mark you make.

Then you realize: the canvas was never blank. It was waiting.

And as you step into the painting, the dream remembers your name, and begins to paint you back.
