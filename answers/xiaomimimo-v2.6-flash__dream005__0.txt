...they all seem to turn and stare at you as you approach, yet no one ever speaks. The rooms keep stretching — a hallway that extends into another room that opens into yet another, each one filled with laughter that echoes just slightly *off*, as if it's coming from somewhere behind you rather than around you.

You try to explain that you're looking for your friend, but every time you open your mouth, you realize you can't remember their name — only the feeling that they're *somewhere nearby*, just out of sight.

A woman with your mother's face (but younger) hands you a warm cup of tea and tells you to sit down. The chair is impossibly soft, almost swallowing you. You look down and realize you're in a coat you've never owned — elegant, heavy, with deep pockets you keep reaching into.

In one pocket, you find a photograph of you and the person you're searching for, but the image keeps changing each time you glance at it. One moment you're at the beach, the next you're in a snowfield, then a forest at night. But their face is always just... *blurred*.

Then the music changes — a song you used to love starts playing from a direction that doesn't seem to match where any speaker would be. A gentle pull in your chest makes you stand, and now you *know* — you can feel them, around a corner, just past the next room.

You start walking faster. The rooms begin to blur together, the people dissolving into soft shapes and colors. The only thing sharp is that pull, that certainty.

You round the corner —

And you wake up just before you see them.
