The spiral becomes a staircase, then a flock of birds whose wings are maps of places you have never been. The canvas peels away from itself and drifts toward you like warm mist, wrapping around your shoulders. You step through, and suddenly the dream rearranges itself behind your eyes.

Colors sing in frequencies you feel in your chest. Triangles turn to rain. Shadows blossom into gardens of slow-moving light. You no longer know if you are the painter or the painted. But it doesn’t matter. You lift your hand, and at your fingertips a new sun begins to form—quiet, patient, waiting for you to decide what it should become.
