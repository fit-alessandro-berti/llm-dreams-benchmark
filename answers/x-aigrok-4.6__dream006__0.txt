...open your mouth, only to find that every word emerges as a flock of luminous paper cranes that unfold themselves in mid-air, spelling out the speech in shifting constellations above the audience. The crowd leans forward, not with judgment but with quiet wonder, as the cranes alight on their shoulders and whisper the next line directly into their ears. You feel the usual knot of stage fright dissolve into something lighter—an odd, floating certainty that the talk was never yours alone to give. The stage itself ripples like water; you take one more step and discover you are standing on the surface of a still lake under a sky that is also the inside of a vast library. Books drift past like slow fish. You keep speaking, and the dream, satisfied, simply continues.
