Everyone looks up. You do too. There’s no ceiling, only a gray sky and, impossibly far above, a row of empty theater seats.

You look down at your notes. In your own handwriting, they say: *Ask them what they came to hear.*

“What did you come to hear?” you ask. This time the microphone works.

For a moment nobody moves. Then a woman in the third row says, “My brother laughing.”

Someone near the back says, “The screen door at our old house.”

“Rain,” says a little boy in the front row. He has his eyes closed.

You set the microphone back in its stand. The rain gets louder. A few people take off their glasses. Nobody gets up to leave.
