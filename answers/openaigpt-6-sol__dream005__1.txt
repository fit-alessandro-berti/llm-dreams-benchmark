each group seems to be talking about a different version of the same evening. In one, the party ended hours ago. In another, it hasn’t begun. A third insists everyone is waiting for you.

You move from room to room, following a voice you recognize. At last, you find your friend standing alone beside a window. Beyond the glass, dawn is breaking over a street you’ve never seen.

“I’ve been looking for you,” you say.

“I know,” they answer. “I was looking for you, too.”

For a moment, you both watch the light reach the rooftops. Then someone calls your name from the room behind you, and you turn.
