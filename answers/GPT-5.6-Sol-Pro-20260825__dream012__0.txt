You are the captain of a ship in the middle of a turbulent sea. Your crew looks to you for guidance as the storm worsens, and you grip the wheel, though the compass is spinning wildly. Through the rain, you see a faint golden light between the waves—not a lighthouse, but something moving beneath the water.

You order the sails lowered and steer toward it, trusting an instinct you cannot explain. The sea rises like a wall before the ship, and for one terrifying moment, everything becomes silent. Then the wave lifts the vessel high into the sky.

From the crest, you glimpse an impossible sight: a calm circular harbor hidden in the center of the storm, with an island covered in glowing trees. You guide the ship toward it, and as soon as you cross into the still water, the storm freezes behind you like a painted backdrop.

The crew cheers, but when you turn around, the deck is empty. In their place are dozens of sealed letters, each bearing your name in a different handwriting. You open the nearest one and read:

**“You did not lead us here. We led you.”**

Then the island’s trees begin to whisper, and every voice sounds like your own.
