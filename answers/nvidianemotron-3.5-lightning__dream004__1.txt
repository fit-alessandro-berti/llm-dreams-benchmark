...a blank canvas floating weightlessly in the air, its surface pulsing with the afterimages of every shape the world had ever worn. You don't question why it's there—logic has long since dissolved into hue and form here. As you reach out, the canvas doesn't resist; it absorbs your touch, and instantly the chaotic dance of colors and shapes outside slows, converging into a single, living picture that unfolds from the canvas like a window opening inward. The moment the final stroke is made—a spiral, a face, or just a burst of gold—the entire landscape settles into a quiet, permanent stillness, as if the world itself had been waiting for you to finish this one image. You step back, and the canvas becomes a mirror, reflecting not the shifting world, but the part of you that had been searching for a place to belong.
