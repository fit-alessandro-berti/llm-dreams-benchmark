**...and you open your mouth to give orders — but the words that come out aren't your own. They're old, deep, and they seem to rise from somewhere beneath the ocean itself.**

The crew freezes. The wind howls, pulling at your coat, rain stinging your face like needles of ice. The mainmast creaks and groans as a wall of black water rises before the bow, taller than anything you've ever seen.

You brace yourself on the rail, knuckles white, and shout a single word — *"Hold!"* — and somehow, the crew listens. Hands grip lines and ropes. The helmsman locks the wheel tight.

**The wave crashes over you.**

Water swallows the deck, cold and absolute. You're dragged beneath the surface, tumbling through chaos — timber, rope, saltwater, darkness. Your lungs burn. And then, just as you think the sea has claimed you —

**Your feet find solid ground.**

You're standing in shallow water, and the storm is silent. The ship drifts calmly behind you as if nothing happened. The moon breaks through the clouds, painting everything silver. And there, sitting at the water's edge, is a **figure you recognize** — someone you lost long ago, smiling gently, as if they've been waiting.

They hold out a hand.

*"You've been fighting the sea,"* they say softly. *"You just have to learn to dance with it."*

The dream shimmers — **and the sound of waves pulls you closer...** 🌊
