You feel a storm approaching and the light turns the color of old silver. The leaves rise off the ground like green birds, circling above your head, while the wind carries a name you almost recognize. The path ahead splits into three, though it was only one path moments ago, and the middle one is flooded with rain falling upward toward a sky full of roots. You step forward without fear, because dreams like this know where you belong: somewhere between the thunder and the trees, where the storm is not danger but a door waiting to open.
