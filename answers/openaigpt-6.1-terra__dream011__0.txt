The first one asks, *Why did you leave the door open?*

You don’t remember a door. You glance around, but the other students are already writing, their pencils scratching in perfect unison. The clock above the chalkboard has no hands, only an eye that slowly turns toward you.

On the second page is a photograph of your childhood bedroom. The door is open. Something stands in the hallway beyond it.

You try to raise your hand, but the proctor is already beside you.

“Take your time,” she whispers. “You’ve been here before.”

Then the bell rings, and everyone begins erasing.
