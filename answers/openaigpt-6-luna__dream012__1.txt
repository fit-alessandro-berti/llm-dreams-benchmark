The wind screams through the rigging. Waves climb higher than the mast, then collapse around the ship, but your crew moves at your command. Together, you lash yourselves to the deck and steer toward a pale light flickering beyond the storm.

As you reach it, the sea falls silent.

The light is a doorway standing upright on the water. You step through—and find yourself on a calm shore beneath a sky full of stars. Behind you, the ship sails on without a sound, its lanterns glowing like a handful of small, brave stars.

Then you wake, still hearing the waves.
