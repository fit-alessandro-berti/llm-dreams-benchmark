Of course — let's drift back into it...

---

You are in a world where colors and shapes are constantly changing. The sky melts from turquoise to tangerine with every breath you take. Mountains in the distance fold in on themselves like origami, then unfold into rolling oceans of violet grass. The ground beneath your feet hums softly, rippling when you step.

As you explore this surreal landscape, you come across a blank canvas and —

it’s just floating there. No easel, no frame. Just a perfect, glowing white rectangle hovering at eye-level in the middle of all the chaos. It’s the *only* thing here that isn’t changing.

Everything else swirls and shimmers, but the canvas stays still. Calm. Waiting.

You reach out to touch it, and your fingertips leave trails of light. Not paint — *memory*. Where your hand brushes, a deep midnight blue blooms, then a sunflower yellow, then a color you’ve never seen before but somehow recognize from childhood.

You realize there’s no brush. *You* are the brush.

So you step closer. You think of the ocean, and waves pour across the canvas. You think of music, and the waves begin to pulse to a rhythm. You think of someone you love, and their laugh appears as little golden spirals in the corner.

But then the canvas does something unexpected — it paints *you* back.

A warm stroke flows out from the center and touches your chest, and suddenly you feel lighter. Your edges start to soften. Your worries start to dissolve into pastel mist. The boundary between you and the canvas, between dreamer and dream, gets thinner and thinner.

You step *through* it.

On the other side, the world is quiet for a moment. White and endless and peaceful. Then, slowly, all the colors you just painted rain down around you like soft snow — only they don’t feel cold, they feel like being remembered.

You lie back and float in them, watching the shapes above you constantly change — now birds, now jellyfish, now lanterns drifting up into a starless sky — and you understand, in that hazy dream-logic way, that you didn’t find the canvas at all.

The canvas was dreaming *you*.
