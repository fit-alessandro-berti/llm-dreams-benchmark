They lead you to a small garden tucked between two towering buildings. Everyone you have ever missed is there, tending flowers or sharing tea. You realize no one is surprised to see you.

When you look back, the strangers are gone. In your hand, the map has changed: it shows only a little circle where you stand, and beside it, the words *You can begin here.*

The city is still vast when you step outside, but you know the way to the garden now.
