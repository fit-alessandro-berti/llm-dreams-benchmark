...look down at the folder in your hands. It's warm, as if someone had been holding it just moments before you. The label reads **PROJECT HALCYON: DUE TOMORROW, 9:00 AM**, though the date beneath it keeps shifting, sometimes last Tuesday, sometimes a year from now.

Your boss, Ms. Varga, stands by the glass door of the conference room. Her face is pleasant, but her eyes don't blink, and the wall clock behind her runs backward.

"No questions," she says. "You'll find everything you need inside."

You open the folder. Inside are no spreadsheets or briefs, only a single sheet of paper with a hand-drawn map of the office building. Every hallway is marked with a small red X, and beside each X is a name, some of them yours, some of them people you haven't seen since grade school.

"Ms. Varga, what exactly is the project?"

She smiles. "Finding out is the project."

You step into the hallway. The carpet has turned to moss, and the fluorescent lights hum a tune you almost recognize. At the first X, you find a door with your own name on it. Inside, your desk is there, but it's surrounded by hundreds of ringing telephones, all of them off the hook. Each one plays a different voice, reading out numbers. You realize they're coordinates.

You start writing them down. Your pen runs out of ink, so you use a lipstick you didn't know you owned. The numbers spell something when you line them up on the wall: a city you've never visited, glowing on the horizon outside the window. The window, which was a plain office window a minute ago, now shows a harbor at dusk, with boats lit like paper lanterns.

Behind you, the door clicks. Ms. Varga leans in.

"Good," she says. "You're faster than the last one. Now you have to decide whether to finish the map or to walk out the front door, which, I should mention, no longer leads to the parking lot."

You look at the map, then at the harbor, then at the red X marked with your own name.

Somewhere in the building, a bell rings nine times, and you realize you're going to have to choose before the tenth.
