You grip the wheel as a wave rises higher than the mast. For an instant, lightning turns the whole sea white, and you see something beneath it: a road of pale stones winding through the depths.

“Hold her steady!” you shout. The crew pulls at the ropes, but the ship has already begun to follow the road. The bow dips. Instead of flooding the deck, the water parts around you, forming dark walls on either side. Fish flicker among the stones like lanterns.

At the end of the road stands a lighthouse, its lamp burning green. You know, with the certainty dreams give you, that someone inside has been waiting for your ship. The storm rages overhead, but down here the only sound is the slow knock of your heart—and three answering knocks from the lighthouse door.
