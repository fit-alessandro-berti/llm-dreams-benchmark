Together, you walk through alleyways that shimmer with color, past windows showing scenes from your own memories, and across bridges made of whispered reassurances. With every step, the loneliness loosens its grip. You realize that being found does not always mean arriving somewhere new; sometimes it means recognizing that you were never truly unseen.

At last, the group stops before a quiet door glowing with soft golden light. They gesture for you to open it. When you do, you see not a street, not a room, but a vast, peaceful dawn stretching endlessly ahead.

You turn to thank them, but they are fading into the morning light, smiling one final time. And as you step through the doorway, you understand: help often comes in unexpected forms, and even in the most crowded places, hope can find you when you let it.
