…find out what year it is.

A bell tolls somewhere beyond the fields. You follow its sound along a muddy road, past cottages with thatched roofs and people who pause to stare at your unfamiliar clothes. At the village well, an old woman tells you the king has been dead for three hundred years.

You ask her what year it is.

She studies your face. “The year you arrived,” she says, as if that explains everything.

Then the bell tolls again—and every person in the village turns to look at you.

You wake before anyone can ask why.
