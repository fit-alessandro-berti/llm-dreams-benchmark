As you explore this surreal landscape, you come across a blank canvas and a single brush hovering just above it, dripping with liquid starlight. You reach out, your fingers passing through the bristles without touching them, yet the canvas responds. Where your shadow falls, a garden of geometric flowers blooms, shifting from triangles to circles as you breathe. The sky above ripples like water, reflecting shapes that haven't been invented yet. You realize you aren't just observing this world; you are the pigment mixing within it. With a gentle wave of your hand, you guide the brush, painting a door that leads back to the warmth of your own bed, knowing that when you wake, the colors will still linger in the corner of your eye.
