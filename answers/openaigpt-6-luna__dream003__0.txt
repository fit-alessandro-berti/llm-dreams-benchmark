A seam appears where there wasn’t one before. The lid lifts—not with a click, but with a sigh—and inside you find a small, glowing moon, no larger than a pearl. It floats above the velvet lining, casting silver light across the room.

Then the box begins to fill with the sound of rain. You look up, and the ceiling has vanished. Stars shimmer overhead, and the moon in the box rises toward them, growing brighter with every inch.

You reach out, and it settles in your palm—warm as a heartbeat. Somewhere far away, a door opens. You know, without knowing how, that you’ve been expected.
