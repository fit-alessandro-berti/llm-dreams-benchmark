You step outside, but the street has become a narrow bridge suspended over a dark sea. Your friend walks beside you, still upset, and every word either of you speaks falls into the water as a small glowing stone.

Soon the sea is full of light.

Your friend stops and points beneath the bridge. There, moving slowly through the depths, is an enormous whale made of all the things you’ve never said to each other. It rises toward you, opens its mouth, and asks, in your mother’s voice, “Would you two like some tea?”

You both start laughing. The bridge softens beneath your feet, and suddenly you’re sitting at your childhood kitchen table. Your friend reaches across it and says, “I don’t remember what we were fighting about.”

You don’t either. But somehow, you both know it still matters.
