You quicken your pace, searching for shelter, but the path beneath your feet has changed. It's no longer the gentle dirt trail you were following; now it's lined with stones that glow faintly with an inner light, pulsing like heartbeats. The thunder crashes overhead, but instead of fading away, each roll of thunder seems to linger, layering upon itself until it becomes a strange, rhythmic music.

The rain begins to fall—but instead of water, you catch shimmering droplets that feel warm against your skin. As they land on the leaves around you, small flowers bloom instantly, spreading a sweet fragrance through the stormy air.

In the distance, you see a small cabin with a warm light glowing in its window. Somehow you know, with the strange certainty that only dreams can provide, that you've been here before. You begin to run toward it as the storm intensifies behind you, lightning splitting the sky into fragments of memory.

As you reach the cabin door and push it open, you wake up—the morning light streaming through your window, the echo of thunder still softly rumbling in your mind.
