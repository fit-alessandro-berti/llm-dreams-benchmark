...written in vanishing ink that only appears when you panic. The first question asks you to derive the exact moment your life went off-track using only the smell of the classroom and the sound of a clock that isn’t ticking. Every time you write an answer the letters rearrange themselves into a different, worse question. The professor is your childhood self, silently judging you from the front of the room while holding a diploma that keeps changing majors. You realize the exam never ends; it just folds into the next dream where you’re already late for the interview that this exam was supposed to get you.
