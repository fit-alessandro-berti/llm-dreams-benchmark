You are at a large social gathering where you only know one person. As you try to find them, you encounter various groups of people and

none of them are where you expect them to be.

The room is too big for the house it's in. It's a ballroom, but the walls are made of kitchen cabinets. The chandelier is slowly dripping water onto a buffet table that is just a long folding table covered in damp white tablecloths. Music is playing from somewhere, but it's muffled, like it's coming through a wall.

You know you're looking for Maya. You only know Maya. You came here because of her. You were told she would be here.

You start walking through the crowd. Every group you approach parts like water, then closes again behind you.

A group of people are sitting in a perfect circle on the floor, all facing inward, staring at a single unlit candle. They don't notice you. One of them looks up and it's your third grade teacher, but he's much younger.

Another group is standing shoulder-to-shoulder by a doorway that leads nowhere, laughing at something on a phone. You recognize your boss, your dentist, and a man who looks exactly like you but with a beard you don't have. You ask if they've seen Maya. They keep laughing.

The music changes. Now it's the song from your childhood kitchen. The ceiling gets lower. You have to duck.

You find a hallway that wasn't there before. It's lined with closed doors, each with a nameplate. You run your fingers down them: Maya, Maya, Maya... the name repeats for a dozen doors. You try the first one. It's locked. The second one opens to a bathroom stall. The third one opens to the same ballroom you just left, but everyone is now wearing swimwear and it's snowing indoors.

Panic starts to rise in your chest, that familiar dream-tightness. You need to find her before you wake up.

You turn a corner and see her. She's by the window, talking to someone you can't see. She looks up, sees you, and waves you over. You start to run toward her and as you get closer, her face shifts into your own. She mouths your name. The window behind her is now a mirror.

You wake up before you can get there.
