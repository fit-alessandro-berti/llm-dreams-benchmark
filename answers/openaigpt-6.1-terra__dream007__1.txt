The moment you stop speaking, the room falls silent—so silent that you can hear the rain hanging in the air outside. Your friend opens their mouth, but a small paper bird flies out instead. It circles the room and lands in your hand.

Written on its wing are the words: *I thought you knew.*

You look up. Your friend is suddenly much younger, standing at the end of a long hallway. You start toward them, but the floor turns soft beneath your feet, and every step leaves a footprint full of seawater.

“Wait,” you call.

They turn. For a moment, neither of you remembers what the argument was about. Then your friend holds out their hand, and you take it. The hallway folds neatly away, like a letter, and you are both sitting on a beach at dawn.
