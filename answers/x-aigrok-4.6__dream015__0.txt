Despite the hardships, you decide to gather what little you have—a dented canteen, a rusted multi-tool, and a faded map scavenged from an old bunker—and set out at first light toward the distant ridgeline. Rumors speak of a hidden valley there where rain still falls clean and a handful of survivors have begun planting again. The journey is harsh: dust storms that taste of metal, skeletal remains of highways, and the constant sense that something watches from the ruins. Yet each step feels purposeful, as if the dream itself is urging you forward. When you crest the ridge at dusk, you see green. Actual green. And for the first time in this long nightmare, you allow yourself to believe it might be real.
