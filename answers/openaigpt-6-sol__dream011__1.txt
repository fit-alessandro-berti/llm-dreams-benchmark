You are about to take the final exam for a course that determines your future career. As you sit down to start the exam, you notice the questions are written in your handwriting.

**1. What did you want to be before anyone told you to be practical?**

You look up. Every other student is writing furiously, though their pencils make no sound. At the front of the room, the professor turns over an hourglass. Instead of sand, tiny folded maps pour through its neck.

**2. Name a place you have never been but miss.**

You try to skip ahead. The remaining pages are blank except for the last question:

**3. When the door opens, will you go?**

At once, a door appears in the wall beside you. Beyond it, you hear rain, laughter, and someone calling your name in a voice you almost recognize.

The professor says, “You may take as long as you need.”

But the hourglass is nearly empty.

You pick up your pencil and write, beneath the final question, *Yes.*

The door opens wider. You leave the exam unfinished, and on the other side find a desk with a small brass plaque bearing your name.

Underneath, it says: **Professor**.
