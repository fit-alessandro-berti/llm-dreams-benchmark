Each question is a moment from your life: the day you almost spoke up, the stranger you never helped, the career you wanted before you were told it wasn’t practical. The answer choices are “Yes,” “Not yet,” and “You already know.” The room slowly fills with everyone who ever believed in you—and everyone who didn’t—all waiting quietly for you to choose. Then you turn the page and find only one question left: “What would you do if this exam didn’t exist?” You reach for your pencil, but it has become a key. The clock begins to melt, and the invigilator whispers, “Whenever you’re ready—the dream is not the test; waking up is.”
