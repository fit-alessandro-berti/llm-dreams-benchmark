You are walking through a peaceful forest when suddenly the weather changes dramatically. You feel a storm approaching, and the trees begin to sway as the sky darkens to a deep, velvety blue.

You hurry toward a small wooden cabin you hadn’t noticed before. Just as you reach its door, the first raindrops fall—though they sparkle like tiny stars. Inside, a warm fire is burning, and a kettle sings softly on the stove. You settle by the window and watch the storm roll across the forest.

Then the rain lifts from the ground instead of falling from the sky. Silver droplets drift upward between the trees, carrying leaves and little lights with them. The forest grows bright again, and in the clearing beyond the cabin, a white stag waits beneath a rainbow.

It looks back at you, then steps into the trees. Without quite knowing why, you follow—feeling that wherever the path leads, you are exactly where you’re meant to be.
