...and you begin to **realize your note cards are blank.**

You blink down at them. The words were there a second ago — now they just shimmer and dissolve like ink in water. A nervous laugh bubbles up in your throat.

You look up. The crowd is still looking expectantly at you, but now you notice something strange — every single person is wearing your face. Hundreds of yous, smiling patiently back at you.

You open your mouth to speak, and instead of words, music comes out. Light spills from the microphone with every note. The stage lights soften from harsh white to warm gold. Your feet lift an inch, then two inches off the wooden stage, and nobody seems surprised at all.

Suddenly you're not nervous anymore. You don't need the cards. You *know* the speech — you've always known it. You talk about flying in a falling elevator, about showing up pants-less to high school, about all the weird dreams that brought you here. The crowd of yous laughs, and nods, and laughs harder.

Just as you get to the best part, the applause swells like an ocean wave — and lifts you right up off the stage, floating weightless above them all...
