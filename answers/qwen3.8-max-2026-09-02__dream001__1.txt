You start to walk quickly, your shoes echoing against streets that seem to rearrange themselves when you’re not looking directly at them. The city rises around you in impossible layers — stairways curling into the sky, bridges leading to doors instead of roads, windows glowing with colors you can’t quite name.

Every person you pass seems to know where they’re going. You ask one of them for help, but when they turn to face you, their expression is strangely familiar, as if they’ve been waiting for you all along.

“You’re not late,” they say calmly. “You’re just early for a different destination.”

Before you can ask what they mean, a clock tower chimes in the distance. The sound ripples through the air like water, and suddenly every street sign begins changing at once, rearranging letters until they all spell the same word: *Remember*.

You realize then that the meeting isn’t in a place — it’s in a memory you’ve been avoiding. One you’ve carried without knowing.

Your heartbeat slows. The city grows quieter. And somewhere deep inside, a door you didn’t know existed begins to open.
