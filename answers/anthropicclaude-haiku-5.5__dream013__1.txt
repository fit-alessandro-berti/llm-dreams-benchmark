As you move deeper into the maze, the walls begin to hum softly, as if they are breathing. The stone beneath your feet shifts from cold grey to a warm amber, and the air smells faintly of rain and old paper. You realize the puzzles are not simply set into the maze; they are growing out of it, like vines of logic curling from the cracks in the walls.

The first door you reach has no lock, only a ring of seven brass dials, each marked with symbols you almost recognize: a crescent moon, a spiral, a broken circle, a key, an eye, a wave, and a small star. Beneath them, words are etched into the metal: *Turn each dial so that no symbol touches its own reflection.* You stare at the dials, and as you concentrate, the symbols begin to glow faintly, rearranging themselves in your mind. You see the pattern before you can explain it, the way you sometimes know a melody before you hear the next note. You turn the dials, one by one, and with a soft click the door swings open.

Beyond it, the corridor stretches on, but now the walls are lined with mirrors. Your reflections move a half-second out of sync with you, and some of them are wearing different faces: your younger self, a stranger with kind eyes, a figure whose face you cannot quite see. One reflection raises a finger to its lips and points down the left-hand passage. You follow it, and the mirrors go dark behind you.

The second puzzle is a chessboard floating in the air, its pieces made of frosted glass. Its squares are not arranged in a grid but in a spiral, and every move you make causes a distant bell to toll somewhere far beneath you. You understand, somehow, that each bell is a memory being unlocked. You don't need to win the game; you need to play it so that the bells ring in the order of a lullaby you once heard as a child. You move a bishop, then a knight, and the board sighs. A faint melody drifts up from below, and you hum along, your fingers finding the right squares as though they remember the way.

The final chamber is open to a sky that is not quite night. In the center stands a stone pedestal holding a single sealed envelope with your name on it. Around it, the maze walls have fallen away, revealing that the labyrinth was never a prison but a path, and every puzzle you solved has been carving the way out. You pick up the envelope, feel its weight, and begin to open it, just as the sound of birdsong starts to rise, and the dream begins to fade into morning light.
