You watch the hands spin, slow, and reverse. Then you notice their shadows: no matter what the clocks show, every shadow points toward the same corner. You follow them and find a narrow passage hidden behind a clock.

It leads into a garden beneath a night sky. The maze is still there, but its walls have become low hedges, and you can see a path through them. You follow it until the first light of morning reaches the leaves.
