...and you realize you’re dreaming.

You smile, roll up your sleeves, and say, “No problem — I’ll just bend time.”

The clock on the wall starts ticking backward. The deadline stretches like taffy into next month. Your boss’s impatient frown melts into confusion, then wonder. Papers float up and arrange themselves into a neat pile, typing itself while you sip coffee that refills magically.

“Done,” you say, handing over a finished report that glows faintly with dream logic.

Your boss stares, speechless. Then they nod and say, “Great. Now do it again — backwards.”

And you do. Because in this dream, you can do anything — except wake up, apparently.
