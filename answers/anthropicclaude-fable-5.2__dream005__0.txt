...each one seems to be holding a party of its own.

The first group stands in a circle near the door, laughing at a joke you just missed. When you ask if they've seen your friend, they turn to you warmly and say, "Oh, you just missed them," and point toward a hallway that wasn't there a moment ago.

The hallway opens into a room that is bigger than the house could possibly contain. A group sits cross-legged on the floor, playing a card game with cards that are blank on both sides. They seem to know the rules perfectly. One of them deals you in without looking up. You hold your blank cards and somehow know you're winning, but you set them down and move on.

You pass a long table where people are eating a dinner that keeps changing: soup, then birthday cake, then a bowl of small, smooth stones. A woman who looks like your third-grade teacher but isn't pats the empty chair beside her. "They were just asking about you," she says. You realize you can't quite remember your friend's name, only the shape of their laugh and the color of their coat.

You climb a staircase that leads, impossibly, to the backyard. It's night now, though it was afternoon when you arrived. Strings of lights hang from trees, and beneath them people are slow dancing to music you can feel but not hear. You catch a glimpse of that familiar coat across the lawn and push through the crowd. People part gently, smiling, saying your name as if they've known you for years.

When you finally reach them, your friend turns around, and it's them, unmistakably, though their face is one you've never seen before.

"There you are," they say. "I've been looking everywhere for you."

And you realize, with the easy logic of dreams, that this was your party all along.
