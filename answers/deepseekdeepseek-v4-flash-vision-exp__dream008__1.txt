...and you realize the project is made of glass, and every word you type shatters into tiny pieces that reassemble into a new project. Your boss smiles, but their face is a clock, and the hands are spinning backwards. You try to ask for more time, but your voice comes out as a spreadsheet. The deadline is a door. You open it, and it leads to your childhood bedroom, where a note on the pillow says: “You already finished this. You just haven’t remembered yet.”

Then you wake up—but only for a second. The boss is still there, waiting. This time, their face is yours. And the project is already done.
