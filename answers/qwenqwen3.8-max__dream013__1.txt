You are in a maze filled with complex puzzles. Each puzzle requires intense concentration and logical thinking to solve, and as you move deeper into the maze, you begin to realize that every lock, symbol, and turning corridor reflects a question you have not yet answered about yourself.

The walls grow quieter. The puzzles become less like barriers and more like memories arranged in patterns. At last you reach a door with no keyhole, and when you place your hand against it, it opens—not because you solved the final puzzle, but because you finally stopped fearing what you might find on the other side.

Beyond it is a soft morning light, and the maze folds itself into a single thought: the way out was always through understanding.
