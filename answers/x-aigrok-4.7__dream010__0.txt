You step into the next challenge and the room changes around you: a quiet workshop with one unfinished project on the bench and a clock that only ticks when you hesitate.

The task is simple and impossible at the same time. Finish the work the way you would if no one were watching and the prize didn’t matter. Every tool you need is already there. Every wrong turn leaves a mark you can still repair.

You stop performing and start making. The noise of the competition fades. When you look up, the judges are gone, the clock has stopped, and the thing on the bench is unmistakably yours.

A door opens onto the job you wanted, not as a trophy, but as the next room of the same work. You walk through still holding the piece you made, and the dream doesn’t end so much as continue.
