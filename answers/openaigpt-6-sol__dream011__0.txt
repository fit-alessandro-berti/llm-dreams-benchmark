You notice the questions are written in your own handwriting.

The first asks, *What did you want to be before you knew you had to choose?* The second is blank. The third reads, *Who told you there was only one right answer?*

Around you, pencils scratch furiously. You turn to the clock, but its hands are pointing at each other. When you look back, every page of the exam has become a map of a different place you once hoped to go.

The proctor stops beside your desk. “You may begin,” they say.

You pick up your pencil and, in the blank space beneath the second question, draw a door. It opens.
