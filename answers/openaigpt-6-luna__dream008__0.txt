Your boss points to a stack of papers. “It’s due in ten minutes.”

You sit down and begin typing. The words spill from your fingers like little birds, flying out the window and gathering in the branches. One by one, the clocks stop.

When the last bird disappears, your boss checks the time and smiles. “Excellent. You’re early.”

Then the office elevator opens onto the ocean, and you step inside.
