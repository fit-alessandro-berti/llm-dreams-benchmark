

…despite the hardships, you decide to keep moving toward the faint green light glowing in the distance, where old trees have reclaimed the skyline and the air feels almost kind.

Your boots crunch over cracked asphalt as you pass the ruins of a shopping center, its glass shattered and vines crawling up the shelves. The wind carries the smell of rain and rust. You are thirsty, your pack is light, and your hands are raw, but something in your chest refuses to give up.

At the edge of the city, you find a river flowing under a sky full of unfamiliar stars. On the far bank, a small camp glows with the warmth of a fire. A woman sitting by the water raises her hand and nods as if she has been waiting for you.

You cross the river without thinking too much, and when you finally speak, your voice sounds calm in a way you forgot it could.

“I found the green place,” you say.

The woman smiles.

“Good,” she replies. “That means we can begin again.”
