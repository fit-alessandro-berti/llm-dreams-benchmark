The crowd doesn’t gasp. They lean forward, as if this is exactly what they came to hear.

You realize you are dreaming, and with that realization, the stage becomes water. You stand on its surface without sinking. The audience now sits in paper boats, drifting gently outward. One of them hands you a microphone made of glass, and when you finally speak, your voice sounds like everyone you have ever loved, saying the things you needed to hear.

The dream folds slowly, like a curtain closing from the edges inward, until only one thought remains:

“You are ready.”
