…look down at the folder in your hands. It’s empty except for a sticky note that says, **“Due yesterday.”**

You try to ask your boss what the project is, but every time you open your mouth, the office lights flicker and the room changes. First it’s a conference room. Then it’s your childhood classroom. Then you’re standing at a bus stop, still holding the folder.

Your boss is there too, calmly checking a watch with no hands.

At last, you open the folder again. This time it contains a single page: a drawing of a door. You touch the handle, and it turns beneath your fingers.

On the other side is your bedroom, morning light coming through the curtains. The folder is gone, but for a moment you can still hear your boss calling, “Just send me whatever you have.”
