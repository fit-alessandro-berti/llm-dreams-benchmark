You step closer and the blank canvas ripples like water. A single drop of liquid light falls from nowhere onto its surface and blooms into a spiral of colors that have no names—hues that taste like forgotten songs and sound like warm stone. Shapes peel themselves off the edges of the world and drift toward it: a triangle that breathes, a circle that remembers your childhood laugh, a line that keeps rewriting itself into doors. You reach out, and your hand becomes a brush made of wind. Whatever you paint doesn’t stay still; it steps off the canvas and walks beside you, changing the landscape with every step, until you realize the dream is painting you back.
