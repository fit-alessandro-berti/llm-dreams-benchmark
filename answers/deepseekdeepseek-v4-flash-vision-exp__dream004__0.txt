You are in a world where colors and shapes are constantly changing. As you explore this surreal landscape, you come across a blank canvas and a single drop of light falls from the sky onto it.

The canvas doesn’t stay blank for long.

The drop spreads into a silver pond, and from the pond grows a forest of glass trees, their leaves whispering in a language you almost understand. The sky turns violet, then peach, then a shade you’ve never seen before—so you name it *breath*. The trees begin to sway, and their reflections ripple upward, joining the sky, until the whole world is layered inside the canvas.

You realize you aren’t looking at the canvas anymore. You are *inside* it.

And the brush that painted this place? It’s in your hand.
