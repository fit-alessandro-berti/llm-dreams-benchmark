You are dreaming. You are in a world where colors and shapes are constantly changing. As you explore this surreal landscape, you come across a blank canvas and a paintbrush floating in the air beside it.

The brush dips itself into a puddle of blue and sweeps across the canvas. Where the paint lands, a small door appears. You turn the handle and step through.

On the other side is a quiet garden beneath a sky full of sleeping stars. The flowers are familiar somehow, though their petals keep changing shape whenever you look away. At the garden’s center stands a little version of you, holding out a golden key.

You take it, and the garden folds gently inward, like a letter being tucked into an envelope. When you open your eyes, the key is gone—but for a moment, you can still smell rain, paint, and flowers.
