You start to run, though the city gives you no direction to run toward.

The streets are wide and pale, like sheets of paper left out in the rain. Buildings lean in tiers, their windows glowing the soft yellow of rooms where no one lives. The sky is the color of a bruise, and the single star above it keeps sliding behind chimneys whenever you look away.

Your phone vibrates against your palm.

**MEETING IN 58:00**  
**LOCATION: —**

You tap the location. The dash fills with a spinning blue circle, then goes blank again.

So you run.

A woman under a silver umbrella says the meeting place is “three streets past where you were happy.” When you ask which street that is, she tilts her head as though the question is in a language
