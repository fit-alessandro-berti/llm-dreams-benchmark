You wander through a glittering ballroom that somehow also feels like a school cafeteria and a forest clearing at once. Clusters of strangers form and dissolve around you: one group is arguing in perfect unison about a memory that belongs to you, another is toasting with glasses that refill themselves with liquid light, a third is dancing in slow motion while their shadows race ahead independently. Every time you ask if anyone has seen your friend, they smile as if they know a secret and point in three different directions at once. The music shifts from waltz to static to the sound of your own heartbeat. Just as you think you glimpse the familiar face across the room, the floor tilts, the walls become water, and you realize the one person you came looking for has been walking beside you the entire time—only now they have your eyes, and they’re asking if you remember how the dream began.
