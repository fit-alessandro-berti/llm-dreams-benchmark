Behind you, the crew gives a ragged cheer. By dawn the storm has spent itself, the sea gleams like hammered silver, and you realize you were never steering the ship alone—every hand aboard was part of the same courage. Then the horizon folds like a blanket, and you wake with the rhythm of waves still rocking you gently.
