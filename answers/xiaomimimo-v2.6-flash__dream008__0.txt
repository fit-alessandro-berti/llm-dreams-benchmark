*You're at your workplace, and suddenly, you are given a project with an impossible deadline. Your boss is waiting for you to start, and you...*

...begin typing furiously, but your fingers have turned to cotton candy. Each keystroke melts into a sugary blur on the screen. The report you're writing rearranges itself into hieroglyphics every time you look away.

Your boss leans over your shoulder, but now he's seven feet tall with a coffee mug that never empties — it just keeps refilling, overflowing across your desk, dripping onto the floor which has become a flowing river.

You stand up to go get help, but your chair has grown roots and the office has turned into a dense jungle. Your coworkers are there, but they're all wearing your face, mumbling about quarterly earnings in perfect unison.

A clock on the wall shows 3:47 PM, then 3:46, then 4:000 — the numbers spiraling backward and forward. You realize you can't remember which project is "impossible," because you now have **forty-seven** projects, each one more urgent than the last.

Just as panic peaks, you notice your laptop screen has become a mirror. Staring back is your reflection — but your reflection **isn't dreaming**. It looks calm, satisfied, and smiles before winking.

Then you wake up, heart pounding, at 5:47 AM — your alarm about to go off.
