"Captain!" shouts Mara, your first mate, her voice nearly lost in the wind. "The compass is spinning! We've lost north!"

You look down. She's right. The needle whirls lazily, as if it were bored by the storm. Then it stops and points straight up, toward a break in the clouds where a single star burns, far too bright and far too close.

"Then we'll follow that," you say, and you're surprised by how calm you sound.

The crew hesitates. Then old Tobin, who hasn't spoken since you left port, begins to hum. One by one the others join in, and the song steadies their hands on the ropes. The waves seem to listen. They still heave and crash, but now they rise and fall in rhythm with the melody, carrying the ship instead of fighting it.

The star drops lower, and you realize it isn't a star at all. It's a lighthouse, impossibly tall, its beam sweeping the water in slow golden arcs. At its base the sea goes still as glass. You steer into that calm, and the storm closes behind you like a curtain.

The crew cheers, but you've gone quiet. The lighthouse door stands open, and in the doorway is a figure holding a lantern. She wears your coat and has your face, and she's smiling as if she's been waiting a very long time.

"You made it," she says. "Come inside. There's something you forgot to bring with you."

You step off the ship, and the deck seems to dissolve behind you into light, and...
