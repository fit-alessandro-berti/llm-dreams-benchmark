You concentrate so intensely that the silence of the maze roars in your ears. You reach into your own pockets and pull out a heavy, ticking pocket watch—though you don't remember owning one—and a single, glowing feather. As you place them on the scales, you realize you aren't solving the maze anymore; you are translating it. The door dissolves into a swarm of silver moths, fluttering away to reveal the next corridor.

With every step inward, the walls become more translucent. Looking through them, you see other versions of yourself solving other puzzles in parallel corridors. When you pause, they pause. Every logical deduction you make seems to unlock a piece of your own subconscious. You solve a puzzle made of tangled music boxes, and suddenly remember the smell of your childhood home.
