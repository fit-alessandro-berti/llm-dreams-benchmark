Let's drift back into it:

---

You are at a large social gathering where you only know one person. As you try to find them, you encounter various groups of people and **every group pulls you in for just a moment.**

First there's a circle of old friends laughing at a joke you arrived just too late to hear. They smile at you like they *almost* know you, hand you a drink you didn't ask for that is warm in your hands, and when you ask, "Have you seen...?" they point all at once in different directions.

You wander on. The room is bigger inside than it should be. The music moves from one room to the next before you do.

Next there's a group huddled around a phone, all talking over each other in a language you understand in the dream but couldn't name awake. One of them looks up and says your name perfectly — even though you've never told them — and you feel a rush of relief until you realize they're talking to someone just behind you.

Then a quiet kitchen group, people sitting on counters, having the kind of deep 2 a.m. conversation you want to join. You linger. Time stretches. You almost forget who you were looking for.

And that's when you see them — the one person you know — across the room, through a doorway that wasn't there a second ago. They're also looking for someone. They're also a little lost. When your eyes meet, they wave like *of course* you found each other here, and the whole noisy, shifting party suddenly gets quieter, smaller, warmer.

You wake up just as you're about to reach them, with that half-happy, half-aching feeling of almost-arriving.
