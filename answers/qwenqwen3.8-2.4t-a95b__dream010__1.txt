

…obstacles that seem to shift like sand beneath your feet.

At first, the challenges feel ordinary: solving puzzles, answering impossible questions, and performing tasks in front of a panel of judges whose faces keep changing. But soon, the competition becomes stranger. One room fills with clocks that all run backward. Another contains a door that only opens when you speak a truth you have been avoiding. In a third, you must build something meaningful out of fragments of old memories—photographs, torn pages, half-finished songs.

With every challenge, you begin to realize the competition is not only testing your skills. It is testing your courage, your patience, and your willingness to remain kind when you are afraid of losing.

The final task takes place in a quiet, empty office that looks exactly like the workplace of your dream job. There is no audience now. No judges. Only a desk, a chair, and a single envelope with your name on it.

You open it and find a note that reads:

“The prize was never something given to you. It was the person you became while trying to earn it.”

When you look up, the room is filled with warm light. You feel a deep sense of calm, as if you have finally arrived somewhere you were always meant to be.

Then you wake up—but the feeling stays with you, like proof that the dream was not just a dream.
