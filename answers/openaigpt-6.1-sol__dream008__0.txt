reach for your keyboard, only to discover that every key is a tiny clock. They all show different times, and the moment you touch one, it begins to ring.

Your boss stands in the doorway holding an hourglass the size of a person.

“Yesterday,” they say. “I need it finished yesterday.”

You open the project folder. Inside is a single instruction:

**BUILD A DOOR WHERE THERE IS NO WALL.**

Somehow, you know exactly what to do.

You gather the ringing clocks and arrange them in a rectangle beside your desk. Your coworkers continue typing, pretending not to notice. When you turn the last clock sideways, a brass handle appears in the air.

Beyond the door is a quiet garden. Rain hangs motionless above the flowers. On a bench sits another version of you, barefoot, eating an orange.

“Did you finish?” your boss asks.

You look back. The hourglass is empty, but they keep turning it over.

“Not yet,” you say, and step through.

The rain begins to fall.
