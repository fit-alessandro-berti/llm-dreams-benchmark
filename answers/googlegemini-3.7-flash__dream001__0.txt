...reach into your pocket for your phone, but instead pull out a heavy, ticking pocket watch whose hands are spinning counterclockwise. The glass face doesn't show numbers, only shifting words: *Soon. Almost. Never.*

Panic spikes in your chest, but when you try to run, your feet feel as though they are sinking into warm, thick sand. The city around you defies geometry. The skyscrapers are made of sea-green glass and dark, polished mahogany, twisting upward into a lavender sky like the roots of an inverted forest. Bridges arch impossibly high overhead, carrying silent trains with glowing paper lanterns in their windows.

You stop a passerby in a long gray coat to ask for directions. When they turn, their face is completely smooth, a featureless mirror reflecting your own wide eyes. 

"Excuse me," you manage to say, your voice echoing as if in an empty cathedral. "Where is the conference?"

The stranger points a gloved hand straight up. 

You look toward the sky, and suddenly gravity tilts. The cobblestone street beneath you becomes a vertical wall, but you don’t fall—you simply walk up it. With every step, the roar of the city fades into a soft, oceanic hum. Windows blur past you, filled with scenes from your childhood, libraries with floating books, and quiet rooms you’ve never seen before.

At the very top of the highest spire, an ordinary wooden door stands free of any frame, hovering among the clouds. A brass plaque reads: **THE MEETING HAS ALREADY BEGUN.**

You reach out, your heart hammering against your ribs, and turn the cool metal handle. The door swings open into blinding white light and the rhythmic, steady beep of a distant alarm, pulling you gently back toward the waking world.
