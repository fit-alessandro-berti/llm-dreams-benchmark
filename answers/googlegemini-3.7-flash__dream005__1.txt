...and each conversation you overhear seems to be about you, yet spoken in a language you only understand when you aren't paying attention. 

In one corner, a circle of strangers in velvet coats is laughing warmly, clinking glasses filled with liquid that shifts colors with their mood. As you pass, one of them turns to hand you a cup of pure sunlight, but when you reach for it, it folds into an origami bird and flutters up into the chandeliers. 

You press on through the crowd. The architecture of the house begins to stretch and breathe—the parquet floor turns into a winding cobblestone path beneath an indoor canopy of willow trees. Another group is gathered around a grand piano, but no one is touching the keys; the music is being played by the shadows cast on the wall behind it, a soft, melancholic waltz that feels like a half-remembered memory.

Every time you ask someone if they’ve seen your friend, they smile knowingly and point toward a different doorway. *"They're just ahead,"* someone whispers with the voice of a radio tuned between two stations. *"They've been waiting by the tide."*

You follow a set of spiral stairs that lead not to a second floor, but to a vast, quiet terrace overlooking a calm, midnight ocean that shouldn't be there. The noise of the party behind you drops to a gentle hum, like distant static.

Standing at the marble railing, looking out at the water where the stars are reflecting upside down, is the silhouette you’ve been searching for. 

You step forward and say their name. They turn around with a warm, familiar smile, holding out a second coat as if they knew the sea breeze would be cold. 

"You made it," they say softly. "Look. The dawn is starting backwards."

And together, you watch the sky turn from deep indigo to violet, the entire world waiting quietly for you to wake up.
