As your fingers trace the carvings, the box grows warm, and the patterns begin to glow like moonlight on water. A soft clicking sound comes from inside, as if something is trying to remember how to open. You lean closer and hear a whisper—not from the box, but from behind you—saying, “The key is what you have forgotten.”

In the dream, you understand at once. You close your eyes and think of a memory you have been holding onto too tightly: a voice, a face, a moment you never said goodbye to. When you let it drift away like mist, a small silver key forms in your palm, cool and weightless.

You place the key into a hidden seam on the box. It turns by itself.

The lid opens slowly, revealing not treasure, but a tiny sleeping world inside: miniature stars, a quiet ocean, and a distant shore where a lantern burns. The light rises from the box and surrounds you, and you realize the dream is no longer something happening to you—it is something you are becoming.

Then, with a gentle hum, the box closes again, and you wake carrying its light behind your eyes.
