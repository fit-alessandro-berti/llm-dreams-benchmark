You start to run, but the pavement beneath your feet feels strangely soft, swallowing the sound of your footsteps. You fumble in your pocket for your phone to check the address, but instead, you pull out a smooth, black stone that hums with a low, anxious vibration. 

Looking up, the cityscape begins to warp. The towering skyscrapers bend and twist like liquid glass, and the street signs are written in a glowing, shifting script you can almost, but not quite, read. You try to hail a taxi, but the vehicle that pulls to the curb is an old-fashioned carriage completely overgrown with glowing blue moss. The driver leans out, and you realize he has no face—just a smooth, blank oval where his features should be. 

"Where is the meeting?" you ask, your voice sounding distant and echoey. 

The faceless driver says nothing, only pointing a long, spindly finger toward a massive, pulsing dome of golden light on the horizon. Just then, a flock of neon birds bursts from a nearby alleyway, swooping around you in a dizzying spiral. As they scatter, the ground falls away entirely, and you find yourself floating gently upward, drifting helplessly toward the glowing dome as the dream begins to stretch and dissolve into a kaleidoscope of color... 

...until you suddenly wake up in your bed, tangled in your sheets, with your morning alarm blaring in your ears.
