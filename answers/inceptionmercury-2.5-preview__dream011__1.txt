

You are about to take the final exam for a course that determines your future career. As you sit down to start the exam, you notice the questions are written in a script that rearranges itself as you read. The words dissolve into images of places you’ve never visited, yet feel familiar. Your pen hovers, unwilling to touch the page. The proctor watches from behind a veil of static, their face shifting like a reflection in water. Time slows, the clock on the wall melting into a puddle of gold. You realize there is no right answer, only a choice to wake up. You breathe in, and the room fades into a quiet hum.
