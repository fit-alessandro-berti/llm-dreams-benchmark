You are participating in a competition where the grand prize is your dream job. As the competition progresses, you face various challenges and...

...they don't make sense the way challenges should.

**The Waiting Room** is first.

It's not a room at all. It's a giant library where all the other contestants are waiting, except every bookshelf is filled with identical versions of *your* resume. You pick one up and the words are swimming on the page. Your name keeps rearranging itself. A woman with a clipboard made of glass calls your number, but when you look down, your number is written on the palm of your hand in ink that won't dry.

She smiles and says, "We're so excited you made it. Follow the hallway."

The hallway stretches forever and gets narrower as you walk, until you're squeezing sideways. At the end is a single door labeled "INTERVIEW."

**Challenge One: The Interview**

You walk in and there are three interviewers sitting at a long table.

One is your sternest teacher from high school.
One is your best friend, trying not to laugh.
One is you, at 8 years old, swinging your legs because your feet don't touch the floor.

They don't ask you about your qualifications. The 8-year-old you asks, *"What do you actually want to do all day?"*

You open your mouth to give the perfect, rehearsed answer, but no sound comes out. Instead, every time you try to speak, a small object falls from your mouth onto the table - a paintbrush, a tiny planet, a keyboard key, a sprout. The interviewers just nod, as if this is exactly the answer they wanted. The little you collects them all in cupped hands and whispers, "Good, you remembered."

A bell rings. You passed.

**Challenge Two: The Task**

The room melts.

Now you're standing in a vast, white space with all the other contestants. Each of you is given the same materials: a pile of clouds, a handful of lightbulbs that are unlit, and a ticking clock with no hands.

The voice over the loudspeaker says, *"Build something that has never existed before. You have five minutes before you forget why you're here."*

Everyone else frantically starts building ladders and towers that reach nowhere, trying to be the tallest. You look at the clock with no hands and suddenly understand - it's not counting down, it's *waiting*.

Instead of building up, you kneel down. You shape the clouds into a small, imperfect room. You place the unlit bulbs inside it, and they glow anyway - warm, soft, without ever being plugged in. You sit inside it. It feels exactly like that feeling you've chased your whole life, where work doesn't feel like work at all.

The loudspeaker crackles again, but this time it’s just your own voice: *"Oh. There you are."*

The white space dissolves. The other contestants and their towers fade like fog.

**Challenge Three: The Final Choice**

You are standing alone on a stage. A velvet curtain is in front of you. Behind it is your dream job - you can feel it glowing, humming, calling your name. A golden envelope floats down into your hands. Inside is a contract that just says:

> *Do you want it? Sign here.*

But the pen they give you is incredibly heavy. As you lift it, you realize you don't have to sign to get it. You just have to *walk through*.

You push the curtain aside.

There is no office. No desk. No boss waiting to shake your hand.

There is just an open sky, and a path made of all those little objects you spoke in the interview - the paintbrush, the planet, the sprout - laid out in front of you like stepping stones. And you understand that the grand prize was never a *place* you would be given. It was permission you were waiting for someone else to give you.

You take a step forward onto the path, and as your foot touches the first stone, you feel yourself getting lighter.

That's when you start to wake up.

You can still feel the warmth of that little cloud-room on your skin, and you realize you're smiling in your sleep. The alarm hasn't gone off yet. For a second, you can still see the path glowing faintly behind your eyelids, waiting for you to continue building it - this time, awake.
