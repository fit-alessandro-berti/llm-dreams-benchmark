You are dreaming. You are about to give a speech to a large audience. As you step onto the stage, you see the crowd looking expectantly at you, and you begin to speak.

But no words come out. Instead, a small golden bird flies from your mouth and circles the room. The audience watches as it lands on the podium, where it begins to sing. One by one, the people in the crowd rise and join in—not with voices, but with the soft glow of lanterns appearing in their hands.

Soon the whole room is filled with warm light. You look down at your notes and find they’ve turned into a map. At its center is a little house beneath a wide, silver moon.

The applause fades into the sound of rain on a window. You step down from the stage, follow the map through a door that wasn’t there before, and wake just as you reach the house.
