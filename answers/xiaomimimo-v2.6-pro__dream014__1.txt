The leader of the group, a woman with silver hair and warm, deep-set eyes, turns to you and says, *"You're not lost. You just haven't found the right path yet."*

She opens a door that wasn't there a moment ago, and beyond it is a glowing map hovering in midair. It shows every street, every bridge, every tunnel in the city — but one route is lit up in gold, and it leads to a place at the very center marked simply: **"Home."**

But then the map begins to dissolve, and the strangers start to fade like mist in morning light. The woman smiles at you one last time and says, *"You already know the way. You've always known."*

You wake up — or think you do — and find yourself standing at a crossroads in the city again. But this time, the streets don't feel so overwhelming. There's a warm golden light faintly visible at the end of one road, and without hesitation, you step toward it.

The city hums around you, no longer empty or chaotic, but alive and full of possibility — as if every stranger you passed was just another part of a path leading you, at last, back to yourself.
