"We sail toward the light!" you command. The navigator protests — charts show only open water there — but something deep in your gut tells you the sea has hidden paths that no map has ever charted.

The storm grows louder, but somehow more focused, as if the ocean itself is funneling you toward that glow. Waves that once struck from every direction now seem to push the ship forward like a hand behind your back.

Then — silence.

Not quiet. *Silence.* The wind dies. The waves flatten to glass. The crew freezes, every eye wide, every breath held. Even the ship stops creaking.

Ahead, where the light is brightest, the water splits apart — gently, impossibly — revealing a staircase descending beneath the waves into a world that glows gold and silver. And standing at the top of those stairs, drenched but unafraid, is someone you swear you lost long ago.

They smile, and extend a hand.

"Welcome back, Captain," they say. "You've been gone a long time."

Behind you, the crew watches in awe. But as you take one step toward that light, you feel the dream itself beginning to thin — the edges of the world softening like smoke.

You know you're waking up.

But you reach anyway.
