The canvas hums softly when you touch it, as if it has been waiting for someone exactly like you. Its surface is not white but a shimmering pale silver, and as your fingers brush across it, ripples spread outward like water, carrying faint echoes of colors you have never seen before: a blue that tastes of cold peppermint, a gold that sounds like distant wind chimes.

Without quite deciding to, you lift a brush that appears in your hand. It is warm, its bristles made of something soft and luminous, like threads of starlight. You dip it into the air itself, and the air obliges, thickening into a rich, swirling paint the color of a memory you can't quite place.

The first stroke creates a door. It isn't drawn so much as revealed, its frame glowing faintly at the edges. You step back, and the door begins to open on its own.

Behind it, the landscape shifts. The floating islands above you slow their rotation, the spiraling rivers below pause mid-flow, and the sky, which had been flickering through a hundred shades a minute, settles into a deep, quiet violet. Somewhere in the distance, a figure turns toward you, and you realize they are holding a brush of their own, waiting for you to decide what to paint next.

You take a breath, raise your brush again, and feel the dream bend gently to your will.
