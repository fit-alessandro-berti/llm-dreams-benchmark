You are walking through a peaceful forest when suddenly the weather changes dramatically. You feel a storm approaching and the air grows heavy with the scent of rain and crushed leaves.

The trees above begin to sway, their branches twisting like slow, living arms. Birds scatter into the darkening sky, their wings flashing silver against the clouds. You hurry along the mossy path, but the forest seems to shift around you, the trail bending in ways it never did before.

Then the first drop falls.

It lands on your hand, warm as breath. Another falls, then another, but instead of soaking the ground, each drop blooms into a tiny blue flower where it touches the earth. Within moments, the forest floor glows with a soft, rippling light.

The storm breaks open—not with thunder, but with music.

The rain sings in low, echoing notes, and the wind carries voices that sound almost familiar. You realize you are not afraid. You walk deeper into the glowing forest until you reach a clearing where a single white tree stands beneath a circle of open sky.

Lightning flashes, but it does not strike the tree. It circles it, like a ribbon of pale fire.

Beneath the tree is a door.

It is small, wooden, and covered in carved symbols that seem to move when you do not look directly at them. A brass handle gleams as if waiting only for you.

The storm grows louder, but now it sounds like a question.

You kneel before the door and reach for the handle.

Just as your fingers touch it, the rain stops.

Everything becomes perfectly still.

And from behind the door, very softly, someone whispers your name.
