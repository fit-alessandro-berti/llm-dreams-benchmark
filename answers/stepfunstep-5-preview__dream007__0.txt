...and you decide to storm out of the room, but the door opens directly back into the argument. You try again, and again, and each time the conversation resumes exactly where you left off, yet the space around you has shifted—first a beach at low tide, then a spiraling staircase, then a vast library where your words echo off shelves of books written in your own handwriting. On the seventh attempt, you stop. You drop into a chair that wasn't there a moment before and say, *"I'm not leaving until we understand each other."* Your friend sits across from you, but now there is a mirror between you instead of a table. When they speak, you hear your own voice—not mocking you, but pleading, worn thin with the same fear you've been carrying. You realize you haven't been arguing with them at all. You reach across the glass, and instead of your reflection, your hand meets theirs, warm and solid. The mirror dissolves. You wake up with your throat tight, certain that you have to call them today.
