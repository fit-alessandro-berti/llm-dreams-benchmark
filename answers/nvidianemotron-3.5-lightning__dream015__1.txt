Despite the hardships, you decide to follow a faint, golden light flickering at the edge of the wasteland. You realize the light isn't leading to a shelter or a safe zone—it's leading you back to the part of yourself you left behind before the world ended, and the dream gently dissolves into the quiet knowing that hope is the only resource that grows when you share it.
