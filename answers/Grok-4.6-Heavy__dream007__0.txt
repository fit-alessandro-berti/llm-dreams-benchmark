You are in the middle of a heated argument with a close friend. The disagreement is about something important to both of you, and you decide to walk out.

You tell yourself you only need air. The hallway lengthens behind you. Your friend’s voice follows, still making the same point, but the words arrive unpacked and put back wrong. When you look back, the doorway is gone.

They are standing in the street now, holding what you were fighting about — except it isn’t a topic anymore. It’s a small living thing, trembling in both of your hands at once. You never decided who would keep it. With the simple certainty dreams allow, you understand that if either of you lets go, it dies.

So you don’t. You stand there in the wrong weather, arguing without words, holding it together.
