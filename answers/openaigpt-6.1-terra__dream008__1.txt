You sit down to work, but the keyboard grows longer every time you reach for a key. The clock above your desk spins backward. Your coworkers are carrying armfuls of water through the hallway, trying not to spill it.

At last, you type a single sentence: *I need more time.*

The office goes quiet. Your boss reads the screen and nods. “Why didn’t you say so?”

She opens a drawer and hands you a small silver hourglass. Inside, instead of sand, there’s a beach. You can hear waves breaking on the shore.

You turn it over, and the beach becomes the office. Everyone is wearing sunglasses now, and the deadline is a red ball bobbing gently out at sea.
