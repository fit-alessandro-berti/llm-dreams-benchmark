I reach into my coat pocket, expecting to find a pen, but instead, my fingers wrap around a smooth, glowing blue feather. In the logic of the dream, this makes total sense. I pull it out and begin to write in the air between us. Wherever the tip of the feather moves, it leaves behind trails of shimmering, golden smoke. 

I look up triumphantly to show my friend, only to realize we are no longer standing in my living room. The walls have dissolved into a vast, twilight desert, and the floor beneath our shoes has turned into shallow, mirror-still water reflecting a sky full of three massive moons. 

My friend opens their mouth to yell back at me, their face still flushed with anger, when a flock of tiny, iridescent hummingbirds flies straight out of their throat instead of words. The birds scatter into the purple sky, humming a chord so deep and resonant I can feel it vibrating in my teeth. 

Suddenly, I can't remember what we were fighting about. Was it money? A secret? A broken promise? The memory slips through my mind like sand. I try to read the golden smoke I just wrote in the air to remind myself, but the letters are shifting, rearranging themselves into a doorway floating a few inches above the water.

My friend's anger melts into confusion, and then awe. They look at me, their voice now sounding like it's echoing from miles away, even though they are right in front of me. 

"Wait," they whisper, pointing at the floating doorway of smoke. "Did you remember to lock that?"

Before I can answer, the water beneath us tilts vertically, turning into a waterfall, and we both tip forward, falling effortlessly straight up into the starry sky—right as my eyes flutter open and the distant, rhythmic *beep-beep-beep* of my alarm clock pulls me back into my bedroom.
