You understand, without being told, that the project can only be finished by remembering something you haven’t experienced yet.

So you open an empty file, and it fills with light. Every task you were afraid of becomes a door in the office wall. You walk through one and find the finished project already waiting there, glowing softly on a desk made of dawn. Your boss nods, not because you met the deadline, but because you realized the deadline was only a dream of time.

Then you wake up, still at your workplace, but with the strange, calm feeling that you have already begun.
