By the third round, the fluorescent-lit office building has stretched into an impossible labyrinth of spiral staircases and velvet curtains. Your fellow competitors start dropping away, not through elimination, but by simply wandering into side doors that lead to misty forests or disappearing when you blink. 

The challenges grow stranger, yet in the moment, they feel completely urgent. You are handed a sleek silver tablet and told to solve a crisis, only to find the screen displays a swirling map of constellations instead of spreadsheets. Somehow, you know exactly which stars need to be connected. Next, you have to deliver a pitch to a boardroom filled with people whose faces keep shifting—one moment it’s your third-grade teacher, the next it’s a towering figure made of static, nodding slowly in approval. 

Suddenly, the floor beneath your dress shoes turns into shallow, warm ocean water. The final test is announced over an intercom that sounds like distant thunder: *Find your desk before the tide comes in.*

You wade through floating cubicles and drifting paper airplanes, your legs feeling as heavy as lead, panic rising in your chest. Just as the water reaches your knees, you spot a massive, ornate mahogany door standing completely alone in the middle of the room, dry and untouched by the waves. A brass nameplate gleams on the front. Every time you try to read the job title engraved on it, the letters shimmer and rearrange themselves, yet you instinctively know it says exactly what you have wanted your whole life.

You reach for the handle, frantically digging through your pockets for a key, pulling out only smooth river stones and old movie tickets. Out of time, you press your palm flat against the wood instead. 

The door swings open effortlessly. 

Inside, there is no desk, no computer, and no boss. There is only a sunlit glass balcony overlooking a quiet city woven into the clouds, the air smelling faintly of rain and fresh coffee. A deep, weightless relief washes over you—you won. You take a single step forward into the warm light, feeling your feet lift completely off the ground, just as a distant alarm begins to chime.
