...feel a wave of panic wash over you. But just as you sit down at your desk and open your laptop, something strange happens — your keyboard begins to melt into the desk like warm wax. You blink, and suddenly your office is filled with a gentle fog.

You look up, and your boss's face begins to shift — every few seconds it changes into someone different. A childhood teacher. A traffic cop from a dream you vaguely remember. A giant golden retriever wearing a tie, who nods at you approvingly.

"You can do this," the dog-boss says in a voice that sounds like it's coming from underwater. "The deadline is… **Tuesday.**"

But today is already Tuesday. Or is it Wednesday? The calendar on the wall has no numbers on it — just tiny spinning globes where the dates should be.

You reach for the project files, but the folders open up like birdcages, and small origami cranes fly out of them, scattering across the office. One lands on your shoulder and whispers, *"Just start with page one."*

You follow its advice and look down at the desk. There is exactly one page. On it, written in handwriting that looks like your own but isn't, are just two words:

**"Begin anyway."**

Something inside you relaxes. The fog starts to thin. Your keyboard reforms. Your boss — now back to normal, though you notice his shoes are somehow made of water — hands you a coffee that tastes like lavender and thunderstorms.

"You started," he says, smiling. "That's all I needed to see."

The impossible deadline dissolves from the whiteboard, letters crumbling like sand. In its place, a single sentence glows softly:

**"There is always more time than you think."**

You exhale — and then your alarm goes off, and the sound of it is the same whispering origami crane from before:

*"Begin anyway."*
