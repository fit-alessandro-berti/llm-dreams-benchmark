Your shoes make no sound on the cobblestones, yet you can hear the faint echo of a clock tower tolling somewhere far away.

A woman with a face made entirely of clocks approaches you and asks if you need directions. You say yes, desperately. She points down an alley where the pavement is missing, revealing an endless sky beneath your feet. "Walk across it," she says, as if it's perfectly natural. You take a hesitant step and realize the sky beneath you is solid — cool and smooth like glass. Stars pass beneath your feet as you walk.

You glimpse what looks like a grand conference room floating in the clouds ahead, its glass walls reflecting your own face dozens of times over. You start running toward it, but no matter how fast you go, the distance never closes. Your phone buzzes — you forgot you had it. A text from an unknown number reads: *"The meeting is within you."*

You stop. The city around you begins to dissolve like sugar in water, peeling away to reveal that you're actually standing in a small, quiet garden. The conference room, the clocks, the sky-street — all gone. A single chair sits beneath a tree with golden leaves. As you sit down, you feel a calm certainty wash over you, like the dream is telling you the one thing you actually needed to hear: you were already exactly where you were supposed to be.

The leaves fall gently around you, and somewhere in the distance, the clock tower chimes one final time.
