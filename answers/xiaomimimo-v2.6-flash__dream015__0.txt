# The Dream

You find yourself in a post-apocalyptic world, with resources scarce and dangers everywhere. Despite the hardships, you decide to **keep going.**

---

Your boots crunch over cracked asphalt as you move through the ruins of what was once a bustling city. The sky hangs a sickly shade of amber, dusted with ash that drifts down like grey snow. Twisted metal and crumbling buildings stretch out in every direction, their shadows long and jagged in the fading light.

But there's a signal — faint, intermittent — coming from beyond the mountains to the east. Someone, somewhere, is broadcasting. A community? A rescue? You don't know. But it's a thread of hope in a world that has otherwise stopped believing in it.

Every day is a calculation. **Water** — rationed from the rain you've learned to collect from tarps stretched over hollowed-out walls. **Food** — gleaned from what little grows in irradiated soil, roots you've learned to identify by memory alone. **Safety** — borrowed from the dark hours, when the roving war parties retreat to their camps.

You carry a worn backpack containing:
- 🔦 A flashlight with dwindling batteries
- 📖 A journal filled with sketches and notes
- 🗡️ A machete, your most trusted companion

Along the way, you find something unexpected: **a child, alone, huddled behind an overturned bus**, eyes wide with fear but still holding a crumpled photograph — a smiling family from another life.

You have a choice to make:

- **Leave them** — save your supplies, increase your chances of survival.
- **Help them** — share your meager resources, slow yourself down, but perhaps rediscover something humanity nearly lost — **compassion**.

Your heart races. The wind howls through the skeletal remains of a building nearby, carrying with it a whisper that could be the past urging you to be careful... or could be your conscience urging you to **be human**.

You kneel down. You extend your hand. The child hesitates, then reaches out.

Together, you face the amber horizon — and in that moment, the dream feels less like an ending and more like **the beginning of something worth fighting for.**

---

*And somewhere, just before waking, you see it — the signal grows stronger. The mountains glow faintly on the horizon. And for the first time in a long time... the world feels possible again.* 🌅
