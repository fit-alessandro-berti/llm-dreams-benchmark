…speak, but your voice comes out as a flock of small, bright birds.

They flutter above the audience, scattering through the theater. Everyone watches them, smiling, as if this is exactly what they came to hear. One bird lands on the microphone and sings a note so clear that the curtains rise by themselves.

Behind them is not a stage set, but a moonlit ocean. The audience begins to float, still seated in their chairs, drifting out over the water. You follow, walking easily across its surface. The birds circle overhead, and at last you understand the speech: it is the sound of the tide returning.

You take a breath to say something more—but wake just as the first wave reaches your shoes.
