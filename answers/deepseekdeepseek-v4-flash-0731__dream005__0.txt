...and each group seems to be waiting for you to ask a question you don’t know.

The first group is standing in a loose circle, holding glasses of something that glows faintly. They stop talking when you approach. One of them smiles and says, “You’re early.” You don’t know what that means, but you nod and keep walking.

The second group is by the window, all wearing coats despite being indoors. They’re discussing a door that doesn’t exist anymore. Someone points at you: “There they are.” But they’re looking past you, at someone behind you. When you turn, no one is there.

You spot your friend across the room—or at least, you think it’s them. They wave, but their arm moves like a flag in slow motion. You push through the crowd, but the room keeps stretching. The music gets softer the closer you get, until it’s just a pulsing hum under the floor.

You reach the spot where they were. A stranger is standing there, holding your friend’s coat. They hand it to you and say, “They left this for you.” Inside the pocket is a note that says: *You were looking for me. I was always the dream.*

Then you wake up—still dreaming.
