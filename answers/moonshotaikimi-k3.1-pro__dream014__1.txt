They lead you to a tiny café tucked between two skyscrapers. Inside, a table is set for you, and on it lies a map with no roads—only the names of people you’ve loved and places you’ve always hoped to see. Whenever you touch a name, a golden path appears beneath your feet.

You turn to thank the strangers, but they have become birds made of warm light. They rise through the ceiling, leaving feathers that drift down and turn into keys.

Just before you wake, you understand that they were never leading you to a single destination. They were showing you that every act of kindness opens a door, and that being lost is sometimes only the beginning of finding a life you hadn’t imagined.

Outside, the city is still vast and noisy, but now every window glows like a small promise—and, for the first time, you know which way to walk.
