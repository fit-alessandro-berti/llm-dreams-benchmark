You trace one of the patterns with your fingertip, and the surface of the box feels warm, almost alive. The symbols brighten, rearranging themselves into shapes you almost recognize: stars, waves, eyes, doors. A soft ticking comes from inside, not like a clock, but like a heartbeat trying to remember how to beat.

Then you realize the box isn’t locked—it’s listening.

You lean closer and whisper something you don’t consciously know: a word that tastes like rain and old mirrors. The patterns stop moving. For a moment, everything in the dream goes silent.

The box opens.

Inside, there is no object, only a small pool of darkness, shimmering like water. When you look into it, you see yourself standing somewhere else—another dream, another corridor, another door you haven’t opened yet. The reflection looks up at you and smiles, as if it has been waiting for you to arrive.

A voice, gentle and distant, says, “What you were looking for was never locked away. It was only waiting for you to stop searching.”

When you reach into the box again, your hand passes through the darkness and touches something familiar: a key, made of warm glass.

You wake with the feeling that it is still resting in your palm.
