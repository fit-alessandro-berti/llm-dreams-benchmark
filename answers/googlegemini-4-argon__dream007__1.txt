Suddenly, you both realize that the argument wasn't about the past at all; it was about the direction you were supposed to swim. Grabbing the trailing end of the blue yarn, which is now anchored to the moon, you both kick off the floating coffee table and begin swimming upward into the violet sky, entirely on the same side again, just as the alarm clock begins to echo from somewhere deep below the floorboards.
