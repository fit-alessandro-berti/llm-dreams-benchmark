You begin to speak, and instead of a voice, a single white dove lifts from your lips. The audience doesn’t gasp—no one even blinks—but the walls of the auditorium begin to unfold like paper, revealing a night sky filled with lanterns. As your words become light, the crowd rises in unison, their faces shifting into strangers you once knew in childhood, and you realize you are no longer delivering a speech; you are remembering something too important to have ever said aloud.
