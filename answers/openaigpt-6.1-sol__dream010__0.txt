…each one seems designed to test a different version of you.

At first, the challenges are simple: solve a puzzle, give a speech, build something beautiful from a box of mismatched parts. But every time you succeed, the arena changes. The floor becomes a moving train. The audience turns into a forest of silver trees. The judges wear clocks instead of faces.

Finally, only two contestants remain: you and someone whose face you cannot quite see.

The last challenge is announced by a voice from everywhere.

“Show us why you deserve your dream job.”

Your opponent steps forward carrying an enormous portfolio. Awards spill from its pages like golden leaves. You look down at your own hands. They are empty.

Panic rises in your throat—until you notice a small door behind the judges’ table. Through its window, you can see someone struggling with the very work you’ve always wanted to do.

So you leave the stage.

The audience gasps. The clocks on the judges’ faces stop ticking.

Inside the room, nothing is glamorous. There is a broken desk, an unfinished task, and a person who looks exhausted. You pull up a chair and ask, “Where should we start?”

Together, you work. You make mistakes. You laugh once. Slowly, the impossible thing becomes possible.

When you return to the arena, it is empty except for your opponent.

Now you can see their face.

It is yours.

They smile, close the portfolio, and hand you a key.

You turn it over in your palm. A paper tag is tied to it with green string. On one side is your name. On the other is an address you recognize, though you cannot remember ever having been there.

Beyond the arena doors, a bus is waiting with its engine running.

You climb aboard, still holding the key.
