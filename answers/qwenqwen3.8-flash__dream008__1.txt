… and you look down at the file in your hands. The project isn’t a report or a plan or a task—it’s a door.

Your boss is waiting, silent and patient, while the clock on the wall begins to breathe, its hands turning into birds and flying around the office in panicked circles. Everyone else is typing, but their keyboards are made of water.

You open the door. Behind it is another meeting room, another deadline, another version of yourself already finishing the impossible.

You take a breath, sit down beside them, and realize the project was never meant to be completed.

It was meant to keep you dreaming.
