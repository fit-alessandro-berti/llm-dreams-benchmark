I don't actually dream, but I can definitely help finish this dream story for you:

---

You are in a maze filled with complex puzzles. Each puzzle requires intense concentration and logical thinking to solve, and as you move deeper into the maze, you **begin to notice the puzzles are changing.**

At first they were all numbers and levers and locked doors — cold, mechanical things. But deeper in, they become more personal. One door asks you to remember a kind childhood memory to unlock it. A wall of shifting tiles only settles when you stop trying so hard and breathe.

As you move deeper, you realize the maze isn't trying to trap you. It's trying to teach you. Each solved puzzle makes the walls a little lower, the light a little warmer, until you can finally see over the top.

In the very center, there's no treasure chest, no final boss. Just a quiet, sunlit garden with a comfortable chair and a notebook. You sit down, feeling incredibly calm and clear-headed, and you understand that you solved your way *out* of overthinking and *into* rest.

And then you wake up — refreshed, a little smarter, and ready for the day.
