You realize you never actually told them your destination, and yet you have no doubt they know exactly where you need to be.

At the end of the alley, the city simply dissolves. 

You step out onto a quiet, moonlit pier stretching over a vast, glassy ocean that shouldn't exist here. Moored at the edge is a small wooden boat, strung with warm paper lanterns that bob gently on the dark water. 

You turn to thank the strangers, your chest filled with a sudden, weightless sense of relief. They are already stepping back into the golden glow of the alleyway, waving with quiet smiles as their shapes begin to blur into the mist.
