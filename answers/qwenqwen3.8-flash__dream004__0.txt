A blank canvas and, as you step closer, its white surface begins to breathe—shifting like milk stirred into light. You reach out, and before your fingertips touch it, the canvas pours your thoughts into shapes: spirals that hum with violet, triangles that whisper in gold, a soft rectangle that feels like the memory of a door you once walked through. The dream colors the painting, then the painting colors the dream. Soon the whole landscape is being rewritten around you, and you realize you are not only observing the canvas—you are its painter, its color, and its next impossible shape.
