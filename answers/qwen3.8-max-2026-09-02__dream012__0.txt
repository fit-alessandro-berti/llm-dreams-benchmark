You realize the storm is not trying to destroy you. It is testing you.

You turn the wheel toward the light.

The waves rage higher, but your ship begins to glide, faster now, as if carried by unseen hands. Rain turns to mist. Thunder softens into the sound of distant drums. The crew falls silent, watching as the storm parts around you like a curtain.

When the sea finally stills, the ship drifts into a calm lagoon beneath a sky full of unfamiliar stars. The island rises before you, lush and luminous, its shores lined with lanterns that seem to have been waiting.

Your crew turns to you, no longer afraid.

You breathe deeply, and in that dream, you understand: you were never just sailing through the storm.

You were being guided to a place you had forgotten but always needed to find.
