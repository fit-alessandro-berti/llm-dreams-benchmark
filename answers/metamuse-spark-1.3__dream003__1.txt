Here's how the dream continues:

...intricate patterns and tiny lines of gold that *move* when you're not looking directly at them.

Vines turning into rivers turning into constellations. As you run your thumb over them, they warm under your touch, pulsing faintly like a heartbeat. You turn the box over and over looking for a keyhole — but there isn't one. No lock, no hinge, no seam. Just one smooth, dark circle in the center, about the size of your fingertip.

In the logic of dreams, you just know what to do. You press your thumb into the circle and hold your breath.

*Click.*

Not a loud click, but a soft sigh, like the box has been waiting for you. The lid doesn't swing open — it dissolves, folding back like petals. Cold air breathes out, smelling faintly of rain and old paper and somewhere you've been before.

Inside, there is no treasure. There is a small, night sky.

Stars swirl slowly in velvet darkness. And floating in the middle is a folded slip of paper in your own handwriting. You unfold it, and it just says:

*You already know what's in here.*

And suddenly you do. You wake up with the feeling still in your chest — warm, light, unlocked — even if you can't quite remember what it was.
