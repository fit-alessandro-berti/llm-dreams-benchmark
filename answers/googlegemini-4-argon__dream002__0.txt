In the distance, a flash of lightning illuminates an old, wooden door standing completely freestanding in the middle of a clearing. It has no walls, no roof, just a brass doorknob that is rattling urgently, as if someone—or something—on the other side is trying to let you in before the sky breaks completely. 

You try to run toward it, fighting that familiar, heavy-limbed slowness of dreams, where the air feels as thick as water. With a final, desperate lunge, your hand grasps the warm brass knob. Thunder cracks directly overhead, shaking the ground, and as you pull the door open, a blinding, brilliant white light floods your vision, and...
