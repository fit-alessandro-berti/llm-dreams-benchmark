**…forge ahead and build a new beginning.**

The sun hangs low behind a veil of ash, casting everything in a dim, amber glow. You push through the rubble of a collapsed overpass, your boots crunching on broken glass and twisted metal. Every sound — a distant gust of wind, a stray cat darting through a doorway — sends your heart racing.

But you keep moving.

You clutch a tattered journal you found months ago, filled with sketches of clean water systems, seed storage techniques, and solar panel diagrams left behind by someone who believed the world could be rebuilt. You don't know who they were, but their hope has become your compass.

After days of travel, you stumble upon a valley untouched by the worst of the destruction — a hidden pocket of green where a river still runs clear and a few stubborn wildflowers push through the cracked earth. Your hands tremble as you drop your pack and fall to your knees beside the water.

This is it.

You begin to dig, to plant, to build a shelter from the bones of the old world. Slowly, others begin to find their way to you — a mechanic with calloused hands, a teacher who remembers how to write things down, a child who still knows how to laugh.

And just as the first fragile sprout breaks through the soil, a sound echoes in the distance — not a gunshot, not a cry of despair, but something you haven't heard in a very long time:

**Someone else singing.**
