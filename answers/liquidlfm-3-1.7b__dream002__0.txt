You’re strolling along a sun‑dappled path, the rustle of leaves a gentle lullaby, the air scented with pine and wildflowers. Then—without warning—the sky bruises from an unseen cloud, and the warm breeze is replaced by a sudden chill. A low, electric hum rises from the earth, and the first drops of rain begin to tap against the trunks like tiny drums.  

The forest seems to hold its breath. The birds freeze mid‑song, their feathers quivering as if caught in an invisible current. Shadows stretch longer, twisting into shapes that look like old stories being whispered. A faint, metallic tang fills the air, and the distant thunder rolls in waves, each one shaking the branches and sending them swaying in rhythm with the storm’s heartbeat.  

In that moment, the world feels both familiar and alien: the peace you once enjoyed gives way to raw, elemental power. You can’t help but glance at your own reflection in a nearby pond—your face streaked with droplets, eyes wide as if searching for a hidden light. And then, just as the lightning flashes across the canopy, a soft, golden glow emerges from the heart of the woods, promising that even amidst the chaos, there’s a quiet magic waiting to be discovered.
