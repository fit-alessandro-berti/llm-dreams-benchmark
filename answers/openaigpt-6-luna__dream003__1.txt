You press your palm against it. The lid opens without a sound.

Inside is not a treasure, but a small night sky, full of stars. One drifts upward and settles in your palm, where it becomes a key. When you look back at the box, it has vanished. In its place is a door you’ve never seen before, standing open beneath the stars.
