The first one reads: **What did you want to be before you knew anyone could be disappointed in you?**

You turn the page. There’s a photograph of you at seven, kneeling beside a puddle with a soup spoon. You remember this afternoon. You were trying to dig a canal to the sea.

“Will there be partial credit?” you ask.

The proctor looks up from her knitting. “For the canal?”

You look down. Water is seeping from the spine of the exam booklet. It trickles over the edge of your desk, and all around you people lift their bags without looking up. From somewhere under the floor comes the low, long note of a ship’s horn.

You put your ear to the page.

“Coming through,” someone calls. And then, a little louder: “Could you make it wider?”
