The first puzzles were simple enough—a sequence of tiles to step on in the right order, a lever that had to be pulled while balancing on one foot. But now, deeper in, the challenges have taken on a strange quality. The walls are covered in equations that rewrite themselves as I read them, and the door ahead is locked with a riddle that keeps changing its own wording, as if it knows I'm trying to memorize it.

*"What remains when you remove everything that can be removed?"*

I answer aloud: "Nothing."

The door doesn't open. I try again: "The concept of removal itself."

Still nothing. I sit down against the cold stone, frustrated, and that's when I notice—the answer isn't in words at all. The riddle is written in a language made of light, and when I stop *reading* it and simply *look* at the pattern of the letters, I see it: the negative space between the characters forms a key.

I press my palm against the empty space between two words, and the door dissolves like sugar in water.

Beyond it, the maze opens into a vast chamber where the puzzles are no longer objects—they're people. Figures made of shadow, each trapped inside a glass case, each mouthing a question I somehow already know the answer to. I free them one by one, and with each release, I feel lighter, as though the puzzles were never in the maze at all. They were pieces of something I'd been carrying.

The final door has no lock, no riddle, no mechanism. Just a mirror.

I look into it, and the reflection doesn't move when I do. It simply smiles and mouths: *"You were never lost. You were just walking in circles around the one answer you refused to see."*

The maze begins to crumble—not violently, but gently, like sand returning to a beach. And as the walls fall away, I realize the sky above was never a sky at all.

I wake up.

Or... I wake into another dream, standing at the entrance of a new maze, except this one feels familiar. Comforting, even.

And somewhere far behind me, faint as an echo, I hear the riddle asking itself again—because in dreams, no puzzle is ever truly solved. It's only waiting for you to come back and see it differently.
