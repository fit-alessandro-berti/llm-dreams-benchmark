And you hesitate for a moment - not out of fear, but because you hadn't realized how heavy that feeling of being alone had become until someone offered to lift it.

You nod, and one of them smiles. Not in a rush, not pitying, just *warm*. 

*"No one really knows where they're going here,"* she says, as if letting you in on a secret. *"We just walk together for a while."*

And you walk.

The bustling city, which a moment ago felt like a maze of too-loud traffic and too-fast strangers, begins to soften. The neon signs above you don't just advertise - they glow like lanterns. The rain-slick streets reflect the lights like a river of stars, and the crowd no longer pushes past you, it flows *around* you, like you belong inside its current.

One of the strangers offers you his umbrella, even though it's barely drizzling. Another shows you a hand-drawn map on the back of a coffee receipt - the lines keep shifting as you look at it, like the city itself is breathing. You laugh, a real laugh that surprises you, and they laugh with you.

You don't even tell them exactly where you were trying to go. You just walk and talk, past a street musician whose violin sounds exactly like a memory you forgot, past a bakery where the warm air smells like home.

And as you turn the final corner, you see it - not necessarily the place you thought you were searching for, but a quiet, open plaza where the noise of the city fades. There's a bench there, string lights overhead, and a feeling that this is exactly where you were supposed to arrive.

One of the strangers gently squeezes your shoulder.

*"See? You were never as lost as you felt."*

You sit down for a moment, watching the city bustle on without you needing to chase it. You feel found. Not because you have all the directions now, but because you don't have to find your way alone anymore.

And somewhere in the dream, you understand - the kindness you were looking for was there all along, waiting for you to look up.
