You try again, and this time a single clear word escapes: *"Help?"*

She looks up and points down a narrow alley you hadn't noticed before. The alley is impossibly long, stretching into a warm golden light at the far end. As you walk toward it, the city noise fades, replaced by a rhythmic hum — something like an elevator, or a heartbeat.

You reach the end of the alley and step through the light into a room that feels both enormous and intimate: a vast conference table surrounded by people you somehow know, though you can't say from where. Your shoes are now bare feet on cool marble. Everyone turns to look at you — and they're smiling.

Someone slides a chair forward. You sit, and suddenly you have the presentation already in your mind, though you've never given it before. The words pour out of you — calm, confident, clear. You don't know what you're saying, but everyone nods as though it's exactly what they needed to hear.

And just as you finish, a chime sounds somewhere far above, and the room begins to glow a deeper shade of gold, warm like morning sun. The people start to dissolve into soft light, and you feel a profound sense that you've arrived exactly where you were meant to be — even though you never learned where the meeting was.

You open your eyes to a grey ceiling, still carrying the faintest echo of that warm light behind your eyelids.
