The patterns are not random. They twist into spirals, then into letters, then into something older than language. As you stare, the symbols rearrange themselves into a single image: your own face, younger, looking back at you with an expression you cannot name.

The lock clicks by itself.

No wind moves, yet the box opens on its own, releasing a curl of warm silver mist. Inside, there is no treasure, no relic, no note—only a second smaller box, identical to the first, except for a single word carved beneath the lid:

**AWAKE.**

Your hand hovers over it. Somewhere far away, you hear a door close in a house you have never entered. The dream light flickers, and for a moment the box is no longer in your hands—it is in your chest, beating once, gently, in time with your heart.
