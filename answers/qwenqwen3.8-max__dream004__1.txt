You are in a world where colors and shapes are constantly changing. As you explore this surreal landscape, you come across a blank canvas and, the instant you notice it, every sound around you softens, as if the dream itself is waiting to see what you will do.

A brush forms in your hand—not from wood or bristle, but from a thin ribbon of moving light. When you lift it toward the canvas, the canvas breathes outward, rippling like water. You realize that this world is not made of paint or stone, but of feelings searching for form.

You touch the brush to the surface, and a door appears. It is violet at first, then silver, then the color of a memory you cannot quite name. As you paint, shapes bloom from the door: stairways that fold into birds, windows that open into oceans, stars that fall gently like snow and melt into your skin.

The landscape begins to answer you. Mountains rise in rhythm with your heartbeat. Rivers curve into spirals whenever you feel curious. The sky shifts from deep indigo to warm gold whenever you feel hope. You understand, in the way one understands things only in dreams, that the blank canvas was never meant to hold a picture. It was meant to show you that the world is unfinished, and that you are one of its makers.

You paint one final thing: a small glowing path leading from the canvas into the distance. The moment you finish it, the path steps out of the frame and lies at your feet.

You take one step onto it, and the dream folds around you gently, like a page turning itself.
