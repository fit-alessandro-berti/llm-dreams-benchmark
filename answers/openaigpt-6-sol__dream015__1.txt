You find a packet of seeds in the pocket of an abandoned coat and carry it for days, searching for a place with water. At last you reach a cracked courtyard where rain has gathered in a rusted basin. You clear a patch of earth and press the seeds into it.

Each morning, you return. At first there is only dirt. Then one day, a green shoot rises through the dust.

You kneel to look closer, and hear footsteps behind you. Three strangers stand at the courtyard gate. You tense, ready to defend the little patch, but one of them sets down a dented watering can. Another holds up a handful of seeds.

Soon all four of you are pulling weeds. The ruined city is still dangerous, and the garden is still small. Before you leave, you agree to come back tomorrow.
