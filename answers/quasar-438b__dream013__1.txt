To finish the maze, you must reach into the mirror and pluck the correct thought out of the air. You hesitate, extending your hand toward the glass, but as your fingertips touch the surface, the mirror shatters into a cloud of silver butterflies.

The butterflies flutter upward, carrying the walls of the maze away with them. Stripped of the labyrinth, you find yourself standing in an empty, blindingly white void. The intense concentration fades, and you realize the true puzzle was never about finding the exit, but understanding that you were the maze itself.
