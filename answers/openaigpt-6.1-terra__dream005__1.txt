You are at a large social gathering where you only know one person. As you try to find them, you encounter various groups of people, each speaking a language you almost understand.

Someone hands you a glass filled with tiny silver fish. You set it down, and when you look back, it’s a glass of water. Everyone nearby applauds politely.

You move through a doorway into another room, then another. In each one, someone says your friend has just left. The rooms grow smaller until you have to turn sideways to pass between the guests, who continue their conversations without seeming to notice.

At last, you open a door and step into your childhood bedroom. Your friend is sitting on the floor, untangling a string of lights.

“I’ve been looking everywhere for you,” you say.

“I know,” they reply. “You kept leaving before I could catch up.”

They plug in the lights. Outside the window, the entire party glows beneath the sea.
