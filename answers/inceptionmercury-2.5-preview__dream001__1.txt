

You start to walk, but the streets breathe, expanding and contracting with a slow, rhythmic pulse. Skyscrapers lean in like old friends whispering secrets you almost recall. Time feels thick, like honey dripping from a spoon.

A figure materializes from the fog, holding a map drawn on shifting sand. They point to a door floating mid-air, framed by blooming roses that sing in minor keys. You step through, and the hallway stretches into infinity, lined with mirrors reflecting versions of you who made different choices.

At the end, a table sits under a sky of swirling nebulas. One chair remains empty. You take your seat, and the agenda unfolds not on paper, but as a melody playing in your chest. The discussion begins with silence, and everyone understands perfectly.
