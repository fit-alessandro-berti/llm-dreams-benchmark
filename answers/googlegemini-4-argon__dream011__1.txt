Desperate to catch up, I reach for my #2 pencil, which immediately turns into a cooked spaghetti noodle in my hand. I try to straighten it out, whispering, *"Hold it together, my future depends on this,"* when the professor—who is now a giant, grandfather clock wearing a tweed jacket—strolls down the aisle. 

Instead of scolding me, the clock-professor leans in, ticks loudly, and whispers in my mother's voice, "Don't forget to show your work, and remember to breathe underwater." 

At that exact moment, the floor gives way beneath my desk. My stomach drops as I plummet through a sky made of velvet, clutching my soggy spaghetti-pencil, and I jolt awake in my bed, my heart racing, tangled in my bedsheets.
