...a set of brushes that seem to hum with a quiet energy of their own.

As you reach for one, the brush leaps into your hand, eager, like it's been waiting for you. There's no paint anywhere, but when you touch the bristles to the canvas, color pours out, drawn not from any palette but from the world around you. A streak of violet peels away from the shifting sky. A ribbon of gold unspools from a passing triangle that was, a moment ago, a bird.

You begin to paint without knowing what you're painting. Your hand moves on its own, and the strokes arrange themselves into a door: tall, arched, the deep blue of the hour just before dawn. The moment you finish the handle, it gleams and turns solid under your fingers.

The landscape goes still. For the first time, the colors stop changing, as if the whole world is holding its breath to see what you'll do.

You open the door.

On the other side is a room you almost recognize, maybe a childhood bedroom or a place you've only visited in other dreams. The walls breathe gently. On a small table sits another canvas, and on it is a painting of you, standing in a surreal landscape, holding a brush, looking at a blank canvas.

The painted version of you turns, meets your eyes, and smiles as if to say, *you're finally here.*

Then the brush in your hand grows warm, the edges of everything soften into light, and you understand, in the way you only understand things in dreams, that you were never exploring this world. You were painting it all along.

The colors begin to shift again, and you raise the brush once more.
