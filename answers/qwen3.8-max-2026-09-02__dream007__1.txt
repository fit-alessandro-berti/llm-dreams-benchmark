You are in the middle of a heated argument with a close friend. The disagreement is about something important to both of you, and you decide to stop speaking—not out of anger, but because you suddenly notice that every word either of you says becomes a small, glowing moth that flutters between you.

The moths circle in the air, their wings carrying fragments of what you’ve been trying to say: memories, misunderstandings, apologies you haven’t made yet. Your friend sees them too. Their expression softens. The room around you begins to shift, the walls turning into curtains moving in a slow wind.

You realize the argument was never really about winning. It was about being seen.

You reach out and catch one moth gently in your hands. When you open your palms, it becomes a tiny light, warm and trembling. Your friend does the same. Without saying anything, you both place the lights together between you. They merge into something brighter, not perfect, but honest.

The dream ends with the two of you sitting side by side in silence, watching the light rise slowly toward the sky like a lantern released into the night.
