You are given a mysterious locked box with no key in sight. As you examine it closely, you notice intricate patterns and living filigree that rearranges itself whenever your eyes drift, forming constellations, then faces, then a single unblinking eye. The metal grows warm; the eye blinks once and the lock dissolves into smoke. Inside is not an object but a smaller version of the same box, nested infinitely, each one whispering a memory you have not yet lived. You reach for the innermost box and find your own sleeping hand already holding it. The dream folds.
