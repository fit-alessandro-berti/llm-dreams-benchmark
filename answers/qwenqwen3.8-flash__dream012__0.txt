You take the wheel, your knuckles white against the carved wood, and whisper, “Hold the line.” The ship groans like an old beast waking, and the next wave crashes over the deck as if the sea itself is trying to swallow you whole. Rain strikes your face, cold and endless, but you do not look away. Below, in the belly of the ship, something begins to knock—slow, deliberate, patient. Your crew freezes. One of them mouths, “Captain…?” and you answer without turning:

“It’s waking up.”
