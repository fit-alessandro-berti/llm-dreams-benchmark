You reach the center only to find a door with your name written on it in handwriting that shifts when you blink. When you push it open, you step out into a room you recognize with perfect, aching clarity—your childhood bedroom, exactly as it was thirty years ago, except the walls are still breathing, and through the window you can see the maze stretching endlessly in every direction, and you understand, finally, that you have always been here, solving, walking, dreaming yourself awake.
