...and each one seems to reshape itself the moment you understand it.

The first challenge takes place in a vast auditorium where the seats are filled with everyone you've ever met. A judge whose face keeps shifting hands you a sealed envelope. Inside is a single instruction: *"Explain what you love to someone who has never loved anything."* You start speaking, and your words leave your mouth as small paper birds that circle the room. Some of the audience catch them. The judge nods, and the floor quietly folds away.

Now you're in a hallway of doors, each labeled with a job you once thought you wanted: astronaut, baker, lighthouse keeper, the person who names paint colors. The other contestants rush through them, but you notice an unmarked door, slightly ajar, with warm light spilling out. You walk through it.

The second challenge is a staircase that only goes up when you stop trying to climb it. You watch competitors sprint in place, frustrated. You sit down on a step to think, and it begins to carry you upward like a slow escalator through the clouds. Somewhere below, a clock strikes thirteen.

At the top is the final round. There is no judge and no audience, only a desk with a mirror on it. Your reflection is wearing the lanyard of the dream job already. It looks tired but happy.

"The last challenge," your reflection says, "is to tell me why you want this. Not the answer you rehearsed. The real one."

You open your mouth, and to your surprise, the truth comes out easily, something simple that you'd forgotten you knew. Your reflection smiles, slides the lanyard through the glass, and says, "You start Monday."

A confetti of paper birds falls from the ceiling. As the dream begins to dissolve at its edges, you clutch the lanyard and try to read the job title printed on it. The letters swim, rearrange, and almost come into focus...

...and then you wake, with the feeling still warm in your chest and the answer you gave just on the tip of your tongue.
