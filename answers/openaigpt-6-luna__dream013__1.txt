A pattern you solved on the wall reappears on the floor, its symbols rearranged. A door opens only when you stop trying to force it. Beyond it, the maze grows quiet. The corridors are lined with mirrors, but none of them show your reflection—instead, each holds a different memory, bright and vivid as if it’s happening now.

At the center of the maze, you find a small room beneath a star-filled sky. A single key rests on a stone table. You know, somehow, that it will unlock the way out. But beside it is a note in your own handwriting:

**You don’t have to solve everything to leave.**

The key turns easily. The walls soften into mist, and you step through—only to wake with the feeling that, somewhere, the maze is still waiting.
