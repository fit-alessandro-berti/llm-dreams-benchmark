you come across a blank canvas and a paintbrush floating beside it. When you take the brush, it feels warm, like a hand you almost remember.

You paint a blue circle. It rolls off the canvas and becomes a pond. You paint a yellow line, and a path unfolds across the water. On the other side stands a small house with a light in its window.

Inside, someone is painting too. You lean over their shoulder and see the landscape you’ve just crossed, the pond, the path, and yourself standing in the doorway.

The painter turns. Their face is your own, though older somehow, or younger. They hand you the brush.

“What happens next?” they ask.

You look at the little painted version of yourself. It looks back, then raises its brush and paints a door.
