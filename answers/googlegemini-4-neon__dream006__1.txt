A strange, weightless confidence washes over you. You clear your throat again, and the blinding spotlight overhead suddenly softens into a warm afternoon sun. The audience rises to their feet in a standing ovation, gently lifting off the floor and drifting toward the open sky like released balloons. As they applaud silently from the clouds, the boat tilts upward to follow them, and you find yourself faintly annoyed because you still haven't gotten to your second bullet point—and you're suddenly not sure if you remembered to turn off the stove before you left.
