The waves rose like mountains of black glass, swallowing the bow whole, then recoiling to reveal jagged crests that shattered against the hull. The wind howled through the rigging, a relentless chorus of unseen voices, and I could feel the *Vanguard* trembling beneath me, alive with the violence of the elements. My crew moved with quiet urgency, their faces half-shadow, half-light from the fitful lightning that streaked the bruised horizon. They looked to me not just for orders, but for the faintest sign that the storm would pass, that the compass still pointed true.

I gripped the wheel until my knuckles turned white, steering not just through the tempest, but into the eye of whatever dream this sea was meant to show me. And in that moment, the thunder didn't drown out the voice inside me—it spoke through me, calm amid the chaos, guiding us both toward a dawn I hadn't believed in.
