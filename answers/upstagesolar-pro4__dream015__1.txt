Despite the hardships, you decide to plant seeds in the ash-choked earth. It seems absurd, perhaps even foolish, to nurture life when survival is the only mantra left. But as you kneel beside the rusted skeleton of a highway sign and bury the last of your heirloom beans, you feel a quiet defiance spark in your chest.

Days turn into weeks. You guard the patch of darkened soil like a dragon guarding gold, shielding it from the scorching radiation winds with scavenged sheets of plastic. Then, one morning, amidst the gray desolation, a splash of vibrant green breaks the surface. It is small, fragile, and utterly miraculous.

That single sprout becomes a beacon. It draws others who thought hope was extinct. Together, you build a fence not to keep people out, but to protect the life within. You decide that this spot will not just be a place to survive, but a place to begin again. By the time the first true leaves unfurl, you know the world isn't ending anymore; it's just waiting for you to start it over.
