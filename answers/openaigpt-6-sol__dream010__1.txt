As the competition progresses, you face various challenges and slowly realize that no one has told you what the job actually is.

In the first round, you build a bridge from paper. In the second, you must persuade a locked door to open. By the third, the other contestants have vanished, though their names still appear on the scoreboard.

For the final challenge, you enter a bright office. On the desk sits a nameplate with your name on it. Behind the desk is a window overlooking every place you have ever wanted to go.

A voice asks, “Do you accept the position?”

You reach for the contract, then notice that the door to the office has no handle on this side.

Instead of signing, you open the window. A warm wind scatters the papers, and somewhere below, a crowd begins to cheer.
