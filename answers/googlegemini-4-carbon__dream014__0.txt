Instantly, the suffocating roar of sirens and hurried footsteps melts away, transforming into the low, soothing hum of distant wind chimes. You notice that the strangers aren't wearing ordinary clothes; their coats seem to be woven from twilight and softly shifting shadows, and their smiles feel inexplicably familiar, like faces from a forgotten childhood memory.

Instead of pulling out a map, the tallest stranger gently taps the wet pavement with the tip of an umbrella. Ripples spread across the asphalt as if it were dark water. Without a word, they lead you down a narrow alleyway you are certain wasn't there a moment ago. As you walk together, the towering, claustrophobic skyscrapers begin to bend outward, unfolding like giant structures of origami to reveal a vast, violet sky dusted with impossibly large stars. 

Gravity loosens its grip. With every step, you feel lighter, rising inches off the cobblestones until you and the strangers are effortlessly gliding upward, past glowing apartment windows where people are dancing in slow motion, and past streetlights that bloom into luminous glass flowers as you float by. The crushing loneliness in your chest dissolves, replaced by a profound, buoyant warmth. 

Finally, you drift over the highest rooftop, only to find it isn't a roof at all—it is a quiet, sun-drenched meadow overlooking an ocean of clouds, with the familiar front door of your very own home standing freestanding in the tall grass. 

You turn to thank your guides, realizing they never actually spoke a single word, yet you understood them completely. Before you can speak, a warm breeze sweeps across the meadow, and the strangers scatter into a flock of iridescent birds, spiraling upward into the starry violet sky. You reach for the doorknob, feeling entirely safe and found, just as the edges of the world begin to fade softly into the morning light.
