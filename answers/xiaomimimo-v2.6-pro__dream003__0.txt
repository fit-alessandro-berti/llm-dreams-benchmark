...strange symbols that seem to shift and rearrange themselves every time you blink. The patterns pulse with a faint, warm glow — like something inside is breathing, is *alive*.

You run your fingers along the surface of the box, and the moment your skin touches the wood, the symbols begin to hum. Not a sound exactly — more like a vibration in your teeth, in the back of your skull. The box *knows* you.

You try to pry it open with your fingernails, with a loose stone from the ground, with a stick you pluck from the air as though it were always there. Nothing works. The box is seamless, impossibly smooth, like it was carved from a single piece of dark, impossible wood that reflects no light.

Then, without thinking, you press your thumb to the largest symbol — a spiral, like the shape of a galaxy seen from impossibly far away. It blazes white-hot beneath your skin, and suddenly, the box isn't locked anymore.

It wasn't locked because of a mechanism. It was locked because of *intention*. It was waiting for someone to want to open it *desperately enough*.

The lid rises on its own.

Inside, you don't find treasure. You don't find a key, or a map, or a dangerous shadow. What you find is a mirror — a small, perfectly round mirror sitting on a bed of black velvet.

But the reflection looking back at you isn't *you*. Not exactly. The reflection is smiling when you are not. The reflection is older — not in age, but in *knowing*. And the reflection speaks, though you cannot hear its words.

Instead, the words appear on the inside of the lid in glowing script:

**"What you locked away, you have now returned to yourself. Carry it gently."**

You wake up with your hand pressed over your heart, still feeling the warmth of the spiral beneath your thumb, still hearing the hum that isn't quite a sound. And on your nightstand, where nothing was before, there sits a single splinter of dark wood — smooth, warm, and faintly glowing in the shape of a spiral.

You are no longer sure if you are still dreaming.
