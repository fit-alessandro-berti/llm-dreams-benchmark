"Lost?" she asks, smiling as if she already knows the answer. "We were all lost once. That's how we found each other."

You walk side by side. The city rearranges itself around you: alleyways become staircases, shop windows flicker with lights that spell out words you almost recognize. The group points to a red door set into a brick wall that shouldn't exist—except it does, and it's exactly where your destination must be.

Before you can ask the question burning in your chest, the kind woman leans close and whispers, "You don't have to find the way alone anymore. That's what we're for."

And then the city dissolves into a vast, quiet expanse of snow, and above you the sky fills with every light you've ever been afraid to look for—and you reach up, and they reach back.
