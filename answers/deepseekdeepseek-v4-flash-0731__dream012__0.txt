...and you grip the worn wooden wheel, feeling the spray of salt and rain on your face. The sky is a churning bruise of black and green, and every wave slams against the hull like a fist. You turn to your crew, their faces pale but trusting, and you shout above the roar: “Hold steady! We’ve weathered worse.” Then a massive wall of water rises ahead — impossibly high, like a mountain moved by some ancient god. The crew freezes. You grit your teeth, spin the wheel hard to port, and whisper to the storm, “Not today.” As the ship tilts into the wave, a strange calm settles over you. The sound of the sea softens. The wind becomes a lullaby. And you realize—this storm isn’t trying to sink you. It’s trying to show you something. Below the surface, something glows. And you know you have to dive.
