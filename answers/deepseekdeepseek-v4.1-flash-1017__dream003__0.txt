…patterns and tiny constellations etched into the wood, shifting whenever you look away. The lid feels warm under your palms, and when you press your ear to it, you hear a faint heartbeat—or maybe your own, doubled.

You trace one of the patterns, and it rearranges into a sentence in a language you somehow understand: *“The key was never lost. You left it in the waking world.”*

At that, the box sighs open by itself. Inside is a smaller box, and inside that, another, each one carved with the same warning in a different hand. The smallest box is no bigger than a seed. You open it, and a gust of bright wind rushes out, carrying the smell of rain on a street you have never walked, the sound of someone laughing just out of sight.

Then you notice the key in your hand—cold, familiar, made of a memory you can’t quite name. The patterns on the boxes all go still. Somewhere beyond the dream, a door you forgot was locked begins to open.
