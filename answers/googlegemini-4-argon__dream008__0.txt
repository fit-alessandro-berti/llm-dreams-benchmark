Your boss is standing inches away, tapping their wristwatch. You glance at the watch and see it doesn’t have numbers—just a spinning wheel of different shades of red, ticking loudly like a grandfather clock. "I need this by yesterday," your boss says, though their mouth doesn't move; the voice is coming from the office potted plant, which is suddenly towering toward the ceiling like a jungle vine.

Panic sets in. You turn to your computer to start typing, desperately trying to look productive. However, the keyboard is soft and squishy, like marshmallows. Every time you press a key, it sinks down and plays a loud, echoing trumpet note instead of typing a letter. *Honk. Blare. Toot.* 

You try to apologize, to explain that the system is down, but your tongue feels heavy and tangled. You look down and realize, with a sudden jolt of classic dream-horror, that you are wearing your childhood pajamas and fuzzy slippers. 

"Well?" your boss asks, though they have now morphed into your ninth-grade math teacher. "Are you going to compile the symphony or not?"

Desperate to meet the deadline, you grab a stapler and start stapling the air. Miraculously, each click of the stapler produces a fully formed, glowing origami swan that floats up toward the fluorescent lights. Your boss nods slowly, looking deeply impressed. "Finally," they whisper. "Someone who understands the new synergy."

Suddenly, the walls of your cubicle fall flat like cardboard cutouts, revealing that your desk is actually sitting in the middle of a vast, sunny beach. The sound of the ticking watch fades into the roar of ocean waves. The impossible deadline is completely forgotten as your swivel chair transforms into a giant inflatable flamingo, and the rising tide gently carries you out to sea.
