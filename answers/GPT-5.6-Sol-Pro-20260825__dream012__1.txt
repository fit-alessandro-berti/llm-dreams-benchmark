You are the captain of a ship in the middle of a turbulent sea. Your crew looks to you for guidance as the storm worsens, and you realize the compass is spinning wildly, pointing in every direction at once.

A wall of black water rises ahead, so tall it seems to touch the clouds. Fear spreads across the deck, but beneath the roar of the wind, you hear something else—a deep, rhythmic sound coming from below the ship, like the beating of an enormous heart.

Instead of turning away, you order the crew to sail directly toward the wave. They hesitate, but your certainty steadies them. Together, they pull the ropes, secure the sails, and brace themselves. The ship climbs the towering wave, higher and higher, until the sea disappears beneath a blanket of clouds.

At the crest, everything becomes silent.

Floating above the storm is a calm, moonlit ocean in the sky. Stars swim beneath its surface like silver fish, and in the distance stands a glowing lighthouse suspended in midair. Its beam falls upon your ship, and the spinning compass suddenly stops, pointing toward it.

When you reach the lighthouse, you find no keeper inside—only a small wooden box bearing your name. You open it and discover a second compass, its face completely blank. As you hold it, a needle appears and points not toward north, but directly at your heart.

The storm below begins to fade. Your crew cheers, believing you have saved them, but you understand that the lighthouse was never guiding the ship. It was waiting for you to trust your own direction.

As the first light of dawn spreads across the sky, the ship transforms into a great white bird. The sails become wings, the deck becomes feathers, and you rise over the quiet sea—just as you wake with the feeling that somewhere, a compass is still pointing toward you.
