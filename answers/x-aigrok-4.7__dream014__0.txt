You follow them down a side street that smells like rain and warm bread. One of them points out a mural you’d walked past twice without seeing—a map painted as a flock of birds—and another lends you their umbrella even though the sky has already cleared. By the time you reach the corner you needed, the city doesn’t feel so loud. You turn to thank them, and they’re already waving, as if helping a stranger find their way was the most ordinary thing in the world.
