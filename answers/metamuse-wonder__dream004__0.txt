...and a paintbrush floating beside it, dripping colors that haven't been invented yet.

You reach for the brush, and the moment your fingers touch it, the whole landscape holds its breath. The hills stop melting. The sky, which a second ago was a swirling purple ocean, freezes mid-wave. Even the trees — tall, thin triangles one moment, soft floating jellyfish the next — turn toward you, waiting.

The canvas isn't blank. Not really. As you lean closer, you see faint shapes moving just beneath its surface, like something dreaming inside the white.

You paint a single line.

It doesn't stay a line. It curves on its own, grows feathers, and lifts off the canvas as a small silver bird. It circles your head once, singing a note that tastes like rain and warm bread, then flies into the sky and becomes a new cloud.

Excited, you paint faster.

A door, crooked and golden, and it creaks open in the middle of the air. A river, and it pours *upward* from the canvas into the sky, carrying glowing fish. A staircase made of piano keys, and each step you imagine plays a soft chord that changes the color of your clothes.

The world starts changing again, but now it's following *you*. When you laugh, the grass turns to ribbons. When you wonder what’s behind the door, a forest grows around it, full of whispering, translucent leaves.

You paint one last thing: yourself, standing in front of the canvas.

The painted you looks up. And smiles. And reaches out of the painting.

Your hands meet — warm, real, covered in impossible paint — and she pulls you gently forward. The canvas doesn't feel like cloth at all. It feels like cool water, like stepping through a mirror at twilight.

On the other side, everything is quiet and bright. You are made of shifting color now, light-footed and weightless, and below you the dream world spreads out like a living painting you can reshape with a thought.

You turn to wave goodbye to the landscape—

—and wake up in your bed, heart pounding softly, morning light on the wall.

But when you lift your hands, your fingertips are stained silver, gold, and a color you still don't have a name for.
