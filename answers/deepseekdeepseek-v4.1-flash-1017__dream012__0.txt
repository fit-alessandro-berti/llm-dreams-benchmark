…and you feel the wheel tremble beneath your hands as if the ship itself is afraid. The crew waits, rain lashing their faces, and you realize the compass is spinning not toward north, but toward a memory—the harbor you left as a child, glowing beneath the waves. You shout an order, but your voice comes out as gull-song; the sails unfurl into wings, the deck becomes a moonlit field, and the storm softens into a thousand whispering voices. Just before dawn, the sea parts like a curtain, and you guide the ship through the dream’s narrow door, certain that when you wake, you will remember only the warmth of the wheel and the courage it gave you to keep steering.
