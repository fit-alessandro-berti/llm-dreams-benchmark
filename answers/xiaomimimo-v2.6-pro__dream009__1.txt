You wake up in a completely different era, with no modern technology around. As you explore your new surroundings, you realize you need to **adapt — and fast.**

The first thing that hits you is the smell — wood smoke, hay, and something earthy and unfamiliar. You're lying on a straw-stuffed mattress in a small, dimly lit room with rough-hewn wooden walls. A single candle flickers on a nearby table beside a clay pitcher of water. There are no phones, no electric lights, no hum of anything plugged in — just the distant sound of roosters crowing and the creak of a cart rolling over cobblestone.

You sit up and find yourself dressed in strange clothes — a linen tunic, rough-spun trousers, and leather sandals. A hand-stitched leather satchel sits at the foot of the bed. Inside, you find a few dried pieces of bread, a small coin purse with heavy metal coins you don't recognize, and a single piece of parchment with handwriting you can barely decipher.

You push open the wooden shutters and peer outside. **A medieval village** stretches before you — thatched rooftops, a market square buzzing with vendors calling out prices in a language that sounds like a strange cousin of Latin, and in the distance, the towering silhouette of a stone castle perched on a hill, its banner fluttering in the morning breeze.

Your mind races. You need to figure out *where* — and more importantly, *when* — you are. You need to find food, shelter, and most urgently, someone who might understand what's happened to you. But deep down, a voice whispers that no one here will understand a word you say... because the language, the customs, the very world — it's all *wrong* for what you know.

You step out the door and into the bustling cobblestone street. A man in a hooded cloak glances at you with suspicious eyes. A woman haggles with a butcher over a slab of meat. Somewhere, a bell begins to toll from a stone church at the end of the lane.

And then you notice it — *you're being followed.*

Two shadowy figures linger at the far end of the street, watching you. They wear dark robes and carry no weapons you can see, but something about their stillness sends a chill down your spine.

You realize you don't just need to adapt to this era...

**You need to survive it.**

The dream shifts — a ripple like heat on a summer road — and the two figures begin walking toward you, their cloaks billowing despite the windless air. The bell tolls louder now. The villagers seem not to notice anything at all.

Your satchel feels heavier than it should. Your hand grips the strap.

*What do you do?*
