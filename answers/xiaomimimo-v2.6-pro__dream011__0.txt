You are about to take the final exam for a course that determines your future career. As you sit down to start the exam, you notice the questions are **written in a language you don't recognize — symbols that seem to shimmer and shift when you look at them too long.**

Your heart pounds. The proctor glides between the rows like they're floating, and when they pass your desk, they whisper: *"Read with your feelings, not your eyes."*

You close your eyes. And suddenly, the words arrive — not as letters, but as **emotions and images that bloom behind your eyelids** like projections on a theater screen.

**Question 1:** *Show us a time you felt true purpose.* A memory flashes — you see yourself laughing with someone, doing something that lit you up from the inside. You scribble the memory down, and your pencil turns warm in your hand.

**Question 2:** *What would you build if no one was watching?* The page feels magnetic. Ideas pour out of you like water, things you didn't even know you knew.

**Question 3:** *Who are you when everything is stripped away?* The room goes silent. The other students flicker like old television static and disappear. It's just you and the page.

You finish the last question and look up. The exam room is now an **endless meadow at golden hour.** The proctor is gone. In its place is a door standing upright in the grass, glowing from behind.

A voice — your own voice, older and warmer — says from behind the door:

*"There is no grade. There was never a grade. The exam was just to remind you that you already know."*

You reach for the handle. The metal is impossibly warm. You turn it and step through into a bright white light —

And your alarm goes off.

But somewhere in the rush of morning, in the half-second between sleep and waking, you **remember the answers.** Not the words. The *feeling* of having known exactly who you were.

You carry that feeling into the day like a small, glowing ember in your chest. And for the first time in a long time, the future doesn't feel like a test.

It feels like a **door you can't wait to open.**
