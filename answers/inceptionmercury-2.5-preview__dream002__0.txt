

...and the wind begins to whisper names you can't quite recall. The trees bend low, their leaves turning silver in the fading light. Thunder rolls not from the sky, but from the ground beneath your feet. You stop walking, and the storm pauses with you, holding its breath. In that silence, you realize the rain that falls isn't cold, but warm, like sunlight on your skin. It guides you forward, past the dark trunks, to a clearing where a single lantern glows. You step toward it, and then you open your eyes.
