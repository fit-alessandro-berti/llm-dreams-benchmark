You wake up in a completely different era, with no modern technology around. As you explore your new surroundings, you realize you need to blend in immediately or risk being labeled a witch, a demon, or worse. The air is thick with woodsmoke, horse dung, and the tang of unwashed wool. You're standing at the edge of a muddy village square that looks vaguely European, maybe 14th-century, though the language the people are shouting is a guttural mix you only half-recognize. Your modern clothes have vanished; you're wearing a coarse linen tunic that itches like hell and leather boots that don't quite fit.

A blacksmith's hammer rings out. Chickens scatter. No one is staring at a phone because there are no phones, no clocks, no plastic, nothing that belongs to the world you know. Your first instinct is to find clean water and figure out the year, but more urgently you need food and a story that explains why you appeared out of nowhere speaking strangely.

You start walking, trying to copy the locals' posture and keep your mouth shut. An old woman selling turnips eyes you too long. A pair of soldiers in dented helmets glance your way. The realization settles in your gut: this isn't a costume party and you can't just wait for the dream to end. You need allies, or at least a place to hide until you understand the rules of this time... or until whatever force dumped you here decides to yank you back.

A boy no older than ten tugs your sleeve and points toward the forest path, whispering something that sounds like a warning. You follow, because staying in the open suddenly feels like the worse option. The dream is only beginning, and survival is the first puzzle you have to solve.
