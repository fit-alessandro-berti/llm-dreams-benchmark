

You are in a world where colors and shapes are constantly changing. As you explore this surreal landscape, you come across a blank canvas and feel it humming beneath your fingertips, warm as a held breath. The emptiness is not empty at all; it is full of possibilities waiting to be named.

A single drop of impossible color appears in your palm—neither blue nor gold, but the feeling of a memory you cannot quite recall. When it touches the canvas, the surface ripples, and shapes begin to rise: spiraling towers of soft light, rivers of geometry that flow backward, birds made of quiet angles and laughter.

You realize you are not looking at a picture. You are looking at the dream itself, and the dream is looking back.

The world around you folds gently, like a letter being sealed. The canvas closes into a single glowing square, then a point of light, then a sound like your own name spoken from a distance.

When you finally wake, your hand is still open, and the air above your palm shimmers for just a moment—before the ordinary colors settle back into place.
