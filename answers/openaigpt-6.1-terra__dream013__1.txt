At the center, you find a final door with no lock. On it are written the words: **What have you forgotten?**

You search your memory for a clue. Behind you, the maze settles and creaks. Somewhere, a clock begins to tick.

Then you remember the sunlight, and the sound of the sea, and the taste of salt on your lips. You remember that you came here from a beach. Someone was waiting for you there.

You push the door open. Beyond it, the beach stretches into a soft blue morning. A child stands at the water’s edge, holding two kites. When you approach, she hands you one, as if you’ve only been gone a moment.
