You keep walking, but the path begins to change. The soft dirt under your feet becomes cool stone, then water, then something that feels like memory. You are no longer sure whether you are moving through the forest or the forest is moving around you.

Lightning flashes.

In each flash, the trees become something else: tall pillars of black glass, rows of silver clocks, walls of whispering eyes. A sound rises from everywhere at once—not thunder now, but a voice just below hearing, repeating your name in a way that feels both familiar and wrong.

You stop.

The storm has not broken open yet, but it is close enough to touch. The leaves tremble, and from between them you see it: a path that was not there before, glowing faintly blue in the storm-light. It leads deeper into the forest, toward a clearing you have never seen before, where something is waiting.

You know, in the dreamer’s way of knowing, that if you follow it, you will remember something important.

But before you can take another step, the rain begins, and each drop that lands on your skin turns to a tiny word. You catch one, then another.

*Turn around.*

The forest holds its breath.

You turn.
