…and the sky turns a bruised purple.

The wind begins to whisper through the trees at first, then grow into a low, rolling moan. Leaves spiral upward in frantic dances. Thunder rumbles somewhere far off, like a giant clearing its throat. You feel the air tighten around you, electric and humming.

You start to run, but your feet feel slow, as if the forest itself is holding you back. The path twists and turns in ways it didn’t before. Roots rise like small hills. Branches reach down, not to grab you, but to shield you.

Then the rain comes—not as drops, but as a curtain. It pours down in silver sheets, and every drop that hits the ground turns into a tiny glowing pearl. The forest floor begins to shimmer, a river of light flowing at your feet. You stop running.

A voice, soft as moss, seems to rise from the ground: “You don’t have to escape. Just let it pass.”

You close your eyes. The storm roars around you, but you feel no fear. The wind becomes a breath, the thunder a heartbeat. When you open them again, the rain is slowing. The purple sky fades to soft gray, then pale gold. The trees are dripping with light, and the air smells like wet earth and something sweet—like apple blossoms after rain.

A path opens ahead, brighter than before. You know you’re not lost. You are exactly where this dream wants you to be. And with a quiet, wondering smile, you keep walking.
