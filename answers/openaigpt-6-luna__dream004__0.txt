You are in a world where colors and shapes are constantly changing. As you explore this surreal landscape, you come across a blank canvas and pick up a brush with no bristles.

You touch it to the canvas. A blue line appears, then curls into a river, flowing off the edge and through the air. You paint a yellow door beside it. When you open the door, you find the landscape on the other side—only now, it is perfectly still.

A small figure stands there, holding a mirror. In it, you see yourself asleep beneath a sky full of shifting colors. The figure smiles and hands you the mirror.

The moment you take it, the canvas lifts like a curtain, and you wake—still holding, for just an instant, the feeling that somewhere a river is running through the sky.
