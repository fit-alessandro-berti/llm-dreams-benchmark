When you reach it, you find a small clearing where a lantern hangs from a low branch, burning without flame. Beneath it stands a door framed in living wood, half-open, revealing a staircase that spirals downward into warm, quiet darkness.

Behind you, thunder rolls across the sky like a slow heartbeat. You realize the storm is not chasing you—it is guiding you. Taking a deep breath, you step through the doorway, and as you descend, the forest above fades into the sound of rain, leaving only the gentle glow of the lantern and the feeling that you have found something you forgot you were looking for.
