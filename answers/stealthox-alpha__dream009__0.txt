You wake up in a completely different era, with no modern technology around. As you explore your new surroundings, you realize you need to **learn the language of the villagers before nightfall—because the strange markings on your palms have already caught the attention of the town elders, and they believe you're the one foretold in their ancient prophecy.**

---

The cobblestones are cold beneath your bare feet as you wander through the market square. Smoke rises from clay chimneys, and the smell of fresh bread mingles with hay and woodsmoke. A blacksmith pauses mid-swing, staring at you. A child tugs her mother's cloak and points.

You try to speak, but your words come out garbled—the language here is old, melodic, unfamiliar. Yet strangely, the more you listen, the more it *unravels* in your mind, as if the dream itself is translating for you.

An old woman with silver braids approaches. She doesn't seem afraid. Instead, she takes your hand, turns it over, and studies the glowing symbols etched into your skin.

*"You've come at last,"* she whispers. *"The stars said you would."*

Before you can ask what she means, a bell tolls from the hilltop tower—and everyone in the square turns to look at you. Not with fear, but with **hope**. And that, somehow, is far more terrifying.

Just as the elder begins to speak the prophecy aloud, you feel a strange pull behind your eyes—

And you wake up.

Your own ceiling. Your own room. But when you glance down at your palm, just for a second, you'd swear the markings are still there, fading slowly, like ink dissolving in water.

*Was it just a dream?*
