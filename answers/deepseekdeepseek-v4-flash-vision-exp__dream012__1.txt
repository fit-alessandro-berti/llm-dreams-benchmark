And in that moment, the waves rise higher, not to drown you, but to lift you. For just a second, the ship becomes a winged thing, leaping between black clouds. Then the sky cracks open, and a single beam of moonlight touches your face.

You realize you are not fighting the sea. You are remembering how to sail it. When you wake, your hands will still be gripping something—but you won’t know if it was the wheel or a dream.
