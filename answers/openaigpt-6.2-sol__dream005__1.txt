each group makes room for you as though you’ve just stepped away to get a drink. You laugh at the end of a story you haven’t heard. Someone hands you a warm pear.

“Have you seen my friend?” you ask.

“Of course,” they say, and point to the staircase. It has been moving quietly around the edge of the room, waiting for a gap in the conversation.

Upstairs, you find your friend sitting on the carpet outside a closed door. The music is softer here. You sit beside them and offer them the pear.

“I’ve been looking everywhere for you,” you say.

“I know,” they say. “You walked past me twice. But you looked like you were having a good time.”

Below you, someone starts telling the story again. This time, from where you’re sitting, you can hear the beginning.
