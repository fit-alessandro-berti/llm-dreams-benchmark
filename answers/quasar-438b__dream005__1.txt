You are dreaming. Can you complete the following dream?

You are at a large social gathering where you only know one person. As you try to find them, you encounter various groups of people and **each group seems oddly familiar in a way you can't place—a bartender who reminds you of a childhood neighbor, a cluster of strangers arguing about something trivial, a woman standing alone at the edge of the room looking vaguely sad. You feel a growing certainty that the person you're looking for is just ahead, so you push deeper into the gathering, until you realize you've been walking in a wide circle and have passed the same clusters again, though they've subtly rearranged. You recognize no one and everyone simultaneously. The person you came to find feels both absent and all around you, their presence dissolved into the social noise around you, so that finding them would mean not locating a face but recognizing a pattern, and the pattern is your own attention, searching.**
