The wind steals your words. Waves climb the sides of the ship, black and shining, and the compass spins as if it’s trying to escape. Then, in the heart of the storm, you see a door standing upright on the water.

It is painted blue. It has the brass doorknob from your childhood home.

“Captain?” someone calls.

You turn back to the crew, but they’re gone. The ship is empty except for you—and a small white dog sitting calmly at the wheel.

The door swings open. Beyond it is a quiet kitchen, warm with lamplight. Rain taps gently at the window.

You step through.

Behind you, the sea gives one enormous sigh, and the ship folds into a paper boat. It floats across the kitchen floor, carrying the storm away.
