You’re perched at the edge of a mahogany desk that feels both familiar and alien—its surface cool to the touch, speckled with faint constellations that pulse in time with your breathing. As you slide the chair back and settle into the seat, a soft hum rises from the walls, like a distant choir of wind chimes.  

When you lift the cover of the exam booklet, instead of neatly printed pages you see **floating glyphs** drifting upward, each one a tiny, shimmering letter that changes color the moment you glance at it. The first question hovers over the blank space, its tip blinking amber:  

> **“Name the feeling you felt the instant you realized you were dreaming.”**  

A second bubble drifts beside it, glowing violet:  

> **“If you could trade one skill for another, which pair would you choose?”**  

A third, silver‑blue sphere appears, pulsing gently:  

> **“List three things you have forgotten about yourself.”**  

Each question seems to anticipate a memory you’ve buried beneath layers of routine. The ink on the page isn’t black or blue; it’s a liquid that shifts hue whenever you stare too long, as if the exam itself is alive and learning from you.  

You feel a sudden tug at the edges of your vision—a whisper that sounds like your own voice, calm and knowing: *“These aren’t ordinary tests. They’re maps of the currents you haven’t yet named.”*  

Below the questions, a thin line of light stretches across the desk, forming a faint outline of a staircase that spirals upward, disappearing into a ceiling painted with swirling galaxies. The air grows cooler, and the scent of rain on pavement mixes with the metallic tang of anticipation.  

You try to answer the first one, and as you begin to speak, the amber glyph dissolves into a cascade of golden sparks that land on the floor, arranging themselves into a small, intricate pattern resembling a compass rose. A smile tugs at the corner of your mouth, though you can’t quite place why.  

The rest of the exam unfolds like a dreamscape:  

- **“Describe the sound of your own heartbeat when you close your eyes.”** – The answer emerges not as a rhythm but as a chorus of distant bells, each toll matching the beat of your imagined pulse.  
- **“Predict the exact date of my next heart attack.”** – A single number flashes in neon green, followed by a cascade of white snowflakes that dissolve into the air.  
- **“Which fictional character would you most likely become if you woke up tomorrow?”** – The question morphs into a portrait of a figure you recognize only in fragments: a tall, lanky silhouette with a scar across the left cheek, wearing a coat that never fits right.  

As you scribble your responses, the pen begins to bleed a dark ink that writes itself, forming sentences that loop back to earlier prompts, creating a feedback circle where every answer reinforces the next. The exam’s final page contains a single, unblinking question:  

> **“In this moment, do you feel the thread that ties your choices together—your past, your present, and the future you’re trying to build?”**  

You pause, the room humming louder now, the walls breathing in sync with your thoughts. The ink on the page starts to glow, and a soft voice—almost like a lullaby—whispers, “You are already the author of those paths. Choose wisely, or let the possibilities dissolve.”  

With that, the exam closes, leaving behind only the lingering echo of the questions and the faint imprint of your answers. You stand, feeling the weight of the decision settling on your shoulders, aware that the course you’re taking today holds the key to the career you’ll eventually walk through the doors of. The dream fades, but the questions remain, waiting for the next breath you take in the quiet after the exam.
