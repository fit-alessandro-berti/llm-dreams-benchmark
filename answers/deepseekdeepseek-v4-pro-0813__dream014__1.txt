You are feeling lost and alone in a bustling city. Suddenly, a group of kind strangers offers to help you find your way, and you realize they are not leading you to a street or station but to a small courtyard where a fountain is made of starlight. Each stranger hands you a smooth stone, and when you hold them, you remember every path you have ever walked. The city noise fades into warm static. You walk with them through streets that rearrange themselves gently, and by the time you reach the edge of the city, dawn is rising in colors that have no name. You turn to thank them, but they have become doorways, trees, morning pigeons. You step through the last gate and wake, feeling found.
