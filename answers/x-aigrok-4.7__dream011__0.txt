You notice the questions are printed in a language that keeps rearranging itself every time you blink—familiar words sliding into nonsense, then snapping back just long enough for you to almost understand. The clock on the wall has no hands, only a soft ticking that speeds up whenever you look at it. Your pencil feels warm, almost alive, and when you try to write your name the letters crawl off the page and pool at the edge of the desk like ink trying to escape. Somewhere behind you a door opens and closes without anyone walking through it, and you realize the real test isn’t on the paper at all.
