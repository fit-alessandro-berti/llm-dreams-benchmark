One of them, a woman with a warm smile and a worn map, gently takes your arm and asks, *“Where did you last remember seeing your hotel?”* You hesitate, then point to a small café corner near a plaza you passed hours ago. She nods, and without a word, steps back and lets you walk ahead. Her companion quietly taps another street on the map, then looks at you with a reassuring shrug.

As you follow the directions, the city’s noise begins to soften. The honking, the crowd, the unfamiliar signs—all recede into a familiar rhythm. You find the café, then the street, then the landmark you were chasing. When you finally turn the corner and see the familiar glow of your building’s lobby, you feel a deep, quiet breath. You’re not lost anymore. You’re not alone, either. And as you step inside, the stranger who helped you nods from the sidewalk, ready to guide you anywhere you need.
