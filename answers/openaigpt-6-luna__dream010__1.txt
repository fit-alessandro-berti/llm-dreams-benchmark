As the competition progresses, you face various challenges: crossing a bridge that rearranges itself beneath your feet, solving riddles whispered by a flock of crows, and giving a speech to an audience whose faces keep changing into people you know.

At first, you try to win by guessing what the judges want. But the final challenge is different. You’re led into a quiet room with a single desk, a blank page, and a window overlooking a place you’ve always wanted to work. A voice asks, “What would you do if nobody were judging?”

You stop trying to impress anyone. You sit down and begin.

When you finish, the judges are gone. The blank page has become an offer letter with your name on it. You step through the window—not out of the room, but straight into your new workplace.

Someone hands you a warm cup of coffee and says, “We’ve been waiting for you.”

Then your alarm goes off.
