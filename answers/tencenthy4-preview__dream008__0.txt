...and you realize the only way to meet an impossible deadline is to stop obeying time.

The office clock melts into a paper bird and flutters around the fluorescent lights. Your boss taps their clipboard impatiently, but the clipboard keeps turning into a slice of cake. You open your laptop, and instead of a blank screen, a small ocean appears. The finished project floats to the surface, dripping with stars.

You gather it into a folder. The deadline, which had been snarling in the corner, shrinks into a sleepy cat and curls at your feet.

Your boss leans over your shoulder and nods, impressed, though neither of you can remember what the project was. They stamp it **APPROVED** with a stamp made of sunrise.

Then the walls fold themselves into a paper airplane. You climb inside and fly out the window, just as your alarm clock begins to ring.
