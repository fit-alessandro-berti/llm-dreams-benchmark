…search for the last place on Earth where rain still falls.

You pack a cracked compass, two cans of beans, and a photograph whose faces you can’t quite remember. At dawn, you leave the shelter and follow a line of silver weeds across the dead highway. The compass spins whenever you pass a ruined town, but at night it points toward a faint blue light on the horizon.

After days of walking, you find a greenhouse standing alone in a valley. Its glass is unbroken. Inside, green vines climb the walls, and warm rain patters against the roof.

Then you hear someone laugh.

A child is crouched beside a little pool, watching the rain make ripples. They look up at you and hold out a seed.

You take it—and wake just as the first drop touches your hand.
