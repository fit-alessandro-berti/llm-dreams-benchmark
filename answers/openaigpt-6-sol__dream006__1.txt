The words scatter across the stage like pale leaves. You look down at the one page that remains. It is blank.

So you tell them about the birds. As you speak, small patches of sky appear in the ceiling. People point them out to one another, smiling. When you finish, there is no applause—just the sound of everyone opening their windows at once.
