...begin to notice that the walls are listening.

Not in a menacing way. They lean in, the way a friend does when you're about to say something important. The first puzzles were simple: sliding tiles, a lock that opened when you hummed the right note. But here, deeper in, the puzzles have started to change shape as you look at them.

You come to a door with no handle, only a grid of symbols carved into the stone. You recognize them, though you've never seen them before. One is the smell of rain on warm pavement. Another is the particular silence of a house after everyone has gone to sleep. You realize the puzzle isn't asking you to arrange them in order. It's asking which one is missing.

You press your palm against the empty space in the grid, and the answer comes the way a forgotten word finally surfaces. It's a memory of being small, lying on a floor somewhere, watching dust drift through a beam of light and feeling no need to be anywhere else. The stone warms beneath your hand, and the door dissolves like fog.

Beyond it, the corridors are no longer stone. The walls are made of bookshelves, then of water held impossibly upright, then of night sky with slow constellations rearranging themselves into arrows. You follow them. The puzzles here don't resist you anymore. Each one opens as you approach, as if the maze has decided you've already proven whatever it needed proven.

At the center, there is no treasure and no monster. There is only a small round room with a mirror in it, and in the mirror is the maze itself, seen from above. You understand, with the calm certainty of dreams, that its winding paths trace the shape of your own thinking, and that every dead end was a question you hadn't finished asking.

You reach out to touch the glass. The surface ripples, and somewhere far above, light begins to pour in, soft and gold, like morning...
