Here's one way your dream could complete:

You are in the middle of a heated argument with a close friend. The disagreement is about something important to both of you, and you decide to **stop talking mid-sentence.**

Just stop. Not storm off, not give in — just pause.

In the dream, the silence is suddenly loud. The room you're in gets bigger, or quieter, or stranger — the walls soften a little, the light shifts. You can hear your own heart pounding, and you can actually *see* your friend for the first time in the dream, not as an opponent, but as someone tired and hurt, just like you.

And you decide to ask, instead of tell: "What part of this are you most afraid of losing?"

Your friend blinks, surprised. The anger drains out of their shoulders. They answer, and as they talk, the heavy thing you've both been carrying — that important thing you were fighting *about* — turns into something you can both hold, side by side, instead of pulling between you.

You don't wake up with the problem perfectly solved. You wake up with the feeling of relief that you chose to listen instead of win.
