As they guide you through the labyrinth of unfamiliar streets, dream logic gently takes hold. The towering steel skyscrapers begin to bend and unfold like giant, luminous origami, their glass windows glowing with the warm amber light of paper lanterns. The roar of honking cars and rushing footsteps dissolves, replaced by the quiet, rhythmic melody of distant rain and soft chimes.

The strangers don’t ask for an address; they speak without moving their lips, communicating entirely through a shared sense of comfort and understanding. Their faces are ever-shifting, carrying the familiar features of old friends, forgotten childhood memories, and kind smiles you haven't seen in years. 

They lead you around a sharp corner where the asphalt turns into cool, velvet moss beneath your feet. Ahead, in the middle of a quiet cobblestone courtyard, stands an ornate wooden door floating upright on its own, framed in soft morning mist. 

One of the guides turns to you, gently placing a silver key in your palm. "You were never truly lost," they say, "you just forgot that all these paths lead back to you."

When you turn the key in the lock and push the door open, a rush of golden sunlight and the scent of fresh air washes over you. You turn back to thank them, but the strangers have already begun to scatter like dandelion seeds into the breeze, leaving you with a profound sense of peace as you step through the threshold and begin to wake.
