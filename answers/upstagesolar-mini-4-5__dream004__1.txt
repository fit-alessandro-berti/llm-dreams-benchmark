You press two fingers to the surface, and a color blooms outward—first a soft apricot, then a violet so deep it feels like velvet. You draw a slow, unthinking line, and the line becomes a staircase that spirals up into the sky, though the sky here is not blue but a restless teal that shifts every few seconds.

You climb the staircase as it turns beneath you, each step a different texture—silk, then static, then a cool, humming glass. At the top, the staircase opens into a room made entirely of doors, and every door is slightly ajar, and behind every door is a version of you, holding a piece of the same canvas. One of them smiles and offers you a paintbrush made of woven light. You take it. The canvas beneath your feet begins to fill—not with color, but with a shape: a door.

You step through, and you are back in your own room, the canvas on the wall, exactly as you left it—blank, but now humming faintly in your presence. And you realize you've been awake for some time, and the dream was only the canvas waking up.
