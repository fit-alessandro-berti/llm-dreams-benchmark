...the air goes suddenly still.

The birds stop singing all at once. The light filtering through the leaves turns a strange, deep green, then grey, as clouds swallow the sky faster than clouds should be able to move. A low rumble rolls through the ground beneath your feet — not just thunder, but something you feel in your chest.

The wind arrives next, smelling of rain and electricity and wet earth. The tall trees around you begin to sway and whisper, their branches reaching toward each other like they're holding hands against the storm.

Then the rain starts, but in your dream, it doesn't fall down.

It falls *up*.

Silver droplets rise from the forest floor, from the ferns and the moss and the little stream beside the path, floating up past your face toward the dark sky. You hold out your hand and a raindrop lands in your palm and stays there, warm and glowing faintly, like a tiny captured star.

You should be frightened, but you're not. You laugh.

The storm grows wilder — wind swirling leaves into spirals, thunder cracking the sky open with veins of purple lightning — and you run, not away from it, but through it. Your feet barely touch the ground. With each step you lift a little higher, until you're running on gusts of wind between the treetops.

Below you, the forest is bending but not breaking. Above you, the storm clouds part just enough to reveal a sky that isn't day or night, but filled with slow-moving constellations.

You realize, with that quiet certainty you only get in dreams, that the storm was never coming to destroy the forest.

It was coming to wake it up.

Every tree you pass bursts into bloom as the upward rain touches it — white, gold, and impossible blue flowers opening in seconds, releasing a scent so sweet it makes you dizzy with happiness.

You float higher, calm at the very center of the storm, watching the whole shining forest dance below you.

And just as the thunder softens into something that sounds almost like a heartbeat, you feel yourself gently drifting down... down... into warmth, and quiet, and morning light behind your eyelids.
