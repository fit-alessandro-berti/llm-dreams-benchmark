You start to panic, your shoes sinking slightly into the air as the street stretches out without end, the skyline a rolling wave of glass and shadow that shifts each time you glance back. Neon signs flicker in languages you almost remember, and the pavement ripples like water beneath each step, urging you forward without ever quite revealing a destination. You try to ask a stranger for directions, but their form dissolves into mist the moment they speak, leaving only a single phrase that hangs in the air: “The meeting is wherever you stop running.” You pause, breath misting in the cold artificial wind, and realize the city isn’t a maze at all—it’s a map of something you’ve already forgotten, and the hour isn’t running out; it’s just waiting for you to arrive exactly where you need to be.
