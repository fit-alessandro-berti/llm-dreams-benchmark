You are in a maze filled with complex puzzles. Each puzzle requires intense concentration and logical thinking to solve, and as you move deeper into the maze, you notice the walls themselves begin to rearrange with every correct answer, folding space like origami so that corridors you just left now lead somewhere entirely new. The puzzles shift from logic grids and riddles into living ones: a chessboard whose pieces argue their own moves, a river of numbers that must be forded by proving a theorem on the spot, a door that only opens when you admit a contradiction you’ve been carrying. Time stretches and compresses; you solve for hours that last seconds. At the very center you find not an exit but a single, perfectly still puzzle: a blank page that waits for you to write the rule that governs the entire maze. The moment you understand it is a dream, the maze dissolves into morning light, the last equation still glowing faintly behind your eyes.
