...and you do the one thing no one expects.

You laugh.

Not a wild laugh, not a frightened one — a deep, steady, captain's laugh that cuts through the wind sharper than any order you've ever given. Your crew freezes at their ropes and oars, rain plastering their hair to their faces, staring at you as if you've lost your mind to the sea.

You plant your boots wide on the slick deck as another wave tilts the world sideways, grab the rail, and shout into the storm:

"She's not trying to sink us! She's testing if we're worthy of her!"

You point past the bow, where the clouds are blackest and the lightning is splitting the sky open.

"There! We don't run from it. We sail *through* it!"

Your first mate's eyes widen. "Captain, that's the heart of it!"

"Aye!" you roar, and now you're grinning, salt and rain in your teeth. "And hearts are made to be passed through, not feared! All hands — trim the sails! Tie yourselves to what you love and HOLD ON!"

Something in your voice — not fear, not bravado, but absolute certainty — moves them. They scramble, suddenly alive again. Hands that were shaking with cold now pull with purpose. Someone starts singing an old shanty, off-key and too loud, and one by one the others join in, singing directly back at the thunder.

You take the wheel yourself.

The sea rises up like a living wall in front of you, green-black and towering, and for a moment at its crest you can see the whole dream-sea at once: islands made of sleeping whales, stars swimming like fish beneath the surface, a lighthouse in the far distance whose light is not white, but warm gold, and turning — turning toward you.

You steer straight for that light.

The wave breaks over you. The world goes silent and dark and full of bubbles. Your crew is gone. The ship is gone. You're floating, weightless, in deep blue quiet.

You aren't drowning. You're breathing.

And from far above you, through the water, you hear your own crew singing, and you realize the storm was never above you at all.

You kick upward, toward the singing, toward the light —
