**The Dream Part 2 (The Shift):**

Suddenly, the stage lighting changes from harsh white to a warm, golden hue. The audience members aren't just faces anymore; you see silhouettes of hands raised in applause before you even reach the podium. You complete the sentence you started with, your voice growing louder and more resonant with each word, not to fill a void, but to fill a room full of people who are ready to listen.

You finish your speech, and the room doesn't just go quiet—it fills with a rolling, genuine applause that seems to vibrate through the floor in time with your heartbeat. You turn to the woman with the sign, and she smiles, waving. She didn't need a perfect performance; she needed your truth.

***

**Dream Part 3 (The Resolution):**

You walk off the stage, and the lights dim back to normal. You notice a spot where you left your notebook open on the stage edge, the pages filled with handwriting you don't remember writing. You flip through it, seeing your own words, but different ones—words about courage and being heard. You realize this wasn't just a dream about performing; it was a dream about surrendering the need to be perfect to the need to be present.

You wake up with a slight tightness in your chest, but also a quiet, lingering warmth, as if the dream had been rehearsing for you a kind of confidence you're still trying to find in your waking life.
