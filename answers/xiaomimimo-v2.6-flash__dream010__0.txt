Then comes the moment I've been dreading — a panel of judges who will test my courage. They ask me to speak my truth without holding back, to share a story of failure that shaped who I am. My voice trembles at first, but as I speak, I feel something shift. The room grows quiet. One by one, the judges nod, moved by what they hear.

The final challenge is the hardest — a mirror. I'm led to a dark room where I must confront my deepest doubts: *Am I really good enough? What if I'm not who I think I am?* The reflection stares back, and for a long moment, I want to turn away. Instead, I reach out and place my hand against the glass. "You're here," I whisper. "That's enough."

The room dissolves into light.

When it opens again, I'm standing before the judges. The eldest leans forward with a warm smile. "We've been watching you not just for your skills, but for your heart." She extends her hand. "Welcome to your dream job."

I wake up with a smile still on my face — and the faint, lingering sense that maybe, just maybe, the dream wasn't entirely a dream at all.
