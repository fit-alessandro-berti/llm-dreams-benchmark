You come across a blank canvas and a single brush floating in mid-air, waiting for you.

The canvas is impossibly large, standing alone in the middle of this shifting world without an easel, without a wall to hold it up. It just hovers, perfectly still, while everything around it swirls and melts and reforms - the sky rippling from violet to gold, the ground folding itself into hills and then into waves.

As you step closer, you realize the brush isn't waiting for your hand. It's pulsing faintly, in rhythm with your breathing.

You reach out and take it. The moment your fingers touch it, the chaotic colors around you pause, as if the whole world is holding its breath to see what you'll do.

You make a single stroke across the blank canvas.

Where the brush touches, color doesn't just appear - it *sings*. A deep blue blooms and suddenly the sky above you steadies into that same blue. You paint a soft, yellow curve, and a warm sun rises in the distance, casting a steady light for the first time.

You understand then - you're not just painting on the canvas. You're painting the world itself.

With each stroke, the shifting, unstable landscape becomes yours. You paint a winding path, and it appears beneath your feet. You paint a tall, gentle tree with silver leaves, and you can hear it rustling beside you. You paint a door in the air, slightly ajar, with soft light pouring from it.

You feel calm, completely in control yet completely free, as you realize you can paint anything you can imagine - and you have all the time in the dream to do it.
