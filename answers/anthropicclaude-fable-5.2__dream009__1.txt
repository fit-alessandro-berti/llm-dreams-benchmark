...find the clockmaker.

You don't know how you know this, only that the knowledge is already in you, the way things are in dreams. Cobblestones press cold through your thin shoes, and the air smells of woodsmoke, horses, and baking bread. A woman carrying a basket of eels nods at you as though she's seen you every morning of her life.

Your hand keeps drifting to your pocket, reaching for a phone that isn't there. Instead, your fingers close around a small brass key, warm as if someone had just been holding it.

The market square unfolds around you. Merchants shout in a language you shouldn't understand but somehow do. A boy leads a goat past a fountain where the water seems to flow *upward*, though no one else finds this strange. Above the rooftops stands a great clock tower, its hands frozen at 11:58.

"It's been stopped since you left," says an old man at your elbow. He wears spectacles with too many lenses. "We've been waiting. Nobody here can grow older until it's wound again."

You want to tell him you've never been here before, but the words come out as, "I'm sorry I took so long."

The tower door is tiny, no higher than your knee, and yet you step through it without stooping. Inside, a staircase spirals past gears the size of mill wheels, all still and furred with dust. Moonlight pours through the clock face, though it was morning a moment ago.

At the top, you find the keyhole, set into the heart of the mechanism. Your key fits perfectly. As you begin to turn it, you hear the whole town below holding its breath, and you suddenly understand that when the clock strikes twelve, you'll wake up.

Your hand pauses.

Through the glass of the clock face, you can see the baker, the eel woman, and the boy with his goat, all looking up at you. Not pleading, just waiting.

You turn the key.

The gears shudder to life. The first chime rolls out over the rooftops like a wave, and the last thing you see before the dream dissolves is the old man in the square, raising his hand in farewell as his beard finally begins to turn white.
