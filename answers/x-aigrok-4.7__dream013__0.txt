You find the puzzles growing stranger: corridors of mirrored logic, doors that open only when you stop trying to force them, and riddles whose answers are the questions you haven’t asked yet. The deeper you go, the quieter the maze becomes, until the only sound left is your own thinking—and even that starts to feel like part of the design. At the center there is no prize, only a still pool that reflects not your face but the path you took to arrive, every wrong turn included, and the realization that the maze was never trying to trap you. It was teaching you how to leave.
