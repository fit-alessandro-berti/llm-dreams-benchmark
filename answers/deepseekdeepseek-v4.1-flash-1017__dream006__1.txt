...begin to speak, and the first sentence comes out not as words but as a ribbon of blue light drifting over the audience. No one looks surprised. They nod as if this is exactly what they came for.

You say the next part, and the stage lifts gently off the floor, carrying you above the crowd. Their faces become hundreds of small moons, each reflecting a different memory of you. You realize you are not giving a speech anymore—you are telling them the shape of your own dream.

When you reach the final sentence, the auditorium folds itself into a paper boat. You sail it across a dark, calm ocean, and just before waking you hear your own voice from far away:

“Thank you for listening.”

Then the boat opens into morning.
