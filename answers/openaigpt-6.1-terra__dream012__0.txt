The waves rise higher than the masts, and in each one you glimpse a different sky: sunrise, starlight, a pale green moon. Your crew falls silent. Beneath the thunder, you hear a bell ringing from somewhere deep below the ship.

“Follow it,” you say.

The helmsman turns the wheel. The ship plunges into the tallest wave—and sails through it as though through a curtain.

The sea is suddenly calm. Above you stretches a ceiling of water, lit by drifting stars. Ahead, a lighthouse shines from the back of an enormous sleeping whale. Its beam passes over your crew, and each sailor smiles as though remembering something they had lost.

When it reaches you, the bell stops.

You know, without knowing how, that you are home.
