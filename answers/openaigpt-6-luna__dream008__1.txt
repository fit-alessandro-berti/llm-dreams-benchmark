You are at your workplace, and suddenly, you are given a project with an impossible deadline. Your boss is waiting for you to start, and you can’t find your desk.

The office has changed while you weren’t looking. The hallways stretch on like airport runways, and every door opens into another meeting room. Your coworkers hurry past carrying enormous stacks of paper, but when you look closely, every page is blank.

Your boss points to a clock on the wall. Its hands are spinning backward.

You sit down at the first desk you find and open your laptop. A new document appears, already filled with words—but they’re drifting off the screen like leaves on water. You reach out to catch them, and the words gather beneath your fingers, arranging themselves into a little paper boat.

The boat sails across the desk, over the edge, and into a puddle that wasn’t there a moment ago. You follow it. The puddle widens into a quiet lake, and the office lights shimmer on its surface like stars.

Behind you, your boss calls your name. Ahead, the paper boat turns toward a distant shore. You step into the water—and just as the deadline chimes, the whole office rises gently into the sky.
