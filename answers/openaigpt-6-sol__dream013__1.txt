You search for a hidden switch. You count the stones in the wall and trace every crack. Nothing moves.

At last, exhausted, you sit down. For the first time since entering the maze, you stop trying to solve it. You notice a cool breeze against your face. It slips through the wall beside the door.

You follow it and find a narrow opening, just wide enough to pass through. Before you step inside, you look back at the glowing question. Then you leave the door behind.
