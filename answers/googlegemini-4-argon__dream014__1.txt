The city grids have completely vanished now, opening up to a quiet, starlit ocean where the water laps gently against the cobblestone street. Sitting at the edge of the water is the exact place you were looking for: a small, glowing wooden door standing completely on its own, radiating the unmistakable warmth of home. 

You step toward the door, the heavy knot of loneliness in your chest untying itself completely. Just as your fingers brush the brass doorknob, a wave of profound peace washes over you, and the world gently fades into the soft light of morning.
