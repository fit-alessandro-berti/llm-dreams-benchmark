You are participating in a competition where the grand prize is your dream job. As the competition progresses, you face various challenges and...

---

...your heart hammers as the announcer's voice echoes across the neon-lit arena. You're one of the final three contestants remaining.

**Round 7: "The Mind Maze"**

You step into a darkened room where holographic puzzles float in midair — each one a real-world problem from the actual job you've been chasing. Your fingers tremble as you solve them one by one, watching the other two contestants through the glass walls. One of them stumbles. Their puzzle collapses into dust. Only you and a rival named Elara remain.

You look at her. She looks at you. The tension is electric.

**Round 8: "The Presentation"**

Everything goes white. Suddenly you're standing before a panel of seven judges — people who look impossibly important. You have ninety seconds to pitch an idea that could change the world. Your mind races. You close your eyes, and suddenly the words pour out of you like water — passionate, precise, alive. When you open your eyes, the judges are leaning forward.

Then Elara steps up and does something brilliant too.

**Round 9: "The Impossible Question"**

The lights dim. The head judge looks at both of you and asks:

*"If you could only accomplish one thing in this role — and never another — what would it be, and would it be worth it?"*

Your throat goes dry. The silence stretches. Elara answers first, confidently. But something in her answer rings hollow. Then it's your turn. You speak from somewhere deep — from every late night, every dream you whispered to no one, every moment you imagined this exact second. You don't just answer the question. You make the judges *feel* it.

**The Final Moment**

The announcer returns. The crowd — which you didn't even notice — erupts into a roar. A spotlight finds you... then jumps to Elara... then back to you...

*"The winner of the competition is..."*

The word hangs in the air like a held breath. Time slows to a crawl. You see your reflection in the polished floor, and you're smiling — not because you know you'll win, but because you realized somewhere along the way that **the competition itself** already gave you something no prize ever could.

The proof that you're ready.

*"…YOU!"*

The word crashes over you like a wave. Confetti explodes from everywhere. Elara grabs your hand and shakes it with genuine warmth. You walk toward the podium, toward the contract, toward every dream you've ever had folded neatly into a single golden envelope.

And just as your fingers touch it—

You wake up.

But you're still smiling. And you realize the dream didn't end. It just showed you where to begin.
