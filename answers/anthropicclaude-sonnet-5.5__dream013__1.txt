...as you move deeper into the maze, you notice the walls begin to change. At first the stone is cold and gray, but now faint lines of silver light trace across it, rearranging themselves whenever you look away, as if the maze is thinking alongside you.

**The first puzzle** is a door with no handle, only three brass dials etched with constellations. You turn them slowly, realizing the stars are not in the sky's arrangement but in the order of your own footsteps since you entered. The door sighs open.

**The second puzzle** is a room full of clocks, each running at a different speed. A whispering voice says, *"Only one of them is telling the truth."* You watch for a long time. The liar is easy to spot once you stop checking the time and start checking *yourself*: only one clock ticks in rhythm with your heartbeat. You touch it, and the rest fall silent.

**The third puzzle** is the hardest. A long corridor of mirrors shows a different version of you in each one. One is older, one is a child, one is someone you might have been. Each offers you a key, and each insists the others are fakes. Your head aches as you weigh their arguments, until you notice that every reflection is holding the key in its *right* hand, except one. Its key is in the left, the way a true reflection would hold it. You take that key, and the other mirrors dissolve into mist.

The key fits a small iron gate at the end of the corridor. Beyond it, the maze opens into a circular garden under a sky full of slowly turning gears instead of stars. In the center is a pedestal with a single sheet of paper. You step closer and read:

*"Puzzle Four: Why did you keep going?"*

You realize there's no trick to it, no pattern, no hidden logic. You think about the locked doors, the lying clocks, the mirrors. You weren't chasing a prize, because you never knew there was one. You just couldn't leave a question unanswered.

You speak the answer aloud: *"Because I wanted to understand."*

The gears overhead stop. The paper turns to light and drifts into your hands, and the garden walls fall away like petals. Behind them is not an exit but a thousand new paths, each glowing faintly, each a new maze waiting.

You smile, feeling the dream begin to thin around the edges, and choose the nearest path just as the morning starts to pull you awake...
