...each one speaking in half-finished sentences, their faces shifting like reflections in disturbed water. Someone holds a glass of light instead of wine, another wears a name tag with the symbol for “almost,” and the hallway you turn into becomes shorter every time you glance at it. When you finally spot the person you know, they wave—but the hand belongs to someone else, and the crowd gently begins to applaud you for being the one who was lost.
