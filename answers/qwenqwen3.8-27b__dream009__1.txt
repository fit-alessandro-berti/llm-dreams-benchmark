

…find the river before the moon rises.

The air smells of woodsmoke and wet stone, and every shadow has the soft, uncertain edges of a dream. You walk through fields that should be empty, yet they are full of footsteps that do not match yours. Behind you, the strange village stirs, its lanterns glowing like trapped fireflies. Ahead, the river bends around a hill, and in its reflection you do not see yourself—you see someone older, wearing clothes you have never owned, holding a key you have never found.

The dream tells you, without words, that if you reach the water before the moon crests the hill, you will remember which era you were truly from. If you do not, you will belong here so completely that you will forget how to return.
