At a quiet corner, they stop beside a small door set into a brick wall. “This is where we leave you,” one says, placing the lantern in your hands.

You open the door and find a garden filled with familiar voices. When you turn to thank the strangers, the city has grown still, and they are gone. But the lantern is warm, and for the first time, you know you aren’t lost.
