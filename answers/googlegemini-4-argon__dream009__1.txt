You look down at your hands. In place of your phone, you are holding a smooth, brass astrolabe that hums with a faint warmth, its rings spinning slowly on their own. Around you, a sprawling marketplace is coming to life. The air is thick with the scent of woodsmoke, crushed rosemary, and damp cobblestone. Stalls draped in vibrant, hand-woven tapestries line the narrow street, and merchants in heavy wool cloaks are bartering with coins made of iridescent glass. Above the timber-framed rooftops, massive stone aqueducts arch across a violet sky, carrying glowing, crystalline water toward the center of the city.

You try to ask a passing baker for directions, but when you open your mouth, no words come out—only the chime of a distant bell. The baker doesn't seem to find this strange. He simply smiles, points a flour-dusted finger toward a towering spire at the edge of the city, and hands you a warm, braided loaf of bread that tastes faintly of starlight. 

As you walk toward the spire, the dream-logic shifts the world around you. Steps that should take minutes take seconds; alleys twist like origami, folding behind you so you cannot turn back. Shadows stretch impossibly long across the stones, whispering in a language made of rustling leaves. 

Finally, you reach the base of the spire. There is no door, only an archway of weeping willow branches. Stepping through, you find yourself in a cavernous circular room. Above you, suspended by thousands of thick silk threads, are the massive wooden gears of a clock the size of a cathedral. They are completely frozen. 

In the center of the room stands a figure draped in a cloak of stitched-together parchment, covered in fading ink. The Keeper. They turn to you, their face hidden behind a polished silver mask. 

Without speaking, the Keeper holds out an open palm. You look down at the brass astrolabe in your hand, realizing it isn't a compass at all—it is the missing heart of the clock. You step forward to place it in the Keeper's hand, knowing that the moment the gears begin to turn, this world will vanish, and you will wake up. 

The brass touches the silver of their glove. A deafening *tick* echoes through the chamber, the floor dissolves into a sea of clouds, and you begin to fall upward into the waking world...
