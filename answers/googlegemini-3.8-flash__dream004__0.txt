...a single, dry brush resting on a ledge of solidified mist. 

The moment your fingers close around the handle, the frantic morphing of the landscape begins to hold its breath. The sky, which had been folding in on itself like origami ribbons of cerulean and rust, slows to a gentle, suspended drift. You realize you aren’t meant to dip the brush into paint; the chaotic world itself is your palette.

You raise the brush toward the canvas, and without even touching it, the surrounding shapes respond. A stroke through the air draws a ribbon of melting violet from the horizon, pulling it onto the taut linen where it pools into a calm, steady lake. A tap against the empty space beside you captures a drifting fractal sun, pinning it in place as a radiant, warm amber glow. With each movement of your hand, the restless turbulence of the dream world funnels into the frame, finding order, quiet, and balance. 

Where the canvas absorbs the shifting forms, the air around you grows peaceful and clear. 

Just as you complete the final stroke—creating an anchor of perfect stillness in an ocean of flux—the surface of the canvas blinks. It ripples like water, turning translucent. Beyond the paint, you see a faint, familiar room: the soft outline of your own bed, the steady rhythm of your breathing, and the first pale sliver of morning light creeping through a window. 

The brush dissolves into dust of warm light between your fingers. Smiling, you lean forward and step through the frame, falling softly out of the dream and into the quiet embrace of waking reality.
