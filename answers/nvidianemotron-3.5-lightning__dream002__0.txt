The sky above you instantly deepens from soft blue to a bruised violet, and a low, resonant thunder rolls through the valley like a warning. The air grows cold and electric, smelling of wet earth, ozone, and pine sap as the wind tears through the canopy, sending cascades of leaves swirling around you. You try to quicken your pace, but the forest itself seems to respond—the trees lean inward, their branches weaving together above you, forming a vaulting dome of storm-darkened green. As the first fat raindrops begin to fall, striking the moss with soft, rhythmic taps, you realize the peaceful walk has become a journey into the heart of the storm, where the sky and earth meet in a single, roaring breath.
