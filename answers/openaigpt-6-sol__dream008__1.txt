You reach for your keyboard, but every key is labeled *tomorrow*.

Your boss taps their watch. Its hands are spinning backward. “Well?” they ask.

You open the project brief. It contains a single instruction: **Build a door where there isn’t one.** The deadline was yesterday.

All around you, your coworkers type faster and faster. Their screens fill with pictures of doors: red doors, glass doors, doors floating in the middle of fields. None of them will open.

You look at the blank wall beside your desk. Then you take your coffee cup and draw a rectangle on it with the last of the coffee. A handle appears under your fingers.

Your boss stops tapping the watch.

“What’s on the other side?” they ask.

“I don’t know,” you say, and pull.

Beyond the door is your workplace again, empty and quiet, with sunlight falling across every desk. The clocks have no hands. For the first time all morning, no one is waiting for you to start.
