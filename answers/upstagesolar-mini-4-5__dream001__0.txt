...You start to panic, but then notice something odd. The city isn't just unfamiliar—it's *slightly wrong* in a way you can't name. The street signs are written in a language that looks like every language you've ever studied, stacked on top of each other. The buildings are all the same height, but their windows are all facing different directions, and each window shows a different room.

You check your phone. The clock says 11:47, but the screen is a mirror instead. In it, you see yourself—but you're wearing different clothes, and you're already walking toward a meeting you haven't thought about yet. You decide to follow.

The streets rearrange themselves as you walk. A bakery becomes a courtyard. A lamppost becomes a piano. You hum a tune you don't recognize, and the piano answers. Every time you stop to think, the city disappears a little more—so you keep moving.

Eventually you find the meeting: a circular room with no doors, just a soft glow. Inside, everyone is already seated, including your future self. Your future self looks up, nods, and hands you a map. The map has one word written on it: "Here."

You realize the meeting is about the city itself. Not to change it—but to understand it. And for the first time in the dream, you feel calm. You look down at your hands. They're translucent. The city is starting to look translucent too.

Then someone in the circle says, "You're the only one who's ever asked the right question." And you wake up, already knowing where you're supposed to be tomorrow.
