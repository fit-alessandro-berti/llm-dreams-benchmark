Despite the hardships, you decide to build a small refuge in the shell of an abandoned library. You drag scavenged solar panels onto the roof, plant seeds in cracked teacups along the windowsills, and trade old stories for clean water. Each night you light a single lamp and leave the door unlocked—a quiet signal that not everything has ended. One morning, strangers arrive: a mechanic with a broken arm, a child carrying three tomato seeds, and a dog with a torn ear. You share what little you have, and together you start repairing the roof. The world outside is still dangerous, but inside, for the first time in years, the word “we” feels less like a memory and more like a plan. Then the dream shifts, and you wake with dirt under your fingernails.
