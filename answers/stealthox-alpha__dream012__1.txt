...you grip the wheel with both hands, salt spray stinging your face as thunder rolls across the sky like the growl of some enormous beast.

"Reef the sails!" you shout over the howling wind. "Secure everything! We ride this out together!"

Your crew scrambles across the pitching deck—old hands and new ones alike, their faces pale but determined. The first mate, a weathered woman with a scar across her cheek, fights her way to your side.

"Captain, the barometer's dropping fast. This is no ordinary squall."

She's right. The waves have grown mountainous, black walls of water that lift the ship skyward before plunging it into the valleys between. And yet—something feels strange. The lightning, when it flashes, seems to hang in the air a moment too long. The rain falls upward at times. You glance down and realize you can't remember boarding this ship, can't remember its name, though it's painted right there on the bow in letters that seem to shift when you look away.

"Hold course!" you call out anyway, because that's what captains do.

Then, through a gap in the storm clouds, you see it: a light on the horizon. Warm. Golden. Steady as a heartbeat while everything around it churns. The crew falls silent, all eyes drawn to it, and somehow you know—without knowing how—that reaching it means waking up.

You turn the wheel toward the light.

The sea parts like a curtain...

And then what happens next? Do you sail through, or does the dream pull you somewhere stranger?
