You are given a mysterious locked box with no key in sight. As you examine it closely, you notice intricate patterns and symbols that seem to shift when you’re not looking directly at them. The metal is warm, almost alive, and faint music hums from within, like someone is singing in a language you used to know. When you press your palm against the lid, the patterns glow blue, and the box opens—not into the dream, but into another dream inside the dream.

Inside is a tiny mirror, and in it, you see yourself asleep in bed, smiling. Then the reflection reaches through the glass, hands you a key made of moonlight, and whispers, “Don’t wake up yet.”
