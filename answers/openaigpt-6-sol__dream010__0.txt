As the competition progresses, you face various challenges and begin to notice that each one resembles a job you once imagined having. In one room, you direct a play with actors who know their lines but refuse to speak. In another, you build a bridge from paper while a river rises beneath your feet.

By the final round, you can no longer remember what your dream job is.

The judges lead you to a door with your name on it. Behind it is a small, sunlit room. A desk waits by the window, holding a blank sheet of paper. You ask what you’re supposed to do.

“Whatever you came here to do,” one judge says.

You sit down. Outside, the river runs quietly past the building. For the first time all day, no clock is ticking.
