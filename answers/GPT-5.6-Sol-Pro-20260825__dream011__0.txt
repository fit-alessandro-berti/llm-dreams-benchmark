**What would you choose if you were not afraid?**
**Whose expectations are you trying to satisfy?**
**What kind of life would feel like your own?**

Around you, the other students write furiously, but every time you look at their papers, the pages are blank. The clock above the door spins backward, and the examiner quietly says, “There is no correct answer, but you must answer honestly.”

Your hands tremble. Then you begin to write—not about grades, titles, or success, but about the work that makes you curious, the people you want to help, and the person you hope to become.

As soon as you finish the final sentence, the examination hall dissolves. The desks become stepping-stones across a wide river, each leading toward a different future. You realize the exam was never meant to determine your career. It was meant to reveal which path you would choose when no one else could choose for you.

You step onto the first stone, and although you cannot see where the path ends, it feels strangely familiar—like a road you have been searching for all along.
