The strangers smile, but their features blur into the shapes of people you’ve loved and long forgotten, and as they fade into the pearled mist, the ground beneath you turns to polished stone that reflects a sky you haven’t seen since childhood.

In your open palm rests a single brass key, warm as a held breath. You don’t know which door it opens, but as you close your fingers around it, the hum of the city returns, now sounding less like chaos and more like an answer you finally stopped trying to hear.
