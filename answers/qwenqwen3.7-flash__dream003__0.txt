The box grows lighter in your hands, weightless as dandelion fluff. A sense of profound realization settles in your chest, heavy yet comforting: there is no lock, only a question held in silence. The box is waiting for an offering, not of coin or blood, but of truth.

You feel a pressure in your throat, and before you can stop yourself, a word bubbles up from the depths of your memory, unbidden and honest. You speak it aloud.

The moment the sound leaves your lips, the box exhales. The latch clicks with the soft sound of a turning page, and the lid swings upward. Inside, there is no treasure, no monster, no key. Instead, floating in a suspension of amber light, is a single, perfect seed that pulses with the rhythm of your own heartbeat.

As you reach for it, your fingers pass through the light, and suddenly you are holding a door handle. You are no longer in the room with the box; you are standing at the end of a long corridor lined with windows showing scenes from your life. The seed in your hand warms until it bursts, releasing a gust of wind that smells of rain and old paper. The wind pushes gently at your back, and the door ahead unlatches itself.

You step forward, knowing that whatever lies beyond is not an end, but a return. The corridor stretches out, inviting, and for the first time, you know exactly where you are going.
