You begin to run, but the forest changes with every flash of lightning. Familiar trees become enormous pillars, their roots rising from the ground like sleeping creatures. Rain pours down in silver sheets, yet strangely, none of it touches a narrow trail glowing beneath your feet.

You follow it until you reach a small wooden cabin hidden among the trees. The door swings open before you knock. Inside, there is no furniture—only a large mirror standing in the center of the room. Instead of your reflection, it shows the forest beneath a clear blue sky and a figure waiting beside an ancient tree.

Thunder shakes the cabin. Realizing the mirror is not merely an image, you reach through its surface. Your hand passes into warm sunlight. You step forward, leaving the storm behind, and find yourself beside the figure from the mirror.

When they turn around, you recognize them as an older version of yourself.

“You made it,” they say with a relieved smile. “The storm wasn’t chasing you. It was bringing you here.”

Before you can ask why, they place a small seed in your palm. The moment your fingers close around it, the storm disappears. The cabin, the mirror, and the older version of you fade away, leaving you alone beneath the ancient tree.

You plant the seed in the soil. It immediately grows into a tiny tree covered in golden leaves, and from somewhere deep in the forest, the birds begin to sing again.
