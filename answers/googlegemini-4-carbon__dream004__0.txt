Emboldened, you scoop up a handful of frantic, shimmering yellow triangles vibrating near your ankles and toss them at the canvas. As soon as they hit the painted twilight, their chaotic energy stills. They lock into place, transforming into tranquil constellations that begin to chime softly, like wind-blown glass. 

You glance over your shoulder and realize that with every stroke you add, the turbulent, shifting world behind you slows down, borrowing harmony from the image you are creating. 

Finally, you dip your fingers into a puddle of liquid silver where your shadow used to be, and draw a winding path right down the center of the canvas. The edges of the frame begin to dissolve, bleeding seamlessly into the world around you until there is no border left between the painting and reality. Taking a deep, weightless breath, you step forward onto the silver path, walking deeper into the quiet, singing stars as the dream gently carries you away.
