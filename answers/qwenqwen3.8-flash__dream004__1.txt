A brush made of starlight rests beside it, and when I touch it, my pulse turns into sound. The first stroke paints a door standing alone in the field. I open it, and behind it is the ocean folded into the shape of a sleeping bird.

Colors rise from my footsteps—violet, molten gold, soft green—and form a path that leads upward into a sky made of glass feathers. As I walk, the path hums a name I almost recognize. When I reach the top, the blank canvas is no longer blank. It has become a window, and inside the window I see myself looking back, holding another canvas, waiting.

I understand then: the dream is not happening to me. It is drawing me.
