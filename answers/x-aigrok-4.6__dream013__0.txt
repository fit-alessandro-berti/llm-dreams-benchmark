Deeper still, the puzzles stop being external. They become questions about the dreamer. A mirror-maze asks whether the “you” solving the last puzzle is the same “you” who entered. A clockwork sphinx demands you prove, using only the logic of the maze, that you are not still asleep at your desk. Every correct answer peels away another layer of certainty until the final chamber is simply a quiet room containing a single, ordinary door labeled “Wake.”  

You reach for the handle, hesitate, then smile, because you finally understand the last puzzle: the maze was never trying to keep you in. It was teaching you how to leave. You turn the handle. Light floods in. The dream ends, but the feeling of having solved something vast remains.
