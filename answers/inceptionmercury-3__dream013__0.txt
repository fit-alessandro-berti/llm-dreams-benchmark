At first, each challenge feels impossible—moving doors that require you to balance equations, mirrors that reflect riddles instead of faces, staircases that only appear after you’ve solved a logic knot. But slowly, you realize the maze isn’t testing you. It’s teaching you.

You begin to see the pattern beneath the pattern: every lock is also a key, every dead end contains a hidden door, every question carries the seed of its own answer. The deeper you go, the less you need to think. The maze becomes a rhythm, a dance, a quiet conversation.

Finally, you reach the center. There is no treasure, no exit, no grand revelation—only a single glowing mirror. You step closer, expecting to see yourself, but the glass is empty.

Then a voice, gentle and certain, says: *“You were never lost. You were always the way out.”*

And you wake up, smiling, with the answer to a puzzle you didn’t know you were solving.
