You are walking through a peaceful forest when suddenly the weather changes dramatically. You feel a storm approaching and hurry beneath the shelter of an enormous, hollow tree.

The wind rushes through the branches, but inside the trunk it sounds like distant music. Rain taps a rhythm on the bark. Then, through a narrow crack, you see a small golden light drifting between the trees. It pauses outside your shelter, as if waiting.

You step out and follow it. The storm fades with every step until the forest is quiet again. Ahead, the trees open onto a meadow you’ve never seen before, where the rain has gathered in a shining pool. Your reflection looks back at you—but it’s smiling, and holding a tiny star in its palm.

When you reach out, the star glows warmly against your fingers. The forest brightens, and you wake with the feeling that the storm came not to frighten you, but to lead you somewhere new.
