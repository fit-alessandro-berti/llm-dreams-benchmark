"You were never really lost," someone says. When you turn, the strangers are gone. There's only your reflection in the fountain's dark water, smiling back at you with an ease you haven't felt in years.

You reach toward the water, and as your fingertips graze the surface, the dream loosens at its edges — colors bleeding into white, the music swelling one last time — and you wake with the quiet, lingering certainty that the way home was somewhere inside you all along.
