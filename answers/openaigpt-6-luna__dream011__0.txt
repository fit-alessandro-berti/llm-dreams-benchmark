…written in a language you somehow understand but have never seen. The first asks, *What would you become if no one were waiting for your answer?*

You look around. Everyone else is writing furiously. The clock has no hands. You turn the page, and find a map of your childhood bedroom, with a small red X where your old desk used to be.

Then the exam room door opens. Your future self steps in, carrying a cup of tea, and whispers, “You don’t have to get this right. You just have to begin.”

You pick up your pencil. The paper is blank now, except for one line:

*I would like to find out.*
