As you move deeper into the maze, you begin to hear your own footsteps ahead of you.

The puzzles change with every turn. A floor of shifting tiles rearranges itself according to a pattern you almost recognize. A stone lion asks for a truth spoken without lies. A bridge appears only when you can explain why some bridges cannot be built from stone.

Each answer opens a door—but never the one you expected.

Deeper still, the torches turn blue. The symbols covering the walls are no longer invented. They are fragments of your own memories, rearranged into questions. You solve them, and with every solution the maze grows quieter. The clicking of hidden gears fades. The whispers from the wrong turns fade. Until, at last, there is only breathing.

You expect a final door.

Instead, you find a mirror.

Your reflection looks exhausted. Behind its shoulders, the entire maze is visible: every chamber, every locked turning, every version of you that chose differently.

Written across the glass in frost are seven words:

**TO GO FORWARD, LEAVE ONE PUZZLE UNSOLVED.**

You look at the questions still waiting in the corridors. All your life, you believed an answer would close a door. Only now do you understand that some doors are never locked from the outside.

So you leave one puzzle unfinished.

The moment you do, the mirror cracks. A hidden door opens behind you, spilling pale morning light across the floor.

Beyond it is another maze.

Its walls are covered in clocks, all ticking backward. At the entrance, someone is waiting for you.

You recognize your face, but you are younger. They smile and hold out a key.

“I thought you might need more time,” they say.

And although you cannot remember which memory they belong to, you know they are right.
