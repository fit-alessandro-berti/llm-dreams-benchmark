The noise of the city softens behind you. One stranger carries a paper lantern; another hands you a warm cup that smells like the kitchen of your childhood. They don’t ask where you’re going. Somehow, this feels less frightening than it should.

At the end of the street stands a small blue door.

“Is this home?” you ask.

“Not necessarily,” says the stranger with the lantern. “But you can rest here while you remember.”

Inside is a quiet room, a table set for one more person, and a window overlooking the sea. You turn to thank them, but they are already taking off their coats.

For the first time all night, you realize they intend to stay for dinner.
