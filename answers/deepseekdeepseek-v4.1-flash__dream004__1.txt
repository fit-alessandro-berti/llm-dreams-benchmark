...it is the only thing in the world that isn’t changing. You step closer. The canvas isn’t blank—it’s the color of a thought before it becomes a thought. When you touch it, your fingers sink through like warm water, and the whole landscape begins to pour into it: the sky that was orange a moment ago, the staircase that turned into birds, the river shaped like your name. The canvas drinks it all and paints a door. You open it. On the other side, you are painting this world from the outside, brush in hand. The you at the canvas looks up, smiles, and says, “There you are. I was wondering when you’d finish me.” Then the colors rush back in, the shapes begin to move, and you wake with paint under your nails.
