At a quiet square, they hand you a map. It is blank except for a silver thread that begins beneath your feet and winds toward a door you hadn’t noticed. “We can show you the way,” one of them says, “but you’re the only one who can open it.”

You step forward. Behind the door is not home exactly, but a room filled with everyone who has ever made you feel less alone—even people you thought you’d forgotten. When you turn to thank the strangers, they are gone, though their kindness remains, warm as a lantern in your hand.

And as the city dissolves into morning, you understand that being lost was never the same as being abandoned.
