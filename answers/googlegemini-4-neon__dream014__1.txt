The moment they gather around you, the deafening roar of traffic softens into a gentle hum, like rain against a windowpane. You notice they are all carrying glowing paper lanterns, even though the sky above the skyscrapers is a strange, luminous violet. One of them—a woman in a coat the color of autumn leaves—presses a warm mug of spiced tea into your hands, though you have no idea where she was keeping it.

"You were reading the street signs too closely," a tall man with a kind smile says, holding up a brass compass whose needle points straight up at the clouds. "Come on, it's just around the corner."

They lead you down a narrow alleyway you are certain wasn't there a moment ago. As you walk, the towering glass buildings begin to lean inward like giant willow trees, their windows flickering with fireflies instead of office lights. The cracked concrete beneath your feet shifts into smooth, mossy cobblestones. You still don't know the name of the place you're trying to reach, yet every step feels lighter, as if gravity itself is gently letting go of you.

Soon, the alley opens onto a quiet wooden pier stretching out over a vast sea of clouds. Moored at the end is an old-fashioned streetcar with green velvet seats and gleaming brass rails, bobbing softly in the mist. The strangers guide you aboard, laughing and chatting in overlapping voices that sound like half-remembered lullabies. 

As the streetcar glides silently across the sky without any tracks, the chaotic city shrinks into a glittering constellation far below. You lean your head against the cool glass, watching the lights blur into ribbons of gold. When a small silver bell finally chimes, the doors slide open onto a quiet, sunlit porch that feels achingly familiar, even though you are certain you have never seen it before.

You step out and turn to thank your guides, but the streetcar is already dissolving into the morning fog, leaving only the faint scent of cinnamon and a few drifting paper lanterns rising into the blue. The heavy knot of being lost has vanished completely. Taking a deep breath, you reach for the doorknob, knowing without a doubt that you are finally home.
