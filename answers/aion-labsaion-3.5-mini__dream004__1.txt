...a blank canvas and a brush that seems to be made of pure, liquid light. As you reach for it, the canvas ripples like the surface of a pond. You realize that this canvas doesn't paint pictures; it paints *possibilities*. You dip the brush into the shifting air, and it comes away coated in a color that doesn't exist in the waking world—a hue that tastes like childhood and sounds like rain on a tin roof.

You touch the brush to the canvas, and the paint doesn't just sit there. It blooms. A forest of glass trees sprouts instantly, their leaves chiming like a thousand tiny bells. You paint a sky, and it immediately begins to storm with soft, glowing feathers. You paint a door, and before you can even finish the handle, it swings open to reveal a staircase made of moonbeams leading up into a swirling nebula.

You step through, and as you climb, you look back to see the canvas has vanished, replaced by a mirror reflecting a version of you that is still fast asleep in a bed far, far away.
