You are about to take the final exam for a course that determines your future career. As you sit down to start the exam, you notice the questions are **written in a language you've never seen before—symbols that seem to shimmer and rearrange themselves every time you blink**.

You squint harder, trying to focus on an anchor of meaning, some familiar grammar or grammatical anchor. But just as you focus on one character, you blink, and on opening your eyes again, the words on the page have rearranged into a entirely new syntax. You look over to the analog clock on the wall: its numbers have been replaced too, and the minute- and hour-hands sway like the pendulum of an owl in the sky.

You blink, and you feel a pang of déjà vu—you had had this clock-fixation earlier. The invigilator calls "FIVE MINUTES!" You blink, and it's three hours later, you've been fast-asleep, eyes-open.
