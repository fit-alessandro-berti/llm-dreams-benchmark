You are feeling lost and alone in a bustling city. Suddenly, a group of kind strangers offers to help you find your way, and you **follow them down a narrow alley that smells of jasmine and fresh rain. The cobblestones beneath your feet begin to glow faintly, like constellations have fallen and embedded themselves in the ground.**

The strangers smile knowingly at each other. One of them — an older woman with silver hair braided with tiny bells — places a warm hand on your shoulder and says, *"You weren't lost in the city. You were lost in yourself."*

The buildings around you begin to shift. The skyscrapers melt into ancient stone archways. The traffic noise dissolves into a soft hum, like a choir singing from somewhere deep below the earth. You look up and realize the sky is made of stained glass, and a single beam of golden light pours down onto the center of a circular plaza where a great fountain stands.

But the fountain doesn't flow with water. It flows with **memories** — scenes from your life flicker like tiny films in the shimmering cascade. Your first laugh. A sunset you almost forgot. The face of someone you loved.

The strangers guide you to the fountain's edge and one by one, they step into the stream of light and simply... vanish, turning into warm motes that float upward.

The old woman is the last to go. She hands you a small, smooth stone and whispers, *"You always knew the way home."*

You close your hand around the stone. When you open your eyes, you're standing at the front door of a place that feels like everything you've ever needed.

**And you wake up smiling.** 🌙
