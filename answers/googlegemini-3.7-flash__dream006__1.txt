You open your mouth, and instead of words, what emerges is a warm, resonant hum that vibrates gently through the floorboards.

The audience doesn’t seem surprised; they nod in deep, quiet agreement. With every breath you take, ribbons of luminous indigo and amber smoke drift from your lips, tumbling over the edge of the stage and pooling around the listeners' feet. 

As you continue your silent, shimmering speech, the walls of the auditorium begin to gently peel away like morning mist, revealing a vast, violet-tinted ocean beneath a sky full of low, glowing moons. The rows of velvet seats turn into smooth wooden boats bobbing softly in the water, carrying each member of the audience out toward the horizon. 

You find yourself weightless, floating inches above the wooden planks of the stage. You are delivering the most profound truth you have ever known—a truth so simple it could explain everything in the waking world—and just as you draw breath to state the final, perfect conclusion, the sound of the ocean begins to turn into the rhythmic ticking of an alarm clock.
