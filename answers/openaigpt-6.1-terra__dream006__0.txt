Nobody seems surprised. A woman in the front row holds out her hand, and one of the birds lands on her finger. She whispers something to it, and suddenly you remember what your speech was supposed to be about.

You lean toward the microphone.

“Thank you for waiting,” you say.

The audience rises to its feet. Beyond them, the doors of the auditorium swing open, revealing a sunlit field. One by one, the people walk outside, following the birds.

You step down from the stage and join them. The grass is warm beneath your bare feet, though you don’t remember taking off your shoes.
