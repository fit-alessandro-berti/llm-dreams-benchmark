You start to feel a faint hum in the air, as if the city itself is breathing. The cobblestones beneath your feet shift, turning into translucent tiles that flicker with fleeting images of places you’ve never visited. Soft amber light spills from streetlamps fashioned of glass, casting long, wavering shadows that dance like restless spirits.

A glance at your watch reveals its hands spinning wildly, refusing to settle on the correct hour. Panic flutters in your chest, but then a gentle voice—perhaps your own imagination—whispers, “Take the path of the silver arches.”  

You turn the corner and there they rise: towering arches of metal and light that pulse with a low, rhythmic thrum. Beneath them a bustling plaza unfolds, filled with vendors selling timepieces that tick at different speeds. One vendor, a woman with eyes like polished obsidian, offers you a small compass that spins without a needle.  

“This will point you to the place where your meeting awaits,” she says, her tone both soothing and cryptic.  

You accept the compass, and though there is no needle, it begins to tilt toward a distant tower that pierces the skyline, its spire wrapped in vines that bloom with tiny, luminous flowers.  

The streets grow narrower, the walls closing in with murals of faces you recognize—friends, strangers, and versions of yourself. Each face murmurs a fragment of conversation you overheard earlier: “Are you ready?” “Time waits for no one.”  

At the foot of the tower the entrance opens automatically, revealing a grand hall lit by chandeliers made of crystal feathers. In the center sits a long table, and at its head a figure cloaked in a dark coat, face obscured, waits.  

The figure lifts a hand, and a single sheet of paper floats down. Written in elegant script is the agenda for your meeting: *“Discuss the future of your own dreams.”*  

A surge of clarity washes over you. The city around you begins to dissolve, the streets melting into mist. You realize the meeting was never about a physical location—it was a call to confront the unknown within yourself.  

As the mist clears, you find yourself back in your bedroom, morning light filtering through the curtains, and the sound of an alarm reminding you that time has indeed passed. You sit up, feeling both relieved and curious about the symbols you encountered, knowing that the dream has left you with a quiet question: what will you do with the time you now have?
