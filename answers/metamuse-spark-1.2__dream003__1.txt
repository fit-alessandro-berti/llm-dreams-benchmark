You notice intricate patterns and they begin to move.

Not like gears or mechanisms — like *breathing*.

Thin, golden lines etched into the dark wood pulse slowly, like veins of light under skin. As you lean closer, you realize they aren't patterns at all. They are tiny, endless scenes.

A forest growing and shrinking. A tide going in and out. A city of lights flickering awake and then falling asleep again. A version of you, turning the box over in your hands, inside the pattern, that also leans closer.

The box is warm.

There is no keyhole. There never was. Just a smooth, circular indentation in the center, exactly the size of your palm.

When you press your hand to it, the box presses back.

*Thump. Thump.*

It has a heartbeat. And for a second, it syncs perfectly with yours.

A whisper comes from inside — not in words, but in a feeling you instantly understand: *“You already know how to open me.”*

You almost laugh. How? And then you just *know*.

You stop *trying* to open it. You stop looking for a latch, a trick, a key. You just hold it. And you breathe with it.

Inhale — the golden lines brighten.
Exhale — they dim.

Inhale — the box grows lighter in your hands, almost weightless.
Exhale — a soft *click* echoes, not from the box, but from somewhere behind your ribs.

The lid doesn't swing open. It *dissolves*. Like mist. Like sugar in water.

Inside, there is no treasure. No jewels. No darkness.

Inside is a soft, glowing light, and in that light — another box. Smaller. Even more intricate. Even more alive.

And you understand instantly, with the perfect, calm logic that only exists in dreams, that there will always be another box inside. And you will always have the key, because you *are* the key.

You reach for the smaller box, smiling, just as you start to wake up.
