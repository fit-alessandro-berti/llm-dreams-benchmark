### The Final Round

As the competition progresses, you face various challenges, and the rules begin to change when no one is looking.

The first round takes place in a vast library. Every book contains a question, and every question has a blank page for your answer. There are no facts to memorize or speeches to recite. The judges want to know how you think.

At first, you panic. You search for the correct answer, but nothing comes. Then a book falls from a high shelf, landing open on the desk in front of you.

Its question reads: *What would you attempt if failure were guaranteed?*

You think about the dream job—the one you mention when nobody is listening, the one you try not to want too much. You begin speaking. Your voice wavers, but the blank page fills with every word.

When you finish, the library doors swing open.

The second round is a race across a narrow bridge suspended above the clouds. There are no opponents beside you, only a clock counting backward and a wind that pushes you back with every step.

Halfway across, the bridge divides.

One path is shorter but covered in cracks. The other is longer but illuminated. You cannot see where either ends. Just as you lose your nerve, you hear your own voice whisper, “You don’t have to know the whole way.”

You take the longer path.

The final stretch is uncomfortable. Your legs ache. The wind nearly knocks you down. But when you cross, the clouds beneath you part, revealing a city glowing in the distance.
