You are participating in a competition where the grand prize is your dream job. As the competition progresses, you face various challenges, and each one seems designed to expose something you have spent years trying to hide.

In the first round, you must give a presentation in a language you barely understand. The other contestants speak fluently, filling the enormous auditorium with confident voices. When your turn comes, the words on your notes rearrange themselves into meaningless symbols. For a moment, you consider walking away. Then you put the notes down and begin speaking honestly about why the job matters to you. Strangely, everyone understands. Even the judges, whose faces are hidden behind silver masks, lean forward to listen.

The next challenge takes place inside a maze of office corridors. Every door bears a different title: **Success**, **Security**, **Recognition**, **Belonging**. You are told that only one leads to the final round. The other competitors rush through the most impressive-looking doors, but you notice a small, unmarked door beneath a staircase. Behind it is a quiet room containing nothing but a desk, a lamp, and a task you genuinely enjoy doing. You sit down, forget about the competition, and become completely absorbed in the work.

When you finally look up, the walls have disappeared.

You are standing on the roof of an impossibly tall building. The entire city stretches beneath you, glowing like a circuit board. Only three contestants remain. For the final challenge, each of you is given an empty glass box and told to place inside it the quality that makes you most deserving of the job.

One contestant fills the box with trophies. Another fills it with certificates, recommendations, and gold coins. You search your pockets but find nothing. Panic rises in your chest. The judges watch silently as the countdown begins.

Ten.

Nine.

Eight.

Then you notice a tiny crack in the bottom of your box. A green shoot is growing through it. You cup your hands around the fragile plant, protecting it from the wind. As you do, its roots spread across the glass and its leaves unfold, each one carrying an image of a moment when you kept going despite fear, disappointment, or uncertainty.

The silver masks fall from the judges’ faces.

They are all versions of you: younger, older, braver, more frightened. The oldest one smiles and says, “The prize was never given to the person who arrived without doubt. It was given to the person who continued while carrying it.”

A bell rings. The city below goes dark, and a single elevator door appears in the air before you. When it opens, you expect to see your dream workplace.

Instead, you see your own bedroom.

On the desk beside your bed lies an envelope bearing the competition’s seal. You open it and find only one sentence:

**Your first day begins when you wake up.**

You suddenly realize that you are still dreaming—but now you are no longer afraid of opening your eyes.
