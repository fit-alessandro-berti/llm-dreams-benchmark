The dream exhales. The edges of everything soften into golden static, and I wake up on a perfectly ordinary Tuesday, heart hammering, cheeks damp, reaching instinctively for the empty space beside me where you used to sleep. Only this time, I don't call right away. I just trace the shape of the stone in my hand until it feels less like a question and more like a door left unlocked.
