**...the air turns electric.** The birdsong stops mid-note. The leaves, which were a soft green a moment ago, flip over to show their silver undersides, and the whole forest seems to hold its breath.

Above the canopy, clouds the color of bruised plums fold over one another. The first drop lands on your shoulder, warm as a tear. Then the wind arrives and the trees begin to *whisper*. You can almost make out words.

*"This way,"* they seem to say. *"Hurry."*

You run, though your feet feel strangely light, as if the ground is pushing you forward. Lightning splits the sky, and in its white flash you see what wasn't there before: a small wooden door standing alone between two ancient oaks, with no walls and no house, only a door with a brass handle shaped like a crescent moon.

Thunder rolls through your chest. The rain falls sideways now.

You grab the handle, and it's warm. The door opens inward, and on the other side the storm is gone. There is a quiet room with a crackling fireplace and a kettle just beginning to sing. An armchair is angled toward the fire, and a blanket is draped over one arm, as though someone knew you were coming.

On the small table beside the chair is a note in handwriting that looks strangely like your own:

*"You always did love watching storms from somewhere safe. Stay as long as you like. The forest will still be there when you wake."*

Through the window, the lightning keeps dancing over the treetops, silent now, almost beautiful. You sink into the chair. The blanket smells like childhood. Your eyelids grow heavy, and somewhere in the dream, you fall asleep within the dream...
