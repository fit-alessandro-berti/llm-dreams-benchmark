The compass doesn't point north; it points *in*. You understand then that we haven't been battling the storm; we've been sailing through our own subconscious, and the turbulence was just the current of forgotten dreams trying to get out.

The ship dissolves into mist, lifting you, your crew, and the endless night sky into the palm of the Great Eye, which closes gently, cradling us in warm, dark comfort.
