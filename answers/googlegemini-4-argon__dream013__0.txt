However, as you venture deeper, the rigid rules of logic begin to bend under the weight of dream-physics. 

You reach a chasm where the bridge is missing. To cross, you have to solve a paradox: you must weigh the silence in the room against a feather, balancing them on a golden scale. Focusing intensely, you hold your breath, gather the stillness around you as if it were a physical cloak, and place it on the pan. The scale tips, the feather rises, and a pathway of iridescent glass crystallizes beneath your feet. 

Further in, the puzzles become deeply personal. You find a locked archway guarded by a chessboard where the pieces are carved to look like versions of yourself at different ages. There is no opponent; to unlock the door, you have to make a move where both sides win simultaneously. Concentrating until your temples throb, you merge a pawn of your past with a king of your future. The board dissolves into a flock of origami birds that burst through the archway, pulling the heavy doors open behind them.

Finally, you arrive at the center of the maze. There are no walls here, only an endless expanse of twilight sky and a single, floating mirror. Written across the glass in frost is the final puzzle, a single question: *Who is the architect?*

You reach out to wipe away the frost, and as your fingers touch the cold glass, you realize your hand is holding the chalk that drew the maze. The intense concentration melts into a sudden, weightless euphoria. The walls of the labyrinth fold inward like the petals of a origami flower, and as the final piece clicks into place, you wake up.
