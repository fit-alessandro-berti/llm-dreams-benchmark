Your pencil hovers. You glance at the clock — the second hand has stopped. You look up, and the field is now your childhood bedroom. You realize you're not taking an exam at all. You're sitting at your old desk, a blank notebook open, and the words on the page now read:

*"Write your own exam. You already know the answers."*
