You are at your workplace, and suddenly, you are given a project with an impossible deadline. Your boss is waiting for you to start, and you...

...reach for your laptop, but your hands pass right through the desk. The desk is made of fog, or maybe it's the same laminated particleboard as always, you can't tell. Your boss is standing right there, three inches from your face, smiling too wide. 

"Great, you're ready to start, right?" he says, and his voice sounds like a meeting notification chime.

You nod because your mouth is full of papers. You open your mouth to say you need specs, a brief, a team, and a time machine, but the words come out as printer paper, fluttering to the floor. 

You look down at your screen. The project file is open. It's blank except for one line at the top: DO IT BY YESTERDAY. The cursor is blinking, but every time you try to type, the keys turn into wet soap and slide off the keyboard. Your coworker walks by with a stack of folders that are all empty, and they wave like they know you.

The boss taps his watch. The watch is the size of a dinner plate and the second hand is moving backwards. The office lights flicker and suddenly you're in the same office, but it's your childhood classroom. Your boss is now your 4th grade teacher, still waiting for you to start.

You stand up to go get help and your shoes are gone. Your feet are stuck to the carpet with velcro. The carpet is moving, inching slowly toward the break room. You realize you have to present the project in five minutes to a room full of people who are all you, sitting in different chairs, all staring.

You try to run and the hallway stretches like an escalator going down. The boss is still right there, now at the far end of the hallway, getting smaller but still waiting, still smiling.

And then your alarm goes off, or maybe it's the office PA system. You don't know if you woke up, or if the dream just changed rooms.
