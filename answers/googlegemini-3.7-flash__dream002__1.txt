I reach out to touch one, and the moment my fingertip grazes the droplet, gravity gently lets go. 

My feet lift from the mossy ground. I begin to drift upward, weaving between the floating rain and rising through the thick boughs of the trees. Breaking through the top of the canopy, I don’t find chaos, but an infinite, swirling sky of indigo clouds woven with starlight. The wind catches me like a sail, carrying me toward the quiet, glowing eye of the storm, where everything finally dissolves into pure, weightless light.
