…each group seems to know something about the person you’re looking for, but they speak in riddles.

One circle of guests is laughing softly around a table covered in empty glasses. When you ask if they’ve seen your friend, they all point toward a hallway that wasn’t there a moment ago. Another group is dancing slowly to music you can’t hear, and when you pass through them, their bodies feel like warm smoke.

You keep moving through rooms that shift size as you enter them — some vast as ballrooms, others as small as closets. In one, someone whispers your friend’s name backward. In another, you find their coat draped over a chair, still damp from rain, though it hasn’t rained all night.

Finally, you see them across the crowd. They smile and wave, but when you reach them, they turn into a mirror. In the reflection, you realize you’re holding a phone with one unread message:

“I was looking for you too.”

When you look up again, the party is silent. Everyone is watching you kindly, as if they’ve been waiting for you to understand something. Then the lights flicker once, and you wake up with the strange, calm feeling that you were never lost — only unfinished.
