**Continuing Your Dream...**

...obstacles that test not just your skills, but your resolve. The first challenge is a room full of riddles — walls covered in glowing words that rearrange themselves every time you blink. You pause, breathe, and suddenly the patterns make sense, like they've always been there. You pass.

The second challenge sends you climbing a tower made of glass. Each floor reveals a memory of every time you doubted yourself. You try to look away, but you can't — so instead, you walk *through* each memory, taking something valuable from each one. At the top, the glass becomes warm and golden, and you realize the doubt was just scaffolding all along.

Then comes the final round. You're standing across from all the other competitors on a quiet stage. A single voice asks:

*"Why should we trust you with this dream?"*

The room falls silent. And for the first time, you don't reach for rehearsed answers. You speak from the place where your deepest ambition and your rawest vulnerability meet. Your voice is steady. You talk about who you were before you were brave, and who you hope to become after.

The judge smiles — and hands you a key.

---

You begin to wake. The key is still in your hand. It's warm. You're not entirely sure it wasn't real.

But you remember **one thing clearly**: you didn't win by being perfect. You won by being **authentic**.

*Your dream gently fades — but the feeling stays.* 🌙
