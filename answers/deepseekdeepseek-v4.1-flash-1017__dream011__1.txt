You notice the questions are not printed on the paper at all. They’re floating in the air above each desk, written in soft, familiar handwriting—your own. The first question reads: *“Describe the person you became when no one was watching.”* The second: *“What did you sacrifice for certainty?”* The third is blank, waiting for you to fill it in. The exam proctor looks up—it’s an older version of you—and says gently, “Take your time. You already know the answers. Now decide whether you’re willing to be honest.”
