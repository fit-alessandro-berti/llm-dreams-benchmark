...a single brush hovering in the air beside it, bristles dripping a color you have no name for. It sits somewhere between the smell of rain and the sound of a cello.

The ground beneath you ripples from violet to gold, and the trees, which were triangles a moment ago, unfold into slow spirals of smoke. Everything here is in motion except the canvas. It is perfectly still and perfectly white, and it seems to be waiting for you.

You reach out. The brush settles into your hand as if it had always been there.

With your first stroke, a line of deep blue spills across the canvas, and the whole landscape *exhales*. The wind shifts. A distant mountain range tilts to match the curve you painted.

You realize the world isn't just changing on its own. It has been waiting for someone to *decide* something.

So you paint a small door, crooked and amber-colored, with a keyhole shaped like a crescent moon. The moment you finish, the paint glows. The canvas is no longer flat. It has depth, like a window onto a hallway that smells of cinnamon and old books.

Behind you, the shifting shapes gather, curious. A flock of origami birds lands on your shoulders. A fox made of stained glass sits at your feet, its tail chiming softly as it wags.

The door clicks open on its own.

On the other side, someone is painting a canvas of their own. In it you can just make out a figure with a brush in hand, standing before a small amber door.

The figure turns and smiles, as if they've been expecting you.

"Finally," they say. "Now we can paint the next part together."

---

Shall I keep dreaming? We could step through the door, paint something new, or see who the other painter is.
